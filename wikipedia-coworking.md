# Coworking

## Summary

Article on coworking: self-employed people and workers from different companies sharing an office, with shared infrastructure and services. It notes cost savings and convenience, and that coworking helps people avoid the isolation of working alone.

## Why It Matters

Read on day 2 of the [[tidewell-commons-move]] while building the [[workspace-cost-comparison]]. The isolation point was the honest reason I was looking, more than the money.

## Caveats

General; nothing on whether coworking brings work. My evidence for that is my own ([[a-desk-outside-home-pays-for-itself]]).
