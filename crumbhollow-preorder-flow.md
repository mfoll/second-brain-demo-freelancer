# Crumbhollow Preorder Flow

Online pre-order and pickup flow for Crumbhollow Bakery's three shops: choose a shop and a pickup slot, order bread and cakes ahead, pay online. My first real client after leaving Orchard & Vane, and the engagement that changed how I price.

## Outcome

Online pre-order and pickup flow for three shops designed and handed off. Reached on 2025-04-25 ([[crumbhollow-handoff-2025-04-25]]). Quoted 18 days at $650; took 27.

## Current State

- Completed and live since May 2025 on the bakery's existing shop platform.
- Fee $11,700, invoiced in two halves of $5,850. The second half was paid 67 days late ([[crumbhollow-final-payment-2025-07-04]]).
- Effective rate $433 a day. That gap is the trigger for [[pricing-model-review]] and [[pricing-decision-2025-11-20]].

## Scope and Boundaries

The pre-order flow, pickup slot selection, a cake customisation form and the staff view of the day's orders. Not the shop platform setup, which [[martin-abara]] and a local developer handled.

## Working Context

Proposal and estimate: [[crumbhollow-proposal-2025-01]]. The overrun came from three places, in order of days lost: the cake customisation form grew from 4 options to 11 after Josie Kalani's staff meeting (5 days); pickup slots per shop needed shop-specific capacity rules (3 days); a second round of handoff fixes for the developer (1 day). None were written down as changes, because I wanted to be easy to work with. Details in [[crumbhollow-overrun-retrospective]] and the [[crumbhollow-time-log]].

Josie was happy with the result and still sends me photos of the queue on Saturdays. The relationship was fine; my scoping was not. I now use a [[change-order]] for any addition over half a day.
