# Lintel Pricing History

Every price Lintel Kit has had, and why.

- **2025-08:** tiered plan drafted (Solo $59, Studio $149, Team $299) and dropped in September ([[lintel-tiered-pricing-idea-2025]]).
- **2025-11-12 to 2025-11-19:** $63.20 per seat, the 20% launch discount, waitlist code. 52 sales.
- **2025-11-19 onward:** $79 per seat. No change since.
- **2026-03:** considered a Design Night discount code after the talk; did not do it. 11 sales that week anyway.
- **2026-09-21:** team license drafted at $249 for up to 10 seats ([[lintel-team-license-draft-terms]]).
- **Open:** $99 per seat for new buyers with V2 ([[should-the-kit-price-rise-with-v2]]).

Price changes need 30 days' notice to [[jae-won-seo]].
