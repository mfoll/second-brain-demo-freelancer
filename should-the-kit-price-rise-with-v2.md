# Should The Kit Price Rise With V2?

Should new buyers pay more than $79 per seat once V2 ships with dark mode and checked color pairs?

## Current Answer

Open. Leaning to $99 per seat for new buyers from 2026-12-01, with a two-week notice to the waitlist at $79. Must be decided before release, and [[jae-won-seo]] needs 30 days' notice of any price change ([[jae-won-seo-revenue-share-agreement]]), so by 2026-10-31.

## For

- 2026 averages about $1,050 a month against a $1,500 target; volume has not grown since spring ([[lintel-sales-dashboard]]).
- V2 does more, and the contrast pairs are the feature reviewers mention.

## Against

- Interacts with the team license: $249 for 10 looks even cheaper next to $99 seats ([[should-lintel-kit-get-a-team-license]]).
- Unknown elasticity: I have never changed the price except the launch discount ([[lintel-pricing-history]]).
