# Subcontracting With Peers

How I hire peers and get hired by them, agreed at the [[solo-studio-circle-session-2025-09-18|September 2025 circle session]] and refined on Copperline.

## Defaults

- Whoever holds the client contract pays the subcontractor within 15 days of their invoice, whatever the client's terms. Copperline pays me net 30; I pay Imani in 15.
- The client is told in writing before the subcontractor starts. Copperline's contract requires notice.
- The subcontractor works inside the client's workspace and data rules, never on their own drives.
- No direct pitching of the client by the subcontractor for 12 months, unless the lead contractor agrees.
- Who presents what is agreed before kickoff.
- A short written scope and fee in a one-page subcontract letter; no deposit between peers. Client-facing terms stay in the [[standard-services-agreement]].

## Caveat

Price the subcontract into the client fee from the start. I nearly forgot on Copperline phase 2 and it was saved by the change order.
