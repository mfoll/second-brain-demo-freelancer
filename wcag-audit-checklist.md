# WCAG Audit Checklist

## Summary

My audit checklist, a spreadsheet of WCAG 2.2 level A and AA success criteria (55 rows) grouped by the page types I usually audit: search, record or product page, forms, account, checkout. Each row has how I test it, tools, and a severity guide.

## Key Details

- Built from Sablewing modules 1 to 11 between April and July 2025, first used on the [[thistlewood-catalog-audit]].
- Severity is by broken tasks and who they block, not by criterion level. Changed after the [[sablewing-final-assessment-2025-08-22|final assessment]] feedback.
- Includes a "do the task, then listen" pass for dynamic content, and a link to [[screen-reader-testing]].
- Finding template: barrier, criterion, who it blocks, fix, severity, screenshot reference.

## Why It Matters

The core of every audit I sell: Thistlewood in 2025, Tallowmere in 2026 ([[tallowmere-ticketing-audit]]). It is also what lets me quote audits in days at the $720 rate.

## Caveats

Not a substitute for testing with disabled users. Version 3, last reviewed 2026-09-10.
