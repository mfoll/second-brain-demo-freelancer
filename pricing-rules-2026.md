# Pricing Rules 2026

One-page replacement for section 3 of the [[pricing-model-2025]], written on 2026-06-22 after the [[pricing-check-in-2026-06-18]]. This is the rule I apply now.

## Key Details

- Under 10 days, audits and workshops: $720 a day, fixed number of days, named deliverable. 50% deposit, as in every contract.
- Over 10 days: three value-based options, option A a paid first step ([[three-option-proposals]], [[paid-discovery-phase]]).
- Borderline (8 to 12 days): ask the client how they compare suppliers. If they ask for a day rate, give it.
- Floor: $720 a day effective, checked with a 30% overrun ([[pricing-floor-calculation]]).
- Day rate shared on request, never published ([[should-i-publish-rates-on-my-site]]).
- Review every January ([[when-should-the-day-rate-rise]]).

## Why It Matters

Short enough to read before every quote. The first quote under it was Tallowmere ([[tallowmere-audit-quote-2026-09]]).
