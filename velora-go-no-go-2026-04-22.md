# Velora Go No-Go 2026-04-22

Two days after the RFP arrived I spent an hour deciding whether to bid. I used the [[qualify-a-new-lead]] checklist, stretched to fit an RFP.

## For

- Mobility app, a domain I wanted, and a product team with a real research budget.
- Timing: 14 weeks from June, when Copperline foundations would be done and transfers would be in full swing at three days a week.
- Size: the RFP's indicated range was $60,000 to $90,000 ([[velora-rfp-2026]]).

## Against

- Cold. I knew nobody at Velora.
- Five bidders announced, likely including agencies.
- Writing the proposal would cost about five days of unbillable time.

## Decision

Bid. My reasoning was that one win this size would cover most of the second half of 2026. I underestimated the capacity question and did not think about a first step. In the end it cost six days of proposal work and taught me more than any course. Whether I'd bid on the next one is open: [[should-i-answer-cold-rfps]].
