# Is A 30 Percent Set-Aside Enough?

Opened in January 2025 when I started moving 30% of every client payment to a tax account.

## Current Answer

No, not any more. 30% covered 2025 with $720 to spare, because tax came to 29.3% of client payments and kit income was outside the rule ([[tax-return-2025-summary]]). Since 2026-04-13 the rate is 32% and Gumroad payouts are included ([[set-aside-rate-raised-2026-04-13]]).

## Next review

After the 2026 return in April 2027. If 32% leaves less than about $2,000 of slack, go to 34%. If the business becomes a company the rule changes shape entirely ([[should-i-form-a-company]]).
