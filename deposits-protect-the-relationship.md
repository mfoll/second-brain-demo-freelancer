# Deposits Protect The Relationship

## Stance

A 50% deposit is good for the client relationship, not just for my cash flow. It removes the moment where I am working while wondering whether I will be paid, which is the moment I become a worse collaborator.

## Scope

Fixed-fee and day-rate client work. Not Lintel Kit sales, and negotiable for large clients who offer regular milestone payments instead.

## Why I think this

- Crumbhollow: by the third reminder I was resentful of a client I liked ([[crumbhollow-final-payment-2025-07-04]]).
- No client since July 2025 has walked away over the deposit. Thistlewood's director asked why; the honest answer was enough.
- The clients who pay deposits on time also answer emails on time. It is a cheap early signal.

## What Would Change My Mind

A good client walking away over it, or two short engagements in a row where the deposit costs more admin than it protects ([[should-every-client-pay-a-deposit]]).

## Implications

Explain the deposit in the proposal, not only in the contract, so it is never a surprise.
