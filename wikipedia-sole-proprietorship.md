# Sole proprietorship

## Summary

Defines a sole proprietorship: a business owned and run by one person, with no legal distinction between the owner and the business. The owner keeps all profit and carries all liabilities personally. A sole trader may still employ people.

## Why It Matters

Background for the [[business-structure-review]]. Before the meeting with [[delphine-arceneaux]] on 2026-07-21 I wanted the general trade-off in plain words, without any country's rules: simplicity and full control on one side, unlimited personal liability and fewer tax options on the other. Delphine's memo is about the tax side; this reminded me that liability is the other half of the question ([[company-structure-memo-2026-07]]).

## Key Details

- One owner, no separate legal personality: the business's debts are the owner's debts.
- Simple to start and run; few formalities compared with companies.
- The article notes that in some systems the business is not taxed separately and its profit counts as the owner's income.
- The article covers many countries' variants; I took only the general picture and nothing about specific tax treatment.
