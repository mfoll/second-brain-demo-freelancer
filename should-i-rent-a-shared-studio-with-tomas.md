# Should I Rent A Shared Studio With Tomás?

## Question

In July 2025 [[tomas-echeverria|Tomás Echeverría]] offered to split a small studio: $520 each a month on a 12-month lease, plus furniture. Should I take it instead of a coworking desk?

## Answer

No. Superseded by the [[tidewell-commons-move]] on 2025-08-11.

- Cost: $520 plus about $600 of furniture against $340 for a Tidewell desk with rooms included ([[workspace-cost-comparison]]).
- Lock-in: a 12-month lease in my first year alone was too much risk.
- People: two people in a room is still close to working alone.

Tomás found another partner within a month. Kept for the reasoning.
