# Yvonne Takahashi Pricing Lunch 2025-10-16

Lunch with [[yvonne-takahashi]] ten days into the [[pricing-model-review]]. I brought a one-page summary of the [[effective-day-rate-sheet]] with Crumbhollow at $433 a day circled.

What she said, as close as I wrote it down:

- "Price the thing they get, not the time you spend." Clients don't buy days, they buy a working checkout or a passed audit.
- Always keep a floor, a number below which you walk away, and never show it. That became [[pricing-floor-calculation]].
- Give people a choice so they argue about scope, not about your rate. That is where the three options come from ([[three-option-proposals]]).
- Write the change rule into the proposal before the work starts. Crumbhollow's overrun was unpriced change, not a bad estimate.

What I pushed back on: she said "never quote a day rate again". I wasn't sure then and wrote "check with small jobs" in the margin. Seven months later the [[pricing-check-in-2026-06-18]] proved the margin note right.
