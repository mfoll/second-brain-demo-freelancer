# Set-Aside Rate Raised 2026-04-13

From this day, 32% of every payment goes to the tax account, and Gumroad payouts are included. It was 30% of client payments only.

## Why

- The 2025 tax came to 29.3% of client payments received; the 30% rule left $720 ([[tax-return-2025-summary]]).
- Kit income was taxable but outside the rule.
- 2026 projected income is higher, and Delphine expects the effective share to rise with it.

## What changed

- [[set-aside-taxes-from-each-payment]] updated the same day; the first payment under the new rate was a Copperline milestone on 2026-04-16.
- The question [[is-a-30-percent-set-aside-enough]] closed.
