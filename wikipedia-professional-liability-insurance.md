# Professional liability insurance

## Summary

Explains professional liability insurance (also called professional indemnity, or errors and omissions): cover for the cost of defending and settling claims that a professional's advice or service was negligent or caused a client financial loss. It is distinct from general liability cover and is sometimes required by contract.

## Why It Matters

Read before my first call with [[sunita-rao]] in February 2025, when I realised a client could blame a design decision for a loss. Thistlewood later required a certificate before signing, and my own [[standard-services-agreement]] now requires the cover. Policy details in [[professional-liability-policy]].

## Key Details

- Covers financial losses claimed from errors, negligence or misrepresentation, and almost always pays defence costs even when a claim turns out to be groundless.
- General liability policies respond mainly to bodily injury and property damage; this cover fills the gap for financial loss.
- Clients sometimes make it a contract condition, and some professions are required by law to hold it.
- Caveat: the article lists many professions (medical, legal, accounting, engineering); design is not discussed.
