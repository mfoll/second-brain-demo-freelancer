# Statement of work

## Summary

Short project management article: a statement of work is a narrative description of the work a vendor will do for a client, with activities, deliverables and timelines, often with requirements, pricing and standard terms. It usually accompanies a master service agreement or follows a request for proposal.

## Why It Matters

Copperline's procurement asked for a statement of work under a master agreement for phase 2, and I had only written proposals before. This helped me structure it: my [[standard-services-agreement]] as the master terms, one statement of work per phase ([[copperline-statement-of-work]]).

## Caveats

Generic; says nothing about design work, research deliverables or change orders, which I added myself.
