# Lintel Kit V1 Launch Day 2025-11-12

Lintel Kit went on sale on Gumroad at 09:00 on 2025-11-12, at $79 per seat with 20% off for the first week.

## What happened

- Launch email to 312 waitlist addresses at 09:00 ([[lintel-launch-email-2025-11-12]]); a post in two design communities at noon.
- 41 sales on the first day, 52 in launch week, 61 by the end of November.
- 19 support emails in the first week, most about duplicating the Figma file on free plans. About six hours of support that week, the most ever.
- One refund: a buyer expecting Penpot files.
- I had blocked the day off Copperline work, which was right; I would block the next morning too.

Launch lessons in [[lintel-kit-v1-launch-retrospective]]. Two weeks later the first bug arrived ([[first-token-bug-report-2025-11-26]]).
