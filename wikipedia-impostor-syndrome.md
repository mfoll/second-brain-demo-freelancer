# Impostor syndrome

## Summary

Impostor syndrome, or the impostor phenomenon, is the experience of feeling like an intellectual or professional fraud despite evidence of competence. The article covers its original description in the 1970s, who reports it, the overlap with perfectionism and anxiety, and the debate about whether it is a syndrome at all.

## Why It Matters

## Key Details

- Not a clinical diagnosis; a pattern of self-assessment.
- Often strongest after transitions, such as going independent.
