# Run A Circle Session

How the host runs a Solo Studio Circle session. Agreed at the one-year review on 2026-04-16.

## Inputs

- The rota in the [[solo-studio-circle-shared-notes|shared notes]]. Each member hosts about once every seven months.
- The small room at Tidewell, held by Hana for the third Thursday, 12:00 to 13:30.

## Steps

1. One week before: post the session's question and the piece of work in the circle chat (a proposal, a price, a contract clause, a design, a hard email).
2. Two days before: confirm the room with Hana and who is coming.
3. 12:00 to 12:15: round of one-line updates. Anyone who has work to pass on says so.
4. 12:15 to 13:00: the host's question and piece of work. Host talks for five minutes at most, then listens.
5. 13:00 to 13:20: open slot for anything urgent.
6. 13:20 to 13:30: who owes whom what; next host confirmed.
7. Same day: host writes the session in the shared notes, client names as initials.

## Guardrails

- [[solo-studio-circle-ground-rules|Ground rules]] read aloud when a new member or guest is present.
- No recording, no AI summaries.
- If fewer than four people can come, move it rather than cancel.

## Verification

The shared notes have an entry for the session and the next host is named.
