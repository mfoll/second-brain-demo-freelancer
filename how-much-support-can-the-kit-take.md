# How Much Support Can The Kit Take?

Lintel Kit's outcome caps support at four hours a week. Is that the right cap, and what happens when V2 and a team license push past it?

## Current Answer

Open. Today the average is about 2.5 hours a week, with peaks of 5.5 after releases ([[lintel-support-log]]). The cap holds on average but not in release weeks.

## Options if it grows

- A searchable FAQ built from the 31% of requests that are setup questions.
- Route team questions through a named admin only ([[lintel-team-license-draft-terms]]).
- Pay [[paloma-ruiz]] to triage the inbox; she would need access to the Lintel mailbox, outside her current brief.
- Raise the price and accept slower answers.

## What would answer it

Four weeks of support data after V2, before the team-license decision ([[should-lintel-kit-get-a-team-license]]).
