# Copperline Kickoff 2025-09-08

Half-day kickoff at Copperline's main office for the discovery phase. Renata Solberg, Priyanka Venkat, Ana Lucía Ferreyra and Hollis Dunmore attended; Desmond Okafor joined for the last hour.

## Notes

- Renata's framing: members complain about online banking more than anything else, and the branch phone line is absorbing it. She wants evidence before she asks the board for a redesign budget.
- Agreed the research plan ([[copperline-discovery-research-plan]]): 14 interviews, a 12-person baseline test, readout on 2025-11-14.
- Ana Lucía showed call log categories: "how do I move money" was the top topic in August.
- Hollis gave me read access to analytics and named the hard limits: core platform APIs fixed, vendor app untouchable for now. I did not ask enough about the app at this point ([[copperline-native-app-concepts]]).
- Desmond asked whether the brand would change. I said discovery does not touch brand. True then; not quite true later.
- First on my list after the meeting: weekly status email every Friday ([[copperline-weekly-status-emails]]).
