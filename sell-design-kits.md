# Sell Design Kits

Lintel Kit as a product: building it, releasing versions, pricing and licensing it, watching sales and answering buyers. It is ongoing because a kit that stops getting updates stops selling.

## Scope

- The kit itself, its releases and its roadmap, under the umbrella [[lintel-kit]].
- Pricing and licensing on Gumroad ([[lintel-kit-license-terms]], [[lintel-pricing-history]]).
- Sales tracking ([[lintel-sales-dashboard]]) and buyer support ([[answer-lintel-support-requests]]).
- The revenue share with [[jae-won-seo]] for the coded tokens.
- Not here: the taxes on kit income, which go through [[run-the-business]]; client design-system work, which stays under [[deliver-client-work]] even when it feeds the kit.

## Current State

- 212 sales and about $15,700 gross by 2026-09-30. 2026 averages about $1,050 a month, below the $1,500 target.
- Support takes about 2.5 hours a week on average, inside the four-hour cap ([[the-kit-must-not-eat-client-time]]).
- [[lintel-kit-v2]] (dark mode and contrast-checked color pairs) is in progress for 2026-12-01.
- The team license is an open question; I decide after V2 ships ([[should-lintel-kit-get-a-team-license]]).
