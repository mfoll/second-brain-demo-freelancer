# Copperline Discovery Readout 2025-11-14

Two-hour readout of the discovery findings to Copperline's leadership team and the digital team. The report was accepted the same afternoon.

- Opened with three 40-second clips of baseline participants trying to transfer money. The room went quiet at P7, who gave up and said she would phone the branch.
- Numbers: SUS 58; transfer completed by 5 of 12; 9 of 14 interviewees had never opened "Move money" ([[copperline-research-report]]).
- The board member present asked about the mobile app. I agreed to a separate short concept sprint, which became [[copperline-native-app-concepts]]. In hindsight the phone browser number (62% of sessions) already pointed the other way.
- Renata asked for a phase 2 proposal by early December. I sent three options on 2025-12-02 ([[copperline-statement-of-work]]).
- Desmond heard the "hard to read teal" finding for the first time and asked for the raw quotes. Sent them the following Monday.
