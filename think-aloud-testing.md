# Think-Aloud Testing

Asking participants to say what they are thinking while they do a task, so the moderator hears the reasoning, not only the clicks. The default in every moderated test I run.

## How I use it

- One short practice task first (find the opening hours on a website) so people get used to talking.
- Prompts kept neutral: "What are you looking for?", "What do you expect to happen?" Never "Is that confusing?"
- Silence is information. If someone stops talking, I wait about five seconds before a prompt.

## Limits

- Talking slows people down, so time on task from a think-aloud session is not a real time.
- Screen reader users cannot talk over their screen reader comfortably. In Thistlewood and Copperline I asked them to pause and explain instead (retrospective think-aloud for those moments).
- Some people narrate what they think I want to hear. The [[system-usability-scale|SUS]] at the end sometimes contradicts the friendly commentary, which is useful.
