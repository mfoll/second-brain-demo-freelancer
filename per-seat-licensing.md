# Per-Seat Licensing

Selling the right to use a product per named user ("seat") rather than per copy, company or project.

## Why I use it

- Price scales with use: a team of five pays five times what a solo designer pays.
- Simple to explain and to enforce on Gumroad with a quantity selector ([[lintel-kit-gumroad-listing]]).
- Revenue shares can be defined per seat, as with [[jae-won-seo]] ([[jae-won-seo-revenue-share-agreement]]).

## Where it breaks

- Teams share seats rather than ask a manager to approve more of them. [[calvin-moyo]]'s team had seven unofficial users ([[calvin-moyo-team-license-email-2026-09]]).
- Purchase approval often depends on a total amount, not on the per-seat price, so a team crossing an approval threshold stalls.
- Flat team or site licenses fix both, at the cost of discounting larger teams ([[should-lintel-kit-get-a-team-license]]).
