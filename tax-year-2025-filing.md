Copperline discovery ($18,500), the native app concept sprint ($9,800), the Ledgerleaf deposit ($12,000 in December) and all the kit revenue landed in the second half and never made it into the estimates. Delphine's line: "the set-aside is your savings, the estimates are your promise; keep both honest."

Records were in good shape because Paperbird had all invoices from March 2025 and receipts were matched monthly ([[paperbird-invoicing-setup]], [[deductible-expenses-log]]). January and February 2025 invoices were in my old spreadsheet and took an afternoon to reconcile. Delphine's fee for the return was $1,450.

What I changed:

1. Set-aside rate 32%, and Gumroad payouts included ([[set-aside-taxes-from-each-payment]]).
2. 2026 estimates of $8,500 a quarter, based on a $125,000 projection, re-checked each September ([[quarterly-estimate-worksheet-2026]]).
