## How decisions get made

- Renata does not overrule anyone in meetings. She decides by email afterwards, in writing.
- Desmond can stall but not block. Giving him ownership of the brand teal's uses kept him on board ([[copperline-accessibility-review-2026-02-11]]).
- Gideon's support carries weight with the board; a standard cited in plain language is what he needs.
- Hollis's "that's two sprints" ends a discussion. Look for a cheaper design rather than argue.

## Lesson

I met Desmond in the last hour of the kickoff. He should have had his own session in week one. The kickoff questionnaire now asks who can say no ([[client-kickoff-questionnaire]]).
