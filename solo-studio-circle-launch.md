## Working Context

Three months into freelancing I noticed that the only people I talked shop with were clients. Rosalind, a content designer I had worked with at an Orchard & Vane client, said the same over coffee in January 2025. We each invited two people we trusted, deliberately mixing roles: research, front end, brand, illustration. We wanted people who would say a price was too low.

Rejected options: an online-only group (we both had that and it faded), and joining an existing designers' association (good for events, bad for talking about money). We chose to meet in person at Tidewell Commons, where Rosalind already had a membership and could book the small room for free at lunchtime.

The first session on 2025-03-20 was rates and nothing else ([[solo-studio-circle-first-session-2025-03-20]]). Everyone shared their day rate on a card. Mine was $650, the second lowest. That number came back in November when the Crumbhollow overrun pushed the [[pricing-model-review]].

Ground rules took two sessions. The one we argued about was confidentiality of client names: agreed that names can be said in the room and never repeated outside without permission ([[solo-studio-circle-ground-rules]]).
