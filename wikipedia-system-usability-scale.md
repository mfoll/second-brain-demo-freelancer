# System usability scale

## Summary

Short article on the SUS: a ten-item Likert-style questionnaire giving an overall view of how usable people find a system. It credits John Brooke at Digital Equipment Corporation, 1986, where it was made for office systems, and explains the alternating positive and negative items and the conversion to a 0 to 100 score.

## Why It Matters

Read in week 2 of the [[research-methods-course-2026|Marrowgate course]] when Lorna asked us to explain the scoring without notes. I had used SUS at Orchard & Vane and on the Copperline baseline without knowing where it came from. My working note is [[system-usability-scale]]; the scoring steps are [[score-a-sus-questionnaire]].

## Caveats

Brief. It does not give the benchmark averages I quote to clients; those come from the course material.
