# Should I Answer Cold RFPs?

## Question

Is it worth answering open RFPs from organizations I have no connection to, given that Velora cost six unbillable days and was lost?

## Current thinking

- Against: all won work came through people ([[client-pipeline-tracker]]); RFPs score capacity, where a solo bid starts low.
- For: referrals alone leave January 2027 empty ([[capacity-calendar-2026]]); the new template answers Velora's three objections; an RFP win would be the first proof that the portfolio works on strangers.
- Filter in the [[rfp-watchlist]]: accessibility or UX scope, stated budget, a timeline I can meet, and a capacity score I can answer with named partners ([[capacity-signalling]]).

## Next step

Answer the next RFP that passes the filter and that I can write in three days or less, using [[proposal-template-2026]]. Decide by 2026-12-31 whether to keep watching.
