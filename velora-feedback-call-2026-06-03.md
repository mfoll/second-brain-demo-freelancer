# Velora Feedback Call 2026-06-03

Thirty-minute video call with [[annika-brandvold]], head of product at Velora Mobility, five days after Velora chose Orchard & Vane for the app redesign. Rashid Kamau offered it in the decision email and I accepted in my reply ([[velora-decision-email-2026-05-29]]). She gave three reasons, in this order.

## At a Glance

> [!abstract]
> Three reasons I lost: one price of $74,000 with no smaller first step, against a $38,000 discovery phase from Orchard & Vane; case studies that showed screens rather than research outcomes; and doubt that one person had the capacity. Each one now has a fix: the proposal template, the portfolio rebuild, and named partners plus the research methods course.

## The three reasons

1. **One price, no smaller first step.** The panel liked my approach but $74,000 was a single yes or no. Orchard & Vane offered a $38,000 discovery phase with the redesign priced as a later option. Annika said that let her "spend a budget I already had" and decide on the rest with evidence. My own November rule said three options; I skipped it because the RFP had a fixed scope ([[pricing-decision-2025-11-20]]).
2. **Case studies showed screens, not outcomes.** Two panelists read my portfolio. They found good-looking screens and no numbers: no test results, no before and after, nothing on what changed for the client. Orchard & Vane's case studies had conversion figures. I had the Copperline SUS baseline and could not use it ([[will-copperline-approve-a-case-study]]).
3. **Solo capacity.** [[rashid-kamau]] had scored me lowest on team and capacity. My answer in the Q&A, "I'll bring in help if needed", sounded like improvisation. Annika said a named researcher and a named developer would have changed that score.

She added that the design thinking in my unlock-flow concept was the best of the three finalists. That was nice to hear and changes nothing.

## What I said and asked

- I thanked her and did not argue any point.
- I asked whether a discovery phase at around $30,000 would have won. Her answer: probably it would have been close, but the capacity score would still have hurt.
- I asked if I could contact her later. Yes, for a later phase, not for a pitch now.

## What changed because of it

- Reason 1: [[proposal-template-rework]] with three options and a paid first step, and the broader [[pricing-check-in-2026-06-18]] fifteen days later.
- Reason 2: [[portfolio-site-rebuild]] around [[evidence-led-case-studies]].
- Reason 3: named partners [[imani-clarke]] and [[bastian-kohl]] ([[partner-availability-agreements-2026]]), and more research credibility through the [[research-methods-course-2026]].

The summary of lessons is in [[velora-lessons]].
