The evening course in user research methods at Marrowgate University Extension that I took after losing the Velora pitch. Completed 2026-09-25.

## Outcome

Marrowgate University Extension research methods course passed, with an unmoderated test and a diary study run on real work. Reached on 2026-09-24 (final presentation) and confirmed by the result email on 2026-09-25.

## Current State

## Scope and Boundaries

The course, the two studies as course assignments, and the method notes. Not here: the Copperline tests (A's notes under [[copperline-online-banking-redesign]]), though I used the course to tighten how I report them, and the Lintel Kit product decisions (under [[sell-design-kits]]).

Participants are pseudonymous (P1 to P6 in the diary study, P1 to P8 in the test). Consent forms and raw entries are in my research folder, not in the vault.

## Working Context

The trigger was the Velora feedback call on 2026-06-03 ([[velora-feedback-call-2026-06-03]]). One of [[annika-brandvold|Annika Brandvold]]'s three reasons was that my case studies showed screens and not what research found or what changed. Fair. At Orchard & Vane a research team did that work and I designed from their reports. On my own I had run tests (Copperline's baseline with Imani's help) but never designed a study from scratch.

I enrolled on 2026-06-08, the last day of registration, and dropped the design tokens course I had planned ([[learning-plan-2026]]). [[lorna-beckett|Lorna Beckett]] taught it with a strong bias toward small, well-planned studies over big surveys, which suits a solo practice.

The rule from 2025 held: apply each method to real work while the course runs ([[courses-only-stick-when-applied-to-paid-work]]). Client research was off the table because Copperline's data cannot leave their workspace and a course assignment is not a client deliverable. Lintel Kit buyers were the obvious alternative. I offered each a free V2 seat for taking part.

What I learned that I did not expect: planning took longer than running. The diary study plan went through three drafts with Lorna ([[lintel-diary-study-plan]]), mostly on prompts that did not lead. And unmoderated tests need a pilot: my first task wording had two participants searching for a page that did not exist.
