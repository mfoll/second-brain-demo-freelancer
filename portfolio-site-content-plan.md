# Portfolio Site Content Plan

The page plan for the rebuilt portfolio, written in the second week of the [[portfolio-site-rebuild]].

## Key Details

- Seven pages: home, three case studies, services, about, contact. Old site had fourteen.
- Home: positioning line ([[positioning-statement-2026]]), three case study cards each with one number, a short services list, contact.
- Services: audits and workshops (day rate on request), product design projects (proposal with options), research with a named partner ([[services-menu-2026]]).
- About: under 300 words, with photo; mentions Orchard & Vane, the circle and Lintel Kit in one line each.
- Contact: the reworked inquiry form ([[website-inquiry-form]]).
- Accessibility: WCAG 2.2 AA, checked page by page before launch.
