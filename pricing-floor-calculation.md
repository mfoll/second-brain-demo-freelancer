# Pricing Floor Calculation

The small sheet behind my walk-away number, built after Yvonne's advice to keep a floor ([[yvonne-takahashi-pricing-lunch-2025-10-16]]). Updated 2026-06-20.

## Key Details

- Target income before tax plus business costs (coworking from September 2025, software, insurance, accountant, the set-aside for taxes handled in [[set-aside-taxes-from-each-payment]]).
- Billable days a year: I assume 130, not 220. The rest is sales, admin, learning, the kit and time off.
- 2025 result: $650 a day. 2026 result: $705, rounded up to $720 for the day rate.

## Why It Matters

Used as a private check on every value-based option ([[value-based-pricing]]) and as the basis of the day rate in [[pricing-rules-2026]].

## Caveats

The 130-day assumption is the weakest number. 2025 had about 150 billable days; if 2026 ends lower, the floor goes up.
