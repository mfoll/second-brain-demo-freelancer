# A Desk Outside Home Pays For Itself

## Stance

For me, the $340 a month for a Tidewell desk is a business cost with a return, not a comfort. The return comes from people, not from the desk.

## Scope

My practice as it is: client-facing work, in-person testing, a peer group that meets in the same building. Someone who works only async with remote clients might not get the same return.

## Why I Think This

- Tallowmere came from a member two desks down ([[quentin-ashby-tallowmere-referral-2026-08-20]]): 8 days at $720, $5,760, more than the year's desk cost.
- The Design Night talk ([[design-night-talk-2026]]) came from being in the building.
- In-person sessions with Imani and the circle need a room; the four included hours a month cover most of it.
- I work fewer evenings since I stopped working where I live. Hard to price, real.

## What Would Change My Mind

A year with no work or collaboration traceable to Tidewell, or a rate rise above about $450.

## Implications

Renew yearly after a short check, as on [[tidewell-desk-renewal-2026-08-11]]. Show up at Design Night even when tired.
