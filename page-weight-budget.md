# Page Weight Budget

A fixed upper limit on how much a page may download (images, fonts, scripts, video), agreed before design starts and checked before launch. A design constraint like a grid, not an afterthought for developers.

## My working numbers

- Content pages: under 1 MB on first load.
- Home or landing pages: under 2 MB, no autoplay video.
- Fonts: two families at most, subsetted.

They come from my own portfolio check, not from a standard. The home page went from 6.8 MB to 1.9 MB ([[portfolio-page-weight-check]]).

## Why it might sell

Faster pages on cheap phones and weak connections. That argument works with clients even when energy use does not. Whether to make it a proposal line is [[does-sustainable-design-belong-in-proposals]].

## Limits

I only control what I design. Third-party scripts (analytics, chat widgets) often outweigh everything else and belong to the client.
