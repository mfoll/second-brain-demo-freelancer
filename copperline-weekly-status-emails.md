# Copperline Weekly Status Emails

The Friday status emails I send Priyanka Venkat, about 55 of them since September 2025. Summarised here as a format, not copied.

## Format

Five headings, never more than one screen:

1. Done this week.
2. Next week.
3. Decisions needed, with a date and the person who needs to decide.
4. Risks, with what I am doing about each.
5. Hours or budget used against the current milestone.

## Why It Matters

Priyanka forwards the "decisions needed" section to Renata Solberg unchanged, so it has to stand alone. The budget line caught the March 2026 foundations overrun two weeks early, which is why the milestone slip happened without a fee argument. I now use the same format with every client over four weeks long.
