# Lintel V2 Scope

The scope I fixed for V2 on 2026-08-05, with where each item came from.

## In

- Dark mode as a second mode on the semantic tokens ([[lintel-v2-dark-mode-token-plan]]). Asked for by Mirela Dobos and 14 others.
- 32 contrast-checked color pairs with allowed uses ([[lintel-v2-contrast-pairs-table]]). From Copperline's two teals and 22% of support requests being about color.
- Accent tokens renamed by role: `text.accent`, `fill.accent`, `decoration.accent`.
- Getting-started page rewrite, with color guidance as its own page, after the unmoderated test showed people could not find it ([[lintel-docs-unmoderated-test-2026-08-24]]).
- Three components: toast, inline alert, data-table header.
- Removal of the 1.1 deprecated spacing aliases.

## Out

Penpot, team license, form patterns, new artwork beyond dark versions of the existing set.

## Budget

About 60 hours of my time from August to November, plus Jae-won under the revenue share. Spent so far: about 38 hours.
