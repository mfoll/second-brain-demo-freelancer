# Lintel Team License Requests

The "Team requests" tab of the [[lintel-sales-dashboard]]: every buyer or prospect who asked for team terms.

## Key Details

- Nine requests from January to September 2026.
- Team sizes: 4, 5, 5, 6, 6, 8, 8, 10 and 12 ([[calvin-moyo]]).
- Seats already held by these teams: 14 in total. Five of the nine teams hold one seat.
- What they asked for: a single license (all nine), an invoice with a purchase-order number (four), a named admin for updates (three), a price for 20 or more seats (none).
- Outcome so far: each was told that seats can be bought in one checkout and that I am considering team terms.

## Why It Matters

The demand side of [[should-lintel-kit-get-a-team-license]]. Most requesters are small teams, which is the argument for a cheap team price aimed at seat sharing.

## Caveats

Requests, not commitments. Only Calvin has said he would buy at once.
