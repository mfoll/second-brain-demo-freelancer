# Green computing

## Summary

Article on green computing (green IT), the study and practice of environmentally sustainable computing: energy use of devices and data centers, hardware lifecycle, recycling and efficient software.

## Why It Matters

The closest Wikipedia article to sustainable web design, which has no article of its own. I read it before measuring my portfolio pages ([[portfolio-page-weight-check]]) to see whether page weight is a meaningful lever. It is a small one next to hardware and data centers, which is why my argument to clients is speed on old phones, not carbon. See [[page-weight-budget]].

## Caveats

Almost nothing on web pages or design decisions.
