# Invoice Register

Monthly export from Paperbird of every invoice since January 2025, with days to payment. Last refreshed 2026-10-05.

## Key Details

- 2025: 31 invoices, $106,200 client revenue received. Average days from invoice to payment: 24 before the new terms, 13 after.
- 2026 to 2026-09-30: 22 invoices. Average 11 days for net-15 clients, 27 days for Copperline's net-30 milestones.
- Longest wait: Crumbhollow final invoice, 67 days ([[crumbhollow-final-payment-2025-07-04]]).
- Late since the new terms: one Ledgerleaf milestone, four days past due in February 2026. No late fee charged.
- Open at 2026-10-05: Copperline's bill pay change-order invoice (net 30, due 2026-10-30) and Tallowmere's deposit, paid on 2026-09-11.

## Why It Matters

The before-and-after numbers are the evidence that the deposit and net-15 terms work ([[contract-terms-update]]). It is also where [[paloma-ruiz]] checks what needs a reminder.

## Caveats

Days to payment are calendar days from the invoice date, not from the due date. January and February 2025 were re-keyed from the old spreadsheet.

## Source Location

Spreadsheet "Invoice register" in the business drive, refreshed from Paperbird's export on the first Monday of each month ([[business-records-folder]]).
