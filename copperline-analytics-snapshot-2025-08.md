# Copperline Analytics Snapshot 2025-08

A summary of Copperline's online banking analytics for August 2025, exported by Hollis Dunmore at my request in the first week of discovery. Aggregate figures only.

## Key Details

- 62% of online banking sessions came from phone browsers, 31% from desktop browsers, 7% from tablets. The vendor app is measured separately and was not in this export.
- The "Move money" menu was opened in 8% of sessions; transfers were completed in 3%.
- Bill pay payee list: median 9 payees per member who uses bill pay; some have over 40.
- Average session length on the transfer page was long relative to other pages, which fits members getting stuck.

## Why It Matters

The 62% figure became the strongest argument for responsive web first ([[copperline-responsive-first-decision-2026-02-20]]). The payee count explains why an unsorted list fails.

## Caveats

One month, no seasonality. Phone browser share may include some members who also use the app.
