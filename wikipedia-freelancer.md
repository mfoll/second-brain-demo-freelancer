# Freelancer

## Summary

The article covers freelancers as self-employed people not committed long-term to one employer: common fields, how freelancers find work (directly, through agencies or online platforms), the trade-offs in income stability and benefits, and the growth of platform-based freelancing.

## Why It Matters

Background for [[working-solo]] and for the podcast, where [[zainab-hussein]] asked me to define what I am ([[design-careers-podcast-recording-2026-08-12]]). I said "independent designer", not "freelancer", because clients hear "freelancer" as a pair of hands for hire. The article confirms the word covers both.

## Key Details

- The income-instability point is the [[feast-or-famine-cycle]] by another name.
- Overflow work for an agency, like my 2025 [[orchard-and-vane-overflow-contract-2025]], is one of the classic freelance arrangements.

## Caveats

Broad and international; says nothing about taxes or business structure that I can rely on. Those questions go to Delphine ([[run-the-business]]).
