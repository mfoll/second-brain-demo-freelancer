# Should Every Client Pay A Deposit?

Should the 50% deposit apply to every client, including repeat clients, large organisations and short day-rate jobs?

## Current Answer

Yes by default, with negotiated exceptions written into the schedule. Answered in June 2025 and confirmed in 2026.

- Repeat clients: yes. Ledgerleaf paid its 50% in December 2025 without comment.
- Large clients with procurement rules: a trade is acceptable. Copperline phase 2 has no deposit and net 30, in exchange for six fixed monthly milestone invoices ([[copperline-statement-of-work]]).
- Short day-rate jobs: still 50% of the estimate. Tallowmere paid its deposit three days before kickoff.

## Still to watch

For engagements of a week or less, a deposit and a final invoice ten days apart is admin for little protection. If a third short job comes along, try 100% on handoff with net 7 and compare ([[standard-services-agreement]]).
