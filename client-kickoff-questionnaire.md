# Client Kickoff Questionnaire

The questionnaire I send clients a week before kickoff, written in August 2025 after Crumbhollow and Thistlewood. Version 4 as of September 2026.

## Questions

1. What outcome would make this project a success, in a number if possible?
2. Who decides, who can say no, and who must be consulted?
3. What has already been tried, and why was it dropped?
4. What can we not change (platforms, contracts, brand rules, deadlines)?
5. Who are the users we can talk to, and who recruits them?
6. What data exists (analytics, support tickets, call logs)?
7. How do you prefer to give feedback, and how fast?
8. What would make you stop the project?

## Why It Matters

Question 2 found Desmond Okafor at Copperline; question 4 would have found the vendor app limit if I had asked it more precisely (version 4 now names "vendor contracts" explicitly, after [[copperline-native-app-concepts]]). Question 6 gave me Tallowmere's phone logs.
