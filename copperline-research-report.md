# Copperline Research Report

The discovery report I delivered to Copperline Credit Union at the 2025-11-14 readout: 14 member interviews and a baseline usability test of the current online banking site with 12 participants. It is the baseline for every later test.

## Summary

Members can see their balances fine. They struggle to move money. The baseline test scored 58 on the [[system-usability-scale]], and only 5 of 12 participants completed a transfer between their own accounts without help. Interviews explained why: transfers are hidden under a "Move money" menu, labels use internal banking terms, and many members phone the branch instead.

## Why It Matters

Every design decision in phase 2 traces back to a finding here, and every later result is compared with these numbers. The SUS 58 and 5 of 12 baseline, set against the prototype's 76 and 9 of 10 ([[copperline-usability-test-2026-05-21]]), is the clearest research outcome I have. It is exactly the evidence the Velora feedback said my case studies lacked ([[velora-feedback-call-2026-06-03]]), and I cannot use it publicly until Copperline approves ([[will-copperline-approve-a-case-study]]).

## Key Details

- **Interviews.** 14 members (P1 to P14), 45 to 60 minutes each, late September to early October 2025. Mix agreed with [[ana-lucia-ferreyra]]: 5 members over 60, 3 small-business members, 6 others; 4 interviews in a branch, 10 by video. Guide in [[copperline-interview-guide]].
- **Baseline test.** 12 participants (P1 to P12, a separate group), moderated, five tasks on the live site with test accounts. SUS 58. Task completion: check balance 12 of 12, find a past payment 10 of 12, transfer between own accounts 5 of 12, add a bill payee 4 of 12, download a statement 9 of 12.
- **Top findings.** 9 of 14 interviewees had never opened "Move money". 6 of 14 regularly phone the branch to move money. The payee list is unsorted and shows nicknames only. Teal text buttons were called hard to read by 4 of the 5 members over 60.
- **Channel data.** 62% of online banking sessions in August 2025 came from phone browsers, not the vendor app. This fed the responsive-first decision ([[copperline-responsive-first-decision-2026-02-20]]).
- **Recommendations.** Transfers and bill pay as top-level tasks; plain-language labels; a design system with accessible color; test again with a prototype before build.

## Caveats

Twelve participants is a small sample: the SUS score is an indication, not a precise measurement. Recruitment through member services skews towards members who already contact the credit union. Test accounts behaved slightly differently from real ones for scheduled transfers.
