# Structure Decisions Wait For Real Numbers

## Stance

Decisions that lock me in for years, like forming a company, should be made on a closed year's numbers, not on a forecast, even if waiting costs a little.

## Scope

Business structure and similar long commitments (a long lease, hiring). Not pricing, which I change quickly on purpose.

## Why I think this

- My 2025 projection was off by about $22,000 in six months ([[tax-return-2025-summary]]). A forecast in July 2026, after a lost pitch, was not better.
- The cost of waiting is bounded: about $1,000 to $1,500 for one quarter by Delphine's numbers ([[business-structure-review-paused-2026-08-03]]).
- Doing it mid-Copperline adds a contract change to the busiest months.

## What Would Change My Mind

[[delphine-arceneaux]] disagrees and may be right: if 2026 closes well above $90,000 profit, I will have paid for caution. If that happens, I should be quicker next time a numbers-based decision comes up.

## Implications

Resume in January 2027 with closed books; default to forming the company if the threshold is met.
