# Marrowgate Research Methods Course Syllabus

## Summary

Syllabus of the Marrowgate University Extension evening course in user research methods, summer 2026, taught by [[lorna-beckett|Lorna Beckett]]. Fifteen Tuesday sessions from 2026-06-09 to 2026-09-22 and a final presentation evening.

## Key Details

- Weeks 1 to 4: planning, interviews, questionnaires (including SUS and Likert items), contextual inquiry.
- Weeks 5 to 8: card sorting and IA, usability testing, unmoderated and remote tests.
- Weeks 9 to 12: diary studies, synthesis and thematic analysis, reporting.
- Weeks 13 to 15: ethics and consent, presenting research, project clinic.
- Assessment: a study plan, two studies run on a real product, a written report and a 12-minute presentation.
- Fee $1,650, paid on enrolment 2026-06-08.

## Why It Matters

The "two studies on a real product" requirement is why both studies ran on Lintel Kit ([[research-methods-course-2026]]).
