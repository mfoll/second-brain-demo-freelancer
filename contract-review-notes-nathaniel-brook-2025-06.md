# Contract Review Notes Nathaniel Brook 2025-06

[[nathaniel-brook]]'s marked-up draft of the new services agreement, returned on 2025-06-19, and my notes from our call the next day. Summarised; the marked-up file stays in the records folder.

## Key Details

- **Late fee:** state it as 1.5% a month on the overdue balance, from the due date. Drop my flat $50 idea; a percentage scales with the invoice and reads as standard.
- **Deposit:** call it a deposit credited against the fee, not "non-refundable". Non-refundable language invites a fight on termination.
- **IP:** move transfer to final payment and add a license to use drafts during the project. His strongest recommendation.
- **Pre-existing materials:** carve out my templates and Lintel Kit explicitly, with a perpetual license to the client for anything built in.
- **Kill fee:** removed. The termination clause already covers work done.
- **Liability cap:** fees paid under the schedule. He asked me to make professional liability cover a stated obligation so clients see it.
- **Portfolio clause:** written approval only. He warned that "we reserve the right to show the work" clauses get struck by procurement teams anyway.

## Why It Matters

These are the reasons behind each clause of the [[standard-services-agreement]]. When a client asks to change a term, check here first whether Nathaniel thought it mattered ([[contract-review-with-nathaniel-brook-2025-06-20]]).
