# Lintel Tiered Pricing Idea 2025

Archived. Replaced by a single $79 per-seat price at launch; history in [[lintel-pricing-history]].

The August 2025 idea: three tiers.

- Solo, $59: Figma library only.
- Studio, $149: library plus coded tokens, up to three seats.
- Team, $299: up to ten seats and priority email support.

Why I dropped it in September 2025:

- Three tiers meant three sets of files to package and three questions per buyer about which to choose.
- "Priority support" was a promise I could not keep while doing Copperline discovery.
- [[jae-won-seo]]'s revenue share is per seat with the coded tokens; a library-only tier complicated it.
- [[ellery-osei]]: "one license, one price, say no to custom terms."

It comes back in a different form as the team-license question ([[should-lintel-kit-get-a-team-license]]).
