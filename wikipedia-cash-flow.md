# Cash flow

## Summary

Introduces cash flow: payments made into or out of a business, project or financial product, usually discussed as expected future flows that have to be forecast. It covers how cash flow differs from profit and how cash flow statements are used.

## Why It Matters

Read the week after the Crumbhollow payment finally arrived ([[crumbhollow-final-payment-2025-07-04]]). The point that landed: a profitable year can still have weeks with no cash. My fixes were structural, not a forecast model: deposits, shorter terms and separate accounts ([[three-account-money-setup]], [[contract-terms-update]]).

## Key Details

- Being profitable does not mean having cash: a business can run short while reporting a profit.
- Businesses forecast expected cash flows because they are uncertain.
- Cash flow statements group flows into operating, investing and financing.
- Caveat: written for finance in general; nothing specific to small service businesses.
