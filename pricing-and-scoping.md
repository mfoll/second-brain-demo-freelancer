# Pricing And Scoping

How I price work and draw its edges: day rates, value-based options, paid first steps, change requests and the arithmetic of what a project actually earned. This is the retrieval surface; decisions live in the linked Events and the current rule lives in [[services-menu-2026]].

## The short history

- 2025: day rate of $650, fixed quotes built from days. [[crumbhollow-preorder-flow]] was quoted at 18 days and took 27, so I earned about $433 a day ([[crumbhollow-time-log]], [[effective-day-rate]]).
- 2025-11-20: day rates retired for all work after the [[pricing-model-review]] ([[pricing-decision-2025-11-20]]).
- 2026-06-18: day rate back at $720 for audits, workshops and work under 10 days ([[pricing-check-in-2026-06-18]]).
