# Portfolio Page Weight Check

## Summary

Measurements of my old portfolio pages in a browser's developer tools on 2026-05-07 and 2026-05-08, before and after quick fixes.

## Key Details

- Home: 6.8 MB before (two autoplay case study videos, 3.9 MB together; four font files). 1.9 MB after: posters instead of autoplay, one font family subsetted.
- Case study pages: 2.4 to 4.1 MB, mostly full-size screenshots. Not fixed yet; waiting for the [[portfolio-site-rebuild]].
- Load on a throttled slow connection: home from about 9 seconds to under 3.

## Why It Matters

My only measured case for a [[page-weight-budget]]. Too thin to put in proposals ([[does-sustainable-design-belong-in-proposals]]).
