# Lintel Diary Study Plan

## Summary

The plan for the Lintel Kit diary study, version 3, approved by [[lorna-beckett|Lorna Beckett]] on 2026-07-08.

## Key Details

- Question: how do buyers actually use Lintel Kit in their first weeks of real work, and where do they work around it?
- Participants: 6 buyers from different setups, pseudonymous P1 to P6, recruited by email to all buyers who opted in to updates.
- Duration: ten working days, 2026-07-13 to 2026-07-24.
- Prompts: two a day, by form, with optional screenshot. Version 1 had five prompts; version 2 removed leading wording ("What did you like...").
- Exit interview of 30 minutes, semi-structured.
- Incentive: a free V2 seat, given after the exit interview.
- Consent: [[lintel-diary-study-consent-form]].
