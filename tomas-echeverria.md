# Tomás Echeverría

Freelance brand designer and founding member of the Solo Studio Circle.

## Where He Matters

- In July 2025 he offered to share a small studio with me, $520 each on a 12-month lease. I chose a desk at Tidewell instead ([[should-i-rent-a-shared-studio-with-tomas]], archived; [[tidewell-commons-move]]). He found another studio partner and there was no friction.
- He is my sounding board on brand teams. Before the Copperline two-teal argument he warned me that a brand director would hear "your color fails" as "your brand fails", and to bring the decoration role for the original teal as an offer, not a concession. Desmond still objected, but the framing helped.
- Circle: brings the brand-side view to pricing; his projects are short and fixed-fee, so he never left value pricing.

## How We Work Together

Informal. We trade small referrals: brand work to him, product UI to me. Nothing written, and that is fine at this size.
