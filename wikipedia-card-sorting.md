# Card sorting

## Summary

Article on card sorting, a technique where participants sort items into groups to show how they categorise a domain. It is used in UX design and information architecture, with open (participants name the groups) and closed (groups given) variants.

## Why It Matters

Marrowgate week 5 was on information architecture. I have not run a card sort for a client yet; the obvious candidate is the Lintel Kit documentation, given the naming problem in the [[lintel-docs-unmoderated-test-results|unmoderated test]]. Course notes in [[marrowgate-course-notes]].

## Caveats

Light on analysis of results.
