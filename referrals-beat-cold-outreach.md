# Referrals Beat Cold Outreach

## Stance

For a solo designer at my stage, time spent with peers, past clients and former colleagues brings more won work than cold outreach or open RFPs.

## Why I Think This

- All seven won engagements since 2025 came through a person; none from cold channels ([[client-pipeline-tracker]], [[where-2026-clients-came-from]]).
- The only cold RFP I bid on cost six days and was lost ([[velora-pitch]]).
- Referred clients arrive pre-trusting. Ledgerleaf and Tallowmere never asked for references.

## What Would Change My Mind

- A cold lead won on the strength of the new portfolio after the relaunch.
- Referrals drying up: fewer than two referred leads in a quarter.

## Implications

- Keep the circle, Tidewell and past-client updates as the main sales activity ([[past-client-update-email-2026-q3]]).
- Answer cold RFPs only when they pass the stricter checks in [[should-i-answer-cold-rfps]].
- Caveat: 25 leads is a small sample, and I have barely tried cold channels, so this is partly self-fulfilling.
