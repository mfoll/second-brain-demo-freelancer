# Should Audits Include A Retest

Should an accessibility audit include a retest of the fixes, and if so, in the price or separately?

## Current Answer

Separately, but always offered at delivery. The audit quote lists the retest as an exclusion; the readout ends with a priced retest offer at the day rate, usually 2 days, valid for 12 months.

## Why

- At the Thistlewood readout the head of digital services asked what a retest would cost and I had no answer ([[thistlewood-audit-readout-2025-07-09]]). Odette Fairweather still wants one when the vendor upgrade lands.
- Including it in the price makes the audit look more expensive to a board comparing day rates ([[tallowmere-audit-quote-2026-09]]), and fixes often take longer than a year.
- A retest offer turns a one-off audit into a second engagement and keeps the fix list alive ([[an-audit-needs-a-ranked-fix-list]]).

## Applied

Tallowmere's readout on 2026-10-30 will end with a 2-day retest offer for spring 2027. Added to [[run-an-accessibility-audit]] as the last step.
