# Warm Referrals

A lead that arrives through someone who knows both me and the client, with context the client trusts. The opposite of a cold RFP.

## Why they win

- The client has already been told what I am good at, so the first call is about their problem, not my credentials.
- The referrer filters: Nadia only passed Ledgerleaf because she knew the scope fit ([[nadia-ferro-ledgerleaf-referral-2025-11-06]]).
- Price matters less. Ledgerleaf took the middle option without negotiation; Velora, a cold RFP, compared me line by line with an agency and I lost ([[velora-pitch]]).

## The numbers so far

Both 2026 engagements won from new opportunities came through peers: Ledgerleaf via the circle, Tallowmere via a Tidewell member ([[quentin-ashby-tallowmere-referral-2026-08-20]]). The one cold lead, Velora, was lost. Pipeline detail in [[client-pipeline-tracker]].

## What it asks of me

Being somewhere people see my work: the circle, Tidewell, Design Night. And sending referrals back, without fees, as the [[solo-studio-circle-ground-rules|circle rules]] say.
