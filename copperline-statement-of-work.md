# Copperline Statement Of Work

Summary of the statements of work between me and Copperline Credit Union: phase 1 discovery, phase 2 design foundations plus transfers and bill pay, and the June 2026 change order. The signed PDFs are not in this vault.

## Summary

Both phases sit under my [[standard-services-agreement]] as the master terms. Each statement of work names deliverables, dates, fee and payment schedule; the general idea is the one described in [[wikipedia-statement-of-work]]. Phase 1 was a fixed fee. Phase 2 was sold as three options and Copperline chose the middle one. The change order moved the bill pay handoff and added the payee search rework.

## Why It Matters

This is where to check what I owe Copperline and what Copperline owes me, before any scope conversation with [[priyanka-venkat]]. It is also the first engagement where a client negotiated my payment terms: net 30 instead of my net 15, in exchange for monthly milestone invoices instead of a 50% deposit. That trade is now an option I offer to larger clients.

## Key Details

**Phase 1, discovery.** Fixed fee $18,500. 2025-09-08 to 2025-11-14. Deliverables: research plan, 14 member interviews, baseline usability test with 12 participants, research report and readout. Paid 50% on signature and 50% on acceptance of the report. See [[copperline-discovery-research]].

**Native app concept sprint.** A separate one-page order, $9,800, November to December 2025, paid in December. Not part of phase 2. See [[copperline-native-app-concepts]].

**Phase 2, foundations plus transfers and bill pay.** Signed 2025-12-15. Three options proposed on 2025-12-02:

| Option | Scope | Fee |
|---|---|---|
| A | Design foundations only | $38,000 |
| B | Foundations plus transfers and bill pay, with one prototype test | $62,000 |
| C | B plus card controls and alerts | $88,000 |

Option B chosen. Six monthly milestone invoices of about $10,333 from January to June 2026, each net 30 and approved by Priyanka before it reaches finance. Late fee clause as in the standard agreement (1.5% a month), never used.

**Change order, June 2026.** Signed 2026-06-24 ([[copperline-bill-pay-change-order-2026-06-24]]). Bill pay handoff moved from 2026-07-31 to 2026-11-27; payee search rework and one extra retest added; handoff support for Hollis Dunmore's team through December 2026. Fee $7,500: half on signature, half on handoff.

**Other terms.** Copperline owns all deliverables on final payment of each phase. Research recordings and consent forms stay on Copperline's shared drive. I may not publish any work or results without written approval ([[will-copperline-approve-a-case-study]]). Subcontracting allowed with notice; I notified them of [[imani-clarke]] in April 2026.

## Caveats

The fee table is from my proposal; the signed statement of work restates option B only. Milestone dates slipped by two weeks in March 2026 when foundations overran, without a fee change.
