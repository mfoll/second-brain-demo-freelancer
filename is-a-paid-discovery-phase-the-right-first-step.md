# Is A Paid Discovery Phase The Right First Step?

## Question

Should option A in every project proposal be a paid discovery phase, or should it sometimes be a smaller design deliverable (a heuristic review, a redesigned first screen)?

## What I know

- Copperline: discovery led to phase 2 ([[copperline-discovery-research]]).
- Velora: the competitor's discovery phase won ([[velora-feedback-call-2026-06-03]]).
- Ledgerleaf's option A was a heuristic review and first-screen redesign, not discovery, and nobody picked it ([[ledgerleaf-proposal-2025-12]]).
- Since the [[proposal-template-2026]] only one project proposal has gone out, the housing cooperative one, still open.

## What would answer it

Three or four proposals with discovery as option A, and whether clients pick A, and whether A leads to B. Revisit in mid-2027.
