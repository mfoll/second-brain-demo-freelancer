# Generative AI

## Summary

Article on generative AI: models that learn patterns from training data and generate new text, images, video, audio or code, usually from natural language prompts. It covers history, model types, applications, and concerns including accuracy, bias, copyright and labour.

## Why It Matters

Background before my one real trial on research synthesis ([[ai-synthesis-trial-notes]]). The sections on accuracy and on training data were the reason I used only my own pseudonymised Lintel diary entries and nothing from clients. Cluster: [[ai-in-design-tools]].
