# Yvonne Takahashi

Retired design director and my informal mentor since about 2019, when she ran a portfolio review I signed up for. She has been through the solo years herself and is the person I call before a decision about money or direction.

## How we work together

Lunch roughly every two or three months, plus the odd email when something is urgent. I bring a specific question and a one-page summary; she prefers paper. I pay for lunch, which she accepts only because I insisted.

## What she has shaped

- Pricing. At lunch on 2025-10-16 she told me to price the outcome, not the time, and to keep a floor below which I walk away ([[yvonne-takahashi-pricing-lunch-2025-10-16]]). That fed the [[pricing-model-review]].
- Portfolio. On 2026-09-10 she read the Ledgerleaf and Thistlewood case studies and made me cut them by a third and put the result in the first paragraph ([[portfolio-review-with-yvonne-2026-09-10]]).
- The in-house offer in July 2026. She didn't tell me what to do; she asked what I would miss in each case, which is how I got to the answer ([[in-house-offer-declined-2026-07-24]]).
