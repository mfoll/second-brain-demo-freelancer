# Copperline Lessons So Far

What I would keep and change from 14 months of Copperline work, written for my future self and for the phase 3 proposal.

## Keep

- A paid discovery with a measured baseline. The SUS and completion numbers carried every later decision ([[paid-discovery-phase]]).
- Fixed task wording across tests, so results compare.
- Observers in the room for research ([[clients-should-watch-users-struggle]]).
- Role-named tokens; they made the second teal cheap.
- The Friday status email with "decisions needed" ([[copperline-weekly-status-emails]]).
- Raising the change order a week after the problem appeared, not at the deadline.

## Change

- Ask about vendor contracts on day one. The native app sprint answered a question one call could have ([[copperline-native-app-concepts]]).
- Meet the person who can say no in week one, not in the last hour of the kickoff ([[copperline-stakeholder-map]]).
- Ask for case-study terms in the statement of work, not after ([[will-copperline-approve-a-case-study]]).
- Check payee data quality before designing search over it ([[how-do-we-fix-payee-search]]).
- Do not let one client become 70% of the year ([[how-many-engagements-can-i-run-at-once]]).
