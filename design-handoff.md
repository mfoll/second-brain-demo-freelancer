# Design Handoff

The point where a design stops being mine to change freely and becomes something a development team builds. Also the package that makes it buildable.

## What I mean by it

A handoff is not a file link. It is a set of agreements: which frames are final, which tokens and components they use, what happens in error, empty, loading and long-content states, what is still open, and who answers questions after I leave. The procedure is in [[hand-off-designs-to-developers]].

## Why it matters for a freelancer

My engagements end. An in-house designer can answer a developer's question next week; I may be on another client. Weak handoffs show up later as support hours I did not price, or as a shipped design that no longer looks like mine. At Crumbhollow the handoff needed a second round of fixes, one unpaid day ([[crumbhollow-overrun-retrospective]]). Copperline's change order now includes paid handoff support through December 2026 ([[copperline-bill-pay-change-order-2026-06-24]]).

## Good signs

- The developer has seen the flows before handoff day (Callum Reyes paired with me; Hollis Dunmore attends design reviews).
- Edge cases are annotated in the design file, where developers look.
- A short recorded walkthrough exists.
