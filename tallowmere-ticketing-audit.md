# Tallowmere Ticketing Audit

Accessibility and usability audit of Tallowmere Theatre's online ticketing: seat selection and checkout. The first engagement quoted at the day rate after the June 2026 pricing check-in.

## Outcome

Accessibility and usability audit of seat selection and checkout delivered in 8 days at the $720 day rate.

## Current State

- Active. 6 of 8 days used as of 2026-10-23. Testing is done; I am writing the report and fix list.
- Fee $5,760 (8 x $720), 50% deposit paid on 2026-09-10 ([[tallowmere-audit-quote-2026-09]]).
- Readout to Marguerite Ostrowski and Teo Barros planned for 2026-10-30.
- Headline findings: the seat map cannot be used by keyboard or screen reader at all, seats are 16 pixels at default zoom, and the 8-minute seat hold expires without warning ([[tallowmere-seat-map-findings]]).

## Scope and Boundaries

Seat map, ticket type selection, basket, checkout and confirmation email, on desktop and phone. Not the theatre's donation flow or memberships. The ticketing platform is a vendor product; like Thistlewood, every issue is tagged by who can fix it.

## Working Context

Lead came from [[quentin-ashby]], a motion designer at Tidewell Commons who makes the theatre's trailers ([[quentin-ashby-tallowmere-referral-2026-08-20]], [[tallowmere-intro-call-2026-08-27]]). Marguerite asked for a day-rate quote because the board compares day rates across suppliers. That is exactly the case Rosalind Ng made at the [[pricing-check-in-2026-06-18]]: audit buyers compare day rates, so a value-based three-option proposal would have looked expensive and odd.

Method: [[run-an-accessibility-audit]], with two extra usability sessions with regular patrons recruited by Teo, one of them a wheelchair user who books companion seats. The companion seat booking is the most interesting finding: it only works by phone. Standards most cited so far: [[wcag-target-size-minimum-docs]], [[wcag-timing-adjustable-docs]], [[wcag-non-text-contrast-docs]].
