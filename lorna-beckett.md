# Lorna Beckett

Research methods instructor at Marrowgate University Extension; taught the evening course I took from June to September 2026. External contact with no hub in this vault.

## Where She Matters

- Reviewed my [[lintel-diary-study-plan]] three times. Most of her comments were on prompts that led participants ("What did you like about...") and on asking for too much: she cut my daily prompts from five to two.
- Pushed small, planned studies over large surveys, and insisted on a pilot before any unmoderated test. I skipped the pilot for my first task wording and lost two participants' data to it ([[lintel-docs-unmoderated-test-results]]).
- Chaired the final presentations on 2026-09-24 ([[marrowgate-final-presentation-2026-09-24]]) and asked the question I still think about: what would make me stop a study early.

Course only. She agreed to be a reference if I ever apply to teach a module, which I have not decided.
