# AI Synthesis Trial Notes

## Summary

My notes from comparing an AI assistant's clustering of the 41 Lintel diary entries with my own affinity map, done on 2026-09-26 and written up on 2026-09-28.

## Key Details

- Input: pseudonymised entries (P1 to P6) only, allowed by the [[lintel-diary-study-consent-form|consent form]]. No client data.
- Time: about 20 minutes including checking, against about an hour for my first sort.
- Result: 7 clusters against my 9. It merged token mapping and dark mode workarounds into one "theming issues" cluster; they led to different V2 decisions.
- Missed: one participant's early mention of a workaround that turned out to be shared by four. It came up again only in exit interviews, which I had and the tool did not.
- Good: it named clusters faster and suggested one I had not labelled (file organisation).

## Why It Matters

The evidence behind [[ai-should-not-write-my-research-findings]] and the open [[can-ai-tools-do-research-synthesis-for-me]].

## Caveats

One study, one tool, one person checking. Not a fair test of the method.
