# Case study

## Summary

The article defines a case study as an in-depth examination of one case in its real-world context, used across medicine, business, politics and other fields. It covers how cases are selected, how case studies are designed and the long-running debate about how far findings from one case generalise.

## Why It Matters

A useful correction while writing portfolio case studies for the [[portfolio-site-rebuild]]. A design case study borrows the name but usually behaves like a gallery. The research sense, one case examined in context with evidence, is what Velora wanted to see ([[velora-feedback-call-2026-06-03]]) and what [[evidence-led-case-studies]] tries to do.

## Key Details

- Case selection matters: I pick cases that show different kinds of evidence (a conversion change, an audit, a usability score), not my three prettiest projects ([[case-study-selection-2026]]).
- One case can argue and illustrate; it does not prove a general claim.

## Caveats

Academic article; nothing about portfolios or marketing.
