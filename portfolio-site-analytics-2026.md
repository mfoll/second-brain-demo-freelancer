# Portfolio Site Analytics 2026

Export of the old site's privacy-friendly analytics for January to June 2026, pulled on 2026-07-01.

## Key Details

- About 310 visits a month on average; March peaked at 520, the month of the Design Night talk.
- Most visited pages: home, the Crumbhollow gallery, about.
- Average time on a case study page: under 40 seconds.
- 4 contact form inquiries in six months: 2 job applications, 1 student, 1 audit lead (later dropped, see [[lost-deals-log]]).

## Why It Matters

Baseline for the relaunch. People visit; nothing makes them stay or write. Supports [[case-studies-should-show-outcomes]].

## Caveats

No cookies, so no tracking across visits; visits are approximate.
