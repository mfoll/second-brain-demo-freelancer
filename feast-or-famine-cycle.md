# Feast-Or-Famine Cycle

The solo freelancer's pattern of being too busy to look for work, then running out of work because nobody looked. The pipeline lags delivery by two to four months.

## How it showed up for me

- Spring 2025: overflow contract, Crumbhollow and Thistlewood at once, and no lead work. July and August 2025 were thin until Copperline started in September.
- Spring 2026: Copperline foundations, transfers and Ledgerleaf overlapped; I bid on Velora partly out of fear of a thin autumn.

## What I do about it

- One hour on the pipeline every Friday, booked or not ([[solo-weekly-rhythm]], [[client-pipeline-tracker]]).
- Look at the [[capacity-calendar-2026]] three months ahead; if anything after the next eight weeks is empty, I send the past-client update ([[past-client-update-email-2026-q3]]).
- Keep audits at a day rate so short gaps can be filled quickly ([[pricing-check-in-2026-06-18]]).
- Six months of costs in the business account, set by [[run-the-business]].
