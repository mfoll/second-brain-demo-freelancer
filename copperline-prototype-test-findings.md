# Copperline Prototype Test Findings

The scoring sheet and findings log from the 2026-05-21 prototype test, delivered with a 12-page findings summary on 2026-05-29.

## Key Details

- Per-participant SUS scores (mean 76, range 60 to 92) and task outcomes.
- 27 findings, each with severity, frequency (n of 10), the flow and a proposed fix. 4 high, 11 medium, 12 low.
- The four high findings: payee search by company name (3 of 10 failed); transfer entry point missing on account details (1 of 10 but blocking); currency read twice by screen reader; scheduled transfer date picker unclear about weekends.
- Status column updated through October: 24 of 27 fixed in design; payee search in iteration 3; two low findings deferred to phase 3.
- A retest tab for the payee search, added after [[copperline-payee-search-retest-2026-10-15]].
