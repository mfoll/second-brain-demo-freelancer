# Lintel Team License

Decide whether to sell Lintel Kit to teams under one license, and launch it or reject it with reasons.

## Outcome

A team license (draft $249 for up to 10 seats) launched or rejected with reasons.

## Current State

- Idea. Opened on 2026-09-21 after a working session on the requests ([[team-license-pricing-session-2026-09-21]]).
- Nine buyers have asked for team terms since January 2026; the largest is [[calvin-moyo]]'s team of 12 designers ([[lintel-team-license-requests]]).
- Draft: $249 for up to 10 seats, one named admin who receives updates and support ([[lintel-team-license-draft-terms]]).
- Two blockers: support load from teams, and renegotiating [[jae-won-seo]]'s 20% share, which is defined per seat.
- Decision after V2 ships, so the team license sells the better product ([[should-lintel-kit-get-a-team-license]]).

## Scope and Boundaries

Team pricing and terms only. Enterprise terms (invoices, procurement forms, custom license text) are out: I will not do them alone ([[the-kit-must-not-eat-client-time]]).

## Working Context

Today a team buys one seat per designer at $79; Calvin would pay $948 for 12. The draft $249 for 10 seats is a big discount per seat, deliberately: most requests come from teams of 4 to 8 who currently buy one seat and share it, which the license does not allow ([[lintel-kit-license-terms]]). The bet is that a cheap legal option converts sharing into revenue. The risk is that teams of 10 who would have paid $790 pay $249.

[[ellery-osei]] advised one simple license and no custom terms. Calvin asked for an invoice with his company's purchase order number, which Gumroad does not do natively; that is the kind of request I expect more of.
