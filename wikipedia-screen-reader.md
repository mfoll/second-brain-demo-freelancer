# Screen reader

## Summary

Encyclopedia article on screen readers: assistive technology that renders text and images as speech or braille, essential for blind people and useful for people with low vision, learning disabilities or other print disabilities. The lead explains that they convey what is on screen through text-to-speech, sound cues or a braille device, using accessibility APIs, operating system features and hooking techniques.

## Why It Matters

Read while setting up NVDA and VoiceOver for the Thistlewood screen reader testing, alongside the Sablewing course ([[accessibility-deep-dive-2025]]). Useful background to send clients who have never seen a screen reader used.
