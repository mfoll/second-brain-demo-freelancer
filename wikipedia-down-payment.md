# Down payment

## Summary

Defines a down payment: an initial partial payment made up front when buying expensive goods or services, usually at the time the deal is finalised, with the remainder paid later, often through a loan.

## Why It Matters

Read alongside [[wikipedia-late-fee]] during the [[contract-terms-update]]. My 50% deposit is a down payment in this sense: it commits the client before I block my calendar and halves the money at risk if they pay the rest late. The article is about cars and houses, but the logic of putting something down first is the same ([[deposits-protect-the-relationship]]).

## Key Details

- A down payment is a partial payment at the start of a purchase.
- It lowers the lender's risk and signals that the buyer can raise money and is not overextended.
- The article focuses on homes and cars financed by loans; nothing about service businesses.
- My terms: 50% before the first working day ([[standard-services-agreement]]).
