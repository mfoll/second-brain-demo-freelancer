# Can AI Tools Do Research Synthesis For Me?

## Question

Can an AI tool take over the first pass of research synthesis (grouping observations into themes) well enough to save real time on client projects, without losing findings?

## What I Know

- One trial on 41 Lintel diary entries ([[ai-synthesis-trial-notes]]): about 40 minutes saved on the first sort, two distinct themes merged, one important workaround missed.
- Client work is blocked anyway without written consent to put research data in a third-party tool. Copperline would not give it.
- [[imani-clarke|Imani]] has tried something similar on interview transcripts; we have not compared notes yet.

## What Would Answer It

A side-by-side on a second study, with Imani doing the human synthesis blind. If the tool finds the same top five themes, I would use it for first passes on my own data.
