# Copperline Brand Guidelines 2019

Copperline Credit Union's brand guidelines, 2019 edition, owned by Desmond Okafor. A 48-page PDF made by their previous agency.

## Summary

Logo, brand teal as the single primary color, a warm gray secondary palette, one typeface family, photography style. The digital section is two pages: teal buttons with white labels, teal text links, no guidance on contrast, focus states or dark backgrounds.

## Why It Matters

The guidelines predate WCAG 2.2 and were never checked for contrast. The two-page digital section is the source of the failing buttons, so it was the first thing I read in [[copperline-design-foundations]]. Desmond treats the document as binding; arguing from his own goal of recognition, not from the document's mistakes, worked better.

## Caveats

Confidential brand material; I describe it and do not reproduce it.
