# Staying Solo Is Deliberate

## Stance

Working alone is the model, not a phase before building a studio. I partner for capacity and don't hire.

## Why I Think This

- I left Orchard & Vane to do the design work, not to manage. Hiring would put me back in management within a year.
- Named partners solve the capacity problem buyers see ([[capacity-signalling]]).
- Four to six clients a year plus the kit is enough revenue at my costs.
- I turned down a $148,000 in-house role in July 2026 ([[in-house-offer-declined-2026-07-24]]); the reasons hold.

## What Would Change My Mind

- Losing two more large bids on capacity grounds despite named partners.
- Copperline-sized work becoming the norm rather than the exception.
- A partner who wants to build something with me, not for me.

## Implications

No studio name, no "we" on the site. Proposals say "I" and name partners.
