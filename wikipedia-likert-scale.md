# Likert scale

## Summary

Article on the Likert scale, the psychometric scale named after Rensis Likert and the most widely used way of scaling answers in survey research. It covers item format, the difference between a Likert item and a scale, and arguments about treating the answers as interval data.

## Why It Matters

SUS is built from Likert items, and the debate about averaging ordinal answers came up in Marrowgate week 2. It is one reason I report SUS with a range and with completion rates, never alone ([[score-a-sus-questionnaire]]).
