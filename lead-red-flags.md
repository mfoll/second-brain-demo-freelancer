# Lead Red Flags

Things in a first email or call that predicted trouble, collected from 2025 and 2026. Not reasons to refuse on their own; reasons to price carefully and ask more.

- "It's a small job, should only take a few days." Crumbhollow said this.
- No named decision-maker after two calls.
- Asks for free spec work or a "quick mockup to see your style".
- Wants a day rate for a project with no defined scope.
- Payment terms questions before scope questions.
- "Our last designer didn't work out" without saying why.
- Budget field left empty on the [[website-inquiry-form]] and "not sure" on timing.
- Asks me to sign their contract with net 60 or longer (I use the [[standard-services-agreement]]).

Used in step 2 of [[qualify-a-new-lead]].
