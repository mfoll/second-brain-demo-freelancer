# Service blueprint

## Summary

Article on the service blueprint, a process chart of service delivery that shows customer actions alongside internal operations. It describes the usual lanes (customer actions, onstage and backstage staff actions, support processes) and the line of visibility between what customers see and what they do not.

## Why It Matters

I used its lanes to draw the [[crumbhollow-pickup-blueprint-sketch]] the next day. My working note is [[service-blueprint]].

## Caveats

Describes the tool, not how to run a workshop with staff, which is what I would need to sell it ([[should-i-offer-service-blueprinting]]).
