# Copperline Prototype Test Plan

Plan and moderator script for the 2026-05-21 prototype test, written by me and reviewed by Imani Clarke.

## Key Details

- 10 participants, quotas as in discovery ([[copperline-participant-recruitment-log]]).
- Six tasks. Tasks 1 and 3 reuse the discovery baseline wording word for word (transfer between own accounts; add a payee). Tasks 2, 4, 5 and 6 are new: scheduled transfer, pay existing payee, member-to-member transfer, find a past transfer.
- Success criteria written per task before the test: unaided completion, no wrong account selected, confirmation recognised.
- SUS administered on paper at the end, before any debrief ([[system-usability-scale]]).
- Moderator rules shared with Imani: no hints until the participant has been stuck for 90 seconds; a hint counts as assisted.
- Observer room rules for Copperline staff: no talking to participants, questions on sticky notes.

## Why It Matters

The fixed wording and per-task success rules are why the 5 of 12 versus 9 of 10 comparison holds. Imani and I scored each other's sessions blind on two tasks to check consistency; we agreed on 19 of 20 judgments.
