# Imani Diary Study Prompts

## Summary

A set of diary study prompts and reminder messages [[imani-clarke|Imani Clarke]] lent me on 2026-06-29, from a past study of her own with the client details removed.

## Key Details

- Her rule: one "what happened" prompt and one "what got in the way" prompt per day, never an opinion prompt.
- Reminder wording that does not guilt people ("No entry yesterday? That's fine, today counts too").
- A mid-study check-in call on day 5 for anyone who has gone quiet.

## Why It Matters

My [[lintel-diary-study-plan]] version 2 took her two-prompt structure; Lorna then approved it. Her day-5 check-in would probably have stopped my mid-study drop.
