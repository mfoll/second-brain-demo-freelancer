# Copperline Discovery Research Plan

Four-page research plan agreed at the [[copperline-kickoff-2025-09-08]].

## Summary

Three research questions: which online banking tasks members do and avoid; where the current site fails them; what members do instead. Two methods: 14 semi-structured interviews and a moderated baseline usability test with 12 participants on the live site, five tasks, SUS at the end.

## Key Details

- Recruitment by [[ana-lucia-ferreyra]] from member services, with quotas: at least 4 members over 60, at least 2 small-business members, at least 1 assistive technology user.
- Incentive: $50 account credit, paid by Copperline.
- Consent forms Copperline's own; recordings kept on Copperline's drive for 12 months.
- Tasks for the test fixed in advance so the prototype test could repeat them ([[copperline-prototype-test-plan]]).
- Timeline: interviews weeks 2 to 4, test weeks 6 and 7, analysis weeks 8 and 9, readout 2025-11-14.

## Why It Matters

Keeping the task wording fixed is what made the SUS 58 to 76 comparison valid. Worth copying into every multi-phase engagement.
