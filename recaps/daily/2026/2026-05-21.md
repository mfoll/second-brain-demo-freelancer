# Thursday, May 21, 2026

## Copperline prototype test

A full day of moderated sessions at Copperline with [[imani-clarke|Imani]] taking four of them ([[copperline-usability-test-2026-05-21|usability test]]). The transfers work: the questionnaire score rose well above the discovery baseline and nearly everyone moved money unaided. These are the first numbers I have that show a redesign of mine changing what people can do, measured the same way before and after ([[system-usability-scale|System Usability Scale]]).

## What did not work

Payee search. People type the company name and search only knows nicknames. Three people gave up or picked the wrong payee. It goes to rework ([[how-do-we-fix-payee-search|payee search question]]); I would rather find this now than after handoff.

## Moments worth keeping

Renata watched a member in her seventies make a transfer in under a minute and said it was the first time she had seen the project work. Working with Imani made a day of ten sessions feel manageable, and it is exactly the named partner Velora said I lacked.
