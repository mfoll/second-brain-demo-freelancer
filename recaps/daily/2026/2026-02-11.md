# Wednesday, February 11, 2026

## Copperline gets two teals

The [[copperline-accessibility-review-2026-02-11|accessibility review]] at Copperline settled the palette question: the brand teal stays for decoration and large type, and a darker text teal carries text, links and filled buttons. The contrast numbers and the options I showed are in the [[copperline-palette-contrast-audit|palette contrast audit]].

## Who moved the room

[[desmond-okafor|Desmond]] argued hard for brand-teal buttons and called the dark teal "the hospital color". I thought we would stall. [[gideon-marsh|Gideon]] mentioned member complaints about unreadable buttons and that ended it. Renata agreed in principle and wants to see the whole component set before signing off. Giving Desmond ownership of where the brand teal goes was the best thing I said all day.

## Afterwards

The color tokens need renaming by role, not color, before Renata sees the full set ([[copperline-token-set|token set]]). And the two-teal story now belongs in next month's [[design-night-talk-2026|Design Night talk]].
