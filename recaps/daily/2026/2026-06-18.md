# Thursday, June 18, 2026

## Day rates are partly back

At the June circle session I reviewed the November rule that dropped day rates for all work ([[pricing-decision-2025-11-20|original pricing decision]]) and partly reversed it ([[pricing-check-in-2026-06-18|pricing check-in]]). Short work, meaning audits, workshops and anything under ten days, goes back to a day rate. Bigger projects keep the three value-based options, now with a paid first step. The rule as I will apply it is in [[pricing-rules-2026|pricing rules 2026]].

## Rosalind was right

[[rosalind-ng|Rosalind]] made the same argument as in November, with numbers this time, and the [[circle-rate-survey-2026-06|circle rate survey]] backed her. Audit buyers compare day rates because their forms ask for one. The two audit leads that went quiet this spring are the evidence I ignored. Nadia argued the other side, and her point about capped upside is fair for product work, less so for an audit.

## Velora in the background

The [[velora-pitch|Velora loss]] was not about day rates, but it is why I was willing to reopen the whole model rather than defend it.
