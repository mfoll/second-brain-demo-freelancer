# Friday, February 20, 2026

## Responsive web first

Copperline's board approved Renata's recommendation: redesign online banking as a responsive site first and leave the vendor app alone until its contract renews ([[copperline-responsive-first-decision-2026-02-20|responsive-first decision]]). Hollis's contract review made the case almost by itself.

## Closing the app work

I marked the [[copperline-native-app-concepts|native app concepts]] project superseded and archived the concept deck. It stings a little; it was good work that nobody could have built. The old question about starting with the app is closed too.

## What it means for me

No app phase in 2026, which takes a possible chunk of revenue off the year. In exchange, transfers and bill pay get designed phone-width first, which suits the members anyway. Replanned the [[copperline-transfers-and-bill-pay|transfers and bill pay]] start for March 2.
