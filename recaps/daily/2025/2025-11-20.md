# Thursday, November 20, 2025

## No more day rates

I presented my 2025 numbers at the November circle session and, walking home, decided to stop quoting day rates for any work. Every proposal now gets three value-based options, with my old day rate kept only as a private floor. The full reasoning, the Crumbhollow arithmetic and the rejected alternatives are in the [[pricing-decision-2025-11-20|pricing decision]]; it closes the [[pricing-model-review|pricing model review]].

## What the circle said

[[nadia-ferro|Nadia]] and [[tomas-echeverria|Tomás]] backed the switch from their own practice. [[rosalind-ng|Rosalind]] did not, for one kind of work: she thinks audit buyers compare day rates and that options on a two-week audit look like hiding the price. I wrote her objection down as a risk and kept the rule for everything. I am not sure that was right, and want to look again in six months.

Ledgerleaf will be the first proposal under the rule, then Copperline phase 2.
