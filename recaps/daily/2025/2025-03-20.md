# Thursday, March 20, 2025

## First Solo Studio Circle session

Lunchtime at Tidewell, all six founders there. The [[solo-studio-circle-first-session-2025-03-20|first session]] turned into a rates round on anonymous cards, and the spread surprised everyone, me included. The two people with the highest rates are the ones who quote fixed fees and only talk days when asked. We agreed to meet every third Thursday; ground rules next time ([[solo-studio-circle-ground-rules|ground rules]]).

## Crumbhollow, honestly

I talked about the [[crumbhollow-preorder-flow|Crumbhollow pre-order flow]], six weeks in and already over plan. [[imani-clarke|Imani]] asked how I had estimated the days, and the true answer is that I used agency estimates built for a team. Nobody told me what to do about it. I wrote "estimate for one person, not for Orchard & Vane" on the train home.

## Afternoon

Back on Crumbhollow screens until six. Still on my $650 day rate; still not sure it is the problem.
