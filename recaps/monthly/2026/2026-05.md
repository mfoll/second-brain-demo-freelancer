# May 2026

A month of one loss and one clear success, a week apart.

## Velora pitch, submitted and lost

The prototype test on [[recaps/daily/2026/2026-05-21|May 21]] showed the redesigned transfers working, measured the same way as the discovery baseline ([[copperline-usability-test-2026-05-21|usability test]]). Payee search failed for some participants and went to rework. Imani's subcontract worked well and is a model for future partner work.
