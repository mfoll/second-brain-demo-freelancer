# November 2025

The busiest month since I went independent: a product launch, a discovery readout, a pricing decision and two proposals.

## Lintel Kit launched

[[lintel-kit|Lintel Kit]] went on sale on [[recaps/daily/2025/2025-11-12|November 12]]. Sales were better than I planned for and support was heavier; the lessons are in the [[lintel-kit-v1-launch-retrospective|launch retrospective]]. Two weeks later the [[first-token-bug-report-2025-11-26|first token bug]] came in from a buyer.

## Copperline discovery delivered

The [[copperline-discovery-readout-2025-11-14|discovery readout]] closed [[copperline-discovery-research|Copperline discovery research]], with a baseline score low enough to justify the whole program. Native app concepts started the following week.

## Day rates dropped

On [[recaps/daily/2025/2025-11-20|November 20]] I decided to stop quoting day rates and send three value-based options for all work ([[pricing-decision-2025-11-20|pricing decision]]). Rosalind's objection about audits was noted and set aside, a choice I revisited in June 2026.

## New work under the new rule

Ledgerleaf came through [[nadia-ferro|Nadia]] and the circle; the proposal went out at the end of the month ([[ledgerleaf-proposal-2025-12|Ledgerleaf proposal]]). The Copperline phase 2 options were drafted in parallel.

## Open at month end

- Whether three options work for a client who only wants to know the day rate.
- How much weekly support the kit will need once launch week is over.
