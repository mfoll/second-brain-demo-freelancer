# Sablewing Course Syllabus

## Summary

My copy of the outline for Sablewing Academy's practical accessibility course, 2025 cohort: 12 modules over seven months, live review sessions every second Wednesday with [[marisol-vance|Marisol Vance]], a mid-course exercise and a two-hour final assessment.

## Key Details

- Modules: 1 foundations and WCAG structure; 2 perceivable content and color; 3 keyboard and focus; 4 screen readers; 5 structure and landmarks; 6 inclusive design and cognition; 7 forms and errors; 8 dynamic content and status messages; 9 mobile and touch; 10 documents; 11 writing audit reports; 12 assessment preparation.
- Fee $1,200, paid 2025-01-28. Estimated 80 to 100 hours; my log says about 95.
- Pass requires the mid-course exercise and the final assessment.

## Why It Matters

I picked it over two cheaper courses for the live reviews and the practical final ([[learning-plan-2025]]). Module 4 landing before the Thistlewood kickoff is what made [[accessibility-deep-dive-2025]] work.
