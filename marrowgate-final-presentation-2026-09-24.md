# Marrowgate Final Presentation 2026-09-24

Final presentations for the Marrowgate University Extension research methods course: 14 students, 12 minutes each, chaired by [[lorna-beckett|Lorna Beckett]].

## My presentation

The two Lintel studies as one story: what buyers struggle with over ten days ([[lintel-diary-study-findings]]) and what new users struggle with in ten minutes ([[lintel-docs-unmoderated-test-results]]). Main point: the diary study found the dark mode workaround and the token mapping problem; the unmoderated test found the naming problem. Neither method would have found all three.

## Questions

- Lorna: what would have made me stop the diary study early. I had no answer then. Now: if fewer than three participants were still writing by day 5.
- Another student asked why I did not use SUS for the diary study. Because it measures a single session's usability, not use over time.
