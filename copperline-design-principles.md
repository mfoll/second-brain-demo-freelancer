# Copperline Design Principles

Five principles agreed with Priyanka Venkat's team in January 2026, drawn from the discovery findings. We use them to settle design review disagreements.

1. **Money tasks first.** Transfers and bill pay are one tap from every account. No "Move money" menus. From: 9 of 14 interviewees never opened that menu ([[copperline-research-report]]).
2. **Say it back.** Every confirmation restates amount, from, to and date in one plain sentence.
3. **Members' words, not ours.** "Move money to savings", not "internal transfer". Labels tested with members.
4. **Readable by everyone.** WCAG 2.2 AA as the floor; contrast decided in tokens ([[color-contrast-for-text]]).
5. **Phone first.** Designed at phone width, then widened ([[copperline-responsive-first-decision-2026-02-20]]).

## How they are used

Each design review agenda lists which principle a disputed decision touches. Desmond Okafor has invoked principle 4 twice himself, which I count as progress.
