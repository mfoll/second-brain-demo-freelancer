# Client Relationships

Retrieval surface for how I work with clients once they are clients: kickoffs, who decides what, scope changes, feedback, late payments and how relationships turn into referrals or repeat work.

## Patterns I keep relearning

- Find the person who can say no early. At Copperline it was Desmond Okafor on brand, not the sponsor ([[copperline-stakeholder-map]]).
- Put scope changes on paper the same week, as a [[change-order]], even when the client is friendly. Crumbhollow taught me what happens otherwise ([[crumbhollow-overrun-retrospective]]).
- Invite decision makers to watch research live. Nothing moved Copperline faster than Renata Solberg watching P7 fail a transfer ([[clients-should-watch-users-struggle]]).
- Ask for a feedback survey at the end of each engagement ([[client-feedback-survey-results-2025]]).
