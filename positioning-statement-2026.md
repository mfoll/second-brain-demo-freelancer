# Positioning Statement 2026

The one line on the home page and in the about section of proposals, rewritten in July 2026 for the [[portfolio-site-rebuild]].

## Current version

"I'm an independent product designer. I redesign the hard parts of digital services, like banking, onboarding and checkout, so that more people can finish them, including people who use assistive technology."

## Earlier versions

- 2024: "UX/UI designer, formerly at Orchard & Vane." Says where I was, not what I do.
- Spring 2026: "Product designer specialising in accessible design systems." Too narrow for Copperline-type work, too vague for audit buyers.

## Notes

- "Finish them" is deliberate: every case study has a completion number.
- Not using "studio" or "we" ([[staying-solo-is-deliberate]]).
- Whether to go further toward accessibility is open ([[should-i-niche-down-to-accessibility]]).
