# Workspace Cost Comparison

## Summary

Spreadsheet comparing three workspace options for twelve months, made between 2025-07-14 and 2025-07-18.

## Key Details

| Option | Monthly | Year one | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Home office | about $45 | about $540 | meeting rooms by the hour for tests and interviews |
| Tidewell dedicated desk | $340 | $4,080 | 4 room hours included, monthly rolling |
| Shared studio with Tomás | $520 | $6,840 | 12-month lease, about $600 furniture |

- Also scored: isolation, testing space, call quality, commute (12 minutes to Tidewell).
- Home won on money, Tidewell on everything else. The studio lost on the lease ([[should-i-rent-a-shared-studio-with-tomas]]).
