# Day Rates Suit Small Fixed Work

## Stance

For audits, workshops and anything under 10 days, a clear day rate times a fixed number of days beats value-based options. Above 10 days, options win.

## Scope

My own practice, with buyers who are product managers, small-organization directors and procurement. Not Lintel Kit, which is a product.

## Why I Think This

- Audit buyers compare suppliers on day rates; their forms ask for one. Two audit leads went quiet after options in spring 2026 ([[lost-deals-log]]).
- Working out the value of an 8-day audit costs a day I can't bill.
- Tallowmere asked for a day rate outright and signed within days of the quote ([[tallowmere-audit-quote-2026-09]]).
- Rosalind's win rate on day-rate audits ([[pricing-check-in-2026-06-18]]).

## What Would Change My Mind

- If a short job routinely turns out worth much more to the client than the days (say, an audit that unblocks a large contract), options would capture that.
- If day-rate audits start overrunning the fixed days the way Crumbhollow did.

## Implications

- Quote small work fast, from [[services-menu-2026]].
- Keep the rate under review once a year ([[when-should-the-day-rate-rise]]).
