# Ledgerleaf Onboarding Interview Notes

Synthesis of 8 interviews with recent Ledgerleaf trial users (P1 to P8), January 2026: 4 who sent a first invoice and 4 who did not.

## Key Details

- The non-senders all stopped at business details. Three said they did not have their tax ID to hand and meant to come back; none did.
- Senders had all signed up with a real invoice to send that day. Intent at signup mattered more than any screen.
- Two did not know what "chart of accounts" meant, and it appeared on the second screen.
- Six wanted to see a finished invoice before giving any business details.
- Recruited and paid ($40 voucher) by [[ifeoma-nwosu]]; 30 minutes each by video.

## Why It Matters

The "show a draft invoice in the first minute, ask for the tax ID at send" design came straight from these notes, and it moved the rate from 31% to 44% ([[ledgerleaf-trial-funnel-metrics]]).
