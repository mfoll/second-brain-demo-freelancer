# How Do I Handle Scope Requests Mid Project

When a client asks for something extra during an engagement, how do I respond without either absorbing it or souring the relationship?

## Current Answer

Say yes to the idea, then put it on paper within the week: a one-page [[change-order]] with the change, the date impact and the fee (or "no fee", stated). Anything under half a day I just do and mention in the weekly status. Anything over gets a change order, without exception.

## How I got here

- Crumbhollow: the cake form grew from 4 to 11 options and pickup slots grew shop-specific rules; 9 extra days absorbed, effective rate $433 a day ([[crumbhollow-overrun-retrospective]]).
- Copperline: the payee search failure became a $7,500 change order a week after the test, signed without argument ([[copperline-bill-pay-change-order-2026-06-24]]).
- The [[standard-services-agreement]] now has a change clause I can point to, which makes the conversation impersonal.

## Phrases that work

"Happy to add that. It moves the date by about a week; I'll send a one-page change so we both have it in writing." Clients have never pushed back on the paper itself, only occasionally on the fee.
