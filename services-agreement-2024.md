# Services Agreement 2024

My first contract template, adapted in October 2024 from the agency template I knew from Orchard & Vane. Retired on 2025-06-27 and replaced by the [[standard-services-agreement]]. Kept for the Crumbhollow and early 2025 engagements, which were signed on it.

## Summary

Five pages. Net 30 on all invoices, no deposit, invoices at milestones chosen per project, no late fee, IP transferred on delivery rather than on payment, termination with 30 days' notice.

## What was wrong with it

- No deposit, so a client could take half the work before paying anything.
- IP on delivery gave me no leverage once the files were handed over.
- No late fee and no right to pause work, which is why the Crumbhollow reminders could only ask nicely ([[crumbhollow-final-payment-2025-07-04]]).
- Agency language about account teams and "the Agency" that I had half-edited out.
