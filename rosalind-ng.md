# Rosalind Ng

Freelance content designer and co-founder of the Solo Studio Circle. The person most likely to tell me I am wrong about money, and usually right.

## Role

Content designer working on product copy, content audits and style guides, independent since 2021. We met on an Orchard & Vane client project in 2022 where she was the client's contractor.

## Where She Matters

- Co-founded the circle with me in early 2025 ([[solo-studio-circle-launch]]); she had the Tidewell membership that got us the room.
- Pricing: in November 2025 she supported my move away from day rates for projects but warned it would not fit audits. At the June 2026 session ([[pricing-check-in-2026-06-18]]) she brought three audit requests she had received in 2026, each asking bidders for a day rate and a number of days, and argued that audit buyers compare day rates line by line. That, plus the Velora loss, brought back my $720 day rate for audits, workshops and work under 10 days.
- She runs content audits that pair well with my accessibility audits; we have talked about bidding together.

## How We Work Together

We alternate hosting the circle when nobody else volunteers. She prefers to disagree in the room and then let it go. When she sends me a lead it comes with a short note on budget and the buyer's temper.
