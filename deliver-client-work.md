# Deliver Client Work

Everything between a signed agreement and a clean handoff: the engagements themselves, research with real users, the design deliverables and the people on the client side. It never ends because there is always a next client, and how I deliver is the main thing clients remember.

## Scope

- Client engagements from kickoff to handoff, including change orders and post-launch check-ins.
- Research with users inside an engagement (interviews, usability tests, audits with assistive technology).
- Client contacts as Person notes, with what they care about and how they like to work.
- Not here: proposals, pricing and leads ([[win-new-work]]), contracts, invoices and late payments ([[run-the-business]]), case studies ([[portfolio-and-marketing]]).
