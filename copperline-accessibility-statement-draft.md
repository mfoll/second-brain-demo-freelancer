# Copperline Accessibility Statement Draft

Notes for the one-page accessibility statement Gideon Marsh asked for at launch. Not yet sent; the final text will live with Copperline's legal pages.

## Planned contents

- Commitment: the redesigned online banking aims to meet WCAG 2.2 AA ([[wikipedia-web-content-accessibility-guidelines]]).
- What was done: accessible color and components in the design system, testing with members who use assistive technology in every research round, a screen reader check of each flow.
- Known limits at launch: sign-in still blocks paste in the code field ([[wcag-accessible-authentication-minimum-docs]]); payee names without clean data; the vendor mobile app is outside this redesign.
- How to report a problem: member services, with a response time Copperline sets.
- Date of last review.

## Open points

- Gideon wants no claim of full conformance. Agreed: "aims to meet" and a list of known issues.
- Who maintains it after I leave: Priyanka Venkat's team. Include the review date in their handoff notes ([[hand-off-designs-to-developers]]).
