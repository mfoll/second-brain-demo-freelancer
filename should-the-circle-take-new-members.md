# Should The Circle Take New Members?

## Question

The Solo Studio Circle capped itself at seven in April 2026. Two people have asked to join since: a freelance service designer and a UX writer. Do we raise the cap, start a second circle, or keep a waiting list?

## What I Know

- Seven fits the small room and a 90-minute lunch; everyone speaks.
- A service designer would fill a gap nobody covers (see [[service-design]]). The UX writer overlaps with [[rosalind-ng|Rosalind]].
- [[tomas-echeverria|Tomás]] wants a second circle that he would host; nobody else has volunteered to co-host.
- Trust took a year. Adding two people at once changes the room.

## Current Leaning

Take the service designer as eighth member on a three-session trial and help Tomás start a second circle for the writer and others. To decide at the 2026-11-19 session.
