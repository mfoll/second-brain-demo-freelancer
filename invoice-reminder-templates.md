# Invoice Reminder Templates

The three reminders I send, rewritten in July 2025 after Crumbhollow. Every one asks for a date, because the reminder that asked a direct question was the one that got an answer ([[crumbhollow-payment-reminders]]).

**1. Day after the due date.** "Hi [name], invoice [number] for [amount] was due yesterday. Could you let me know when I can expect payment? Invoice attached again in case it got lost."

**2. Seven days overdue.** "Hi [name], following up on invoice [number], now a week overdue. Is anything holding it up on your side, and what date should I expect? Under our agreement a late fee of 1.5% a month applies from [date]; I would rather not need it."

**3. Fourteen days overdue.** Phone call first, then an email confirming what was said and the agreed date. Mention that work pauses after 30 days overdue, as the [[standard-services-agreement]] allows.

Rules: send from my own address, never from Paperbird's automatic sender; copy the person who approved the work, not only accounts payable. [[paloma-ruiz]] drafts the first two; I send them.

Used since July 2025: eleven first reminders, two second reminders, no third.
