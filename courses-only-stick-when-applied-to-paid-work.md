# Courses Only Stick When Applied To Paid Work

## Stance

A course is worth its fee only if I apply each method to real work, paid or my own product, while the course runs. Exercises on made-up cases fade within months.

## Scope

Skills courses for my practice. Not reading for curiosity, which lives in [[exploratory-reading]].

## Why I Think This

- Accessibility: module 4 on screen readers landed three weeks before the Thistlewood audit. I still use that routine weekly ([[screen-reader-testing]]). The module on cognitive accessibility had no project attached, and I remember almost none of it.
- Research methods: the diary study and unmoderated test were run on Lintel Kit, so they produced V2 decisions and a case study, not only a grade ([[research-methods-course-2026]]).
- At Orchard & Vane I did three internal workshops on design ops. Nothing stuck; no project used them.

## What Would Change My Mind

A course where the made-up cases were as hard as real ones. I have not seen one.

## Implications

Pick courses by what work is coming in the next six months. Schedule the real application before enrolling. If no work fits, choose a product study on Lintel Kit.
