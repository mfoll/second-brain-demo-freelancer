# Lintel Support Log

A row per support email since launch: date, buyer ID, category, minutes spent, outcome.

## Key Details

- 2025-11-12 to 2026-09-30: 164 requests, about 92 hours. Launch week alone took about 6 hours.
- 2026 average: about 2.5 hours a week. Highest week: 5.5 hours after the 1.2 release.
- Categories: setup and Figma plan questions 31%, color and contrast 22%, coded tokens 18%, license and invoices 14%, bugs 9%, other 6%.
- Team buyers: three buyers with five or more seats account for 21% of 2026 time.
- Bugs confirmed: 7, all fixed in 1.0.1, 1.1 or 1.2 ([[lintel-kit-changelog]]).

## Why It Matters

The only measure of whether the kit stays within four hours a week ([[the-kit-must-not-eat-client-time]]). The color and contrast share is one reason [[lintel-kit-v2]] adds checked pairs.
