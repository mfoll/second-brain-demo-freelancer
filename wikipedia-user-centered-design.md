# User-centered design

## Summary

Article on user-centered design: a framework in which usability goals, user characteristics, environment, tasks and workflow get attention at every stage of design, with testing throughout.

## Why It Matters

I reread it in March 2025 after Crumbhollow's owner asked why research was in my quote at all. It reminded me how much of my agency process assumed a research budget that small clients do not have. The question it raised turned into the research course a year later.

## Caveats

Textbook level. No help with selling research to a three-shop bakery.
