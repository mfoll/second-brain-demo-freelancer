# First Meeting With Delphine Arceneaux 2025-02-18

First meeting with [[delphine-arceneaux]], recommended by [[yvonne-takahashi]], to set up the business's taxes properly after a few months of guessing.

- My 2024 partial year, which I had filed on my own; she found nothing to fix but two missed expense categories.

Tell her when the year changes shape. In September 2025 Copperline doubled my projected second half and I never sent her a line, which is the root of the $3,140 balance ([[tax-return-2025-summary]]).
