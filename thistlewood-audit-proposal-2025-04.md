# Thistlewood Audit Proposal 2025-04

My proposal to Thistlewood Libraries for the catalog accessibility audit, sent 2025-04-24 and accepted 2025-05-02.

## Key Details

- Fixed fee $9,200. Estimated 13 days, but quoted as a fixed fee because Odette Fairweather's budget line was fixed.
- Scope: nine templates, WCAG 2.2 AA, keyboard and two screen readers, zoom, automated checks, five short patron sessions, report, fix list, readout.
- Explicit exclusions: main website, e-book app, remediation work, retest.
- Payment: 50% on signature, 50% on delivery, net 15. The first proposal that used the terms drafted during the [[contract-terms-update]].

## Why It Matters

The exclusions list kept this engagement on budget: 12.5 days actual. The missing retest option is the gap noted in [[should-audits-include-a-retest]].
