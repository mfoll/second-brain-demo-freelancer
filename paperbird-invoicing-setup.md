# Paperbird Invoicing Setup

How I set up Paperbird, the invoicing and expense app I have used since March 2025, replacing a spreadsheet and word-processor invoices.

## Key Details

- Plan: the solo plan, $16 a month.
- Invoice numbering: `TB-YYYY-NNN`, restarting each year. 2025 started at TB-2025-004 because three invoices were issued from the old spreadsheet in January and February.
- Default terms: net 30 until 2025-06-27, net 15 since, with the deposit invoice set to "due on receipt".
- Automatic reminders: off. I send reminders myself, from [[invoice-reminder-templates]], because the automatic ones sound like a utility company.
- Late fee: not automatic. Added by hand if ever needed.
- Expense categories set up with [[delphine-arceneaux]] in March 2025 ([[deductible-expenses-log]]).
- Receipts: photographed into Paperbird the day they happen; matched monthly by [[paloma-ruiz]] since February 2026.
- Delegated access: Paloma (invoices and expenses, no bank connection), Delphine (read-only reports).

## Why It Matters

Paperbird is the system of record for what clients owe me and what I spent. The [[invoice-register]] is its export; the tax set-aside is triggered by its "paid" status ([[set-aside-taxes-from-each-payment]]).
