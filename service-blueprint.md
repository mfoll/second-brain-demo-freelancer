# Service Blueprint

A diagram of a service in lanes: what the customer does, what staff do in front of the customer, what happens backstage, and the systems that support it, all along one timeline. It shows where a customer problem is really a staff or system problem.

## Why I care

My work stops at the screen. The Crumbhollow pre-order flow did what it was designed to do, and three of the first five complaints were about the pickup shelf, printing and staff not seeing slot changes. A blueprint would have shown that the order flow ends at the shelf, not at the confirmation screen ([[crumbhollow-pickup-blueprint-sketch]]).

## What I would do differently

Draw a rough blueprint in the first week of any project that touches staff: bakery counters, box offices, member services. One hour, on paper, with someone who works the front line.
