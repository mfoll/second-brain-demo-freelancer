# Velora RFP 2026

My summary of Velora Mobility's request for proposal for the rider app redesign, received by email from [[rashid-kamau]] on 2026-04-20. The full RFP was under NDA and went back with the NDA closure on 2026-06-10.

## Key Details

- Scope: rider app on iOS and Android. Find a bike, unlock, end a ride, buy a pass. Research with riders required. Native development by Velora's own team.
- Timeline: start early June 2026, about 14 weeks, design handoff by mid-September.
- Indicated budget: $60,000 to $90,000.
- Submission deadline: 2026-05-14, 09:00; finalist presentations the same afternoon.
- Scoring: approach 30%, relevant experience 25%, price 25%, team and capacity 20%.
- Five bidders announced; three finalists.
- Clarifications through a shared log ([[velora-clarification-log]]).

## Why It Matters

The scoring sheet told me what would decide it, and I under-read it. Team and capacity at 20% was a fifth of the score for a one-person bid ([[capacity-signalling]], [[velora-feedback-call-2026-06-03]]). Nothing in the RFP required a single price; I chose that.
