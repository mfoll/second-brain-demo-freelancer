# Year One Business Review 2025

My own review of the first full independent year, written on 2026-01-04 before the tax work started.

## Numbers

- $112,400 gross: $106,200 client work, $6,200 Lintel Kit ([[revenue-summary-2025]]). Expenses about $15,300.
- Seven paying client engagements plus the overflow contract and two workshops.
- Longest unpaid invoice: 67 days (Crumbhollow). Average after new terms: 13 days ([[invoice-register]]).

## What worked

- The tax account: boring and right ([[set-aside-taxes-from-each-payment]]).
- The June contract rewrite ([[contract-terms-update]]).
- Saying yes to Copperline discovery at a fixed fee.

## What did not

- Day-rate quoting on Crumbhollow: 27 days for 18 days' pay.
- Telling nobody, including Delphine, when the second half of the year changed shape.
- Building the kit in the gaps; the launch slid two months.

## For 2026

Fewer, bigger engagements; a buffer of three months; tell Delphine in September; give the kit fixed hours instead of gaps.
