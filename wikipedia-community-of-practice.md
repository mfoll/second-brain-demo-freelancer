# Community of practice

## Summary

Article on communities of practice: groups who share a concern for something they do and learn to do it better by interacting regularly. It credits Jean Lave and Etienne Wenger (1991) and Wenger's later book, and describes the domain, community and practice that define one.

## Why It Matters

[[rosalind-ng|Rosalind]] sent it when we were planning the circle ([[solo-studio-circle-launch]]). The "interact regularly" part is why we fixed a monthly date before anything else, and why the [[solo-studio-circle-ground-rules]] are about how we talk, not what.

## Caveats

Mostly about organisations and education. Our circle is smaller and more about money than the article's examples.
