# Three-Account Money Setup

How money moves through the business. No account numbers here.

1. **Business account.** Every client payment and Gumroad payout lands here. Business expenses go out from here.
2. **Tax account.** 32% of every payment, moved the day it arrives ([[set-aside-taxes-from-each-payment]]). Only estimate payments and the annual balance leave it ([[tax-account-ledger]]).
3. **Buffer account.** Target: three months of my pay plus business costs, about $21,000. Topped up at the end of each month from whatever is left in the business account above $4,000. Reached the target in May 2026.

I pay myself a fixed $5,000 a month from the business account on the 1st, whatever came in that month.

Why: the Crumbhollow summer of 2025 showed that profit and cash are different things ([[crumbhollow-final-payment-2025-07-04]], [[wikipedia-cash-flow]]). The buffer is what let me treat the Velora loss in May 2026 as a disappointment rather than an emergency.

If I form a company ([[business-structure-review]]) the fixed pay becomes a salary and the structure stays.
