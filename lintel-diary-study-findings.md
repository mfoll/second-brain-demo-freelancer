# Lintel Diary Study Findings

## Summary

Findings from the ten-day Lintel Kit diary study (P1 to P6, 41 entries, exit interviews), synthesised on 2026-07-28 and written up on 2026-07-30.

## Key Findings

- Token mapping: 5 of 6 struggled to map Lintel's color tokens onto their existing styles in the first three days. Two gave up and kept their old styles for text.
- Dark mode workarounds: 4 of 6 duplicated the color variables by hand to make a dark theme. Only one had mentioned it in any support email.
- File organisation: 3 of 6 moved components into their own library structure and then lost updates.
- Positive: all 6 kept using the button and form components every day.

## Why It Matters

The dark mode finding is the strongest evidence for [[lintel-kit-v2]]'s dark mode, and the token mapping finding supports V2's contrast-checked color pairs. Shared with Jae-won on 2026-08-03, the day V2 started.

## Caveats

Six participants, self-selected, all engaged buyers. Says nothing about people who bought and stopped.
