# Lintel Kit

My Figma design-system starter kit: a token-driven library and coded design tokens, sold per seat on Gumroad. The umbrella for its versions and licensing.

## Outcome

Lintel Kit earns at least $1,500 a month on average with under four hours of support a week.

## Current State

- V1 launched 2025-11-12 at $79 per seat; current version 1.2 (2026-05-06) ([[lintel-kit-changelog]]).
- 212 sales and about $15,700 gross by 2026-09-30. 2026 so far averages about $1,050 a month: income is real, the target is not met ([[lintel-sales-dashboard]]).
- Support averages about 2.5 hours a week, inside the cap ([[lintel-support-log]]).
- [[lintel-kit-v2]] is Active for 2026-12-01: dark mode and contrast-checked color pairs, free for existing buyers.
- [[lintel-team-license]] is an Idea; the decision waits for V2 ([[should-lintel-kit-get-a-team-license]]).

## Scope and Boundaries

The product, its releases, pricing and licensing. Client design-system work stays with each client, and nothing from a client file goes into the kit: the kit's naming and color logic come from what I learned, not from Copperline's assets. Taxes on kit income go through [[run-the-business]].

## Working Context

I built the kit because I kept rebuilding the same starting point for clients: primitives, a semantic layer, a handful of components wired to tokens. Selling it is a hedge against the feast-or-famine months, not a business plan. The test for every kit decision is whether it costs client time ([[the-kit-must-not-eat-client-time]]).

What is in it:

- A Figma library with about 180 variables in three collections (primitives, semantic, component) and 42 components ([[lintel-kit-figma-library]]).
- Coded tokens in DTCG-style JSON and CSS custom properties, built and maintained by [[jae-won-seo]] for a 20% revenue share ([[lintel-kit-coded-tokens-repo]], [[jae-won-seo-revenue-share-agreement]]).
- A getting-started page and a license per seat ([[lintel-kit-license-terms]]).

Subprojects:

- [[lintel-kit-v1-launch]], Completed 2025-11-12.
- [[lintel-kit-v2]], Active, target 2026-12-01.
- [[lintel-team-license]], Idea since 2026-09-21.

Evidence about buyers comes from three places: the support log, the buyer diary study I ran for the Marrowgate course ([[lintel-buyer-diary-study-2026-07-13]]) and the unmoderated test of the getting-started page ([[lintel-docs-unmoderated-test-2026-08-24]]). The diary study is why V2 puts text guidance next to every color pair.
