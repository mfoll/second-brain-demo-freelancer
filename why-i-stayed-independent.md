# Why I Stayed Independent

Written on 2026-07-25, the day after declining the in-house offer, so I can reread it in a bad month.

## Why I left in 2024

Six years at Orchard & Vane, the last two mostly managing other designers' work. I wanted to design again and choose the problems.

## Why I said no in July 2026

- The work: I'd have spent most of my time on alignment for one product. Copperline, Ledgerleaf and the audits are three different problems in one year.
- The money is closer than it looks. The offer was $148,000 plus bonus; I'm on track to beat 2025's $112,400 revenue, and costs and taxes are mine either way. The difference is risk, not level.
- The things I'd miss in-house (a team to argue with) are mostly covered by the circle and Tidewell.
- Velora was one loss in a year with two wins through peers ([[where-2026-clients-came-from]]).

## What I'd miss about the job

Paid leave, a steady salary, and colleagues who know the product as well as I do. Real costs, not pretend ones.

See [[should-i-take-an-in-house-role]] for when I'd reconsider.
