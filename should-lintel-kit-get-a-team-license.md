# Should Lintel Kit Get A Team License?

Should I sell Lintel Kit to teams under one license, at a draft price of $249 for up to 10 seats, alongside the $79 per-seat license?

## Current Answer

> [!question]
> Still open as of 2026-10-23. Leaning yes, but I will decide within a month of V2 shipping (target 2026-12-01), so a team license sells the better product and I can see V2's support load first.

## Why it is open

Demand is real but small. Nine buyers asked for team terms between January and September 2026 ([[lintel-team-license-requests]]), against 212 seat sales ([[lintel-sales-dashboard]]). The loudest is [[calvin-moyo]], whose team of 12 already holds five seats and would rather buy one license with a purchase-order invoice ([[calvin-moyo-team-license-email-2026-09]]).

## Arguments for

- Most requesters are teams of 4 to 8 who share one seat today, which the license does not allow ([[lintel-kit-license-terms]]). A cheap legal option could convert sharing into revenue.
- Teams are steadier buyers: they come back for updates and recommend the kit inside larger companies.
- It moves the kit toward the $1,500 a month target, which it misses by about $450 a month in 2026.

## Arguments against

- **Support load.** Teams ask team questions: onboarding several designers, merging the kit into an existing library, invoices with purchase-order numbers. Three of the nine requesters already account for a fifth of 2026 support time ([[lintel-support-log]], [[how-much-support-can-the-kit-take]]).
- **Cannibalisation.** A team of 10 that would pay $790 in seats pays $249. Calvin's team would pay less than it already has.
- **The revenue share.** [[jae-won-seo]] gets 20% of net sales, defined per seat ([[jae-won-seo-revenue-share-agreement]]). A team sale needs a new rule, and I have not asked him yet.
- [[ellery-osei]]'s advice: one simple license, no custom terms.

## Options

1. **$249 for up to 10 seats**, one named admin, support through the admin only ([[lintel-team-license-draft-terms]]).
2. **Volume discount on seats** (for example 25% off from five seats), no new license text. Lower support risk, less appealing to Calvin.
3. **No team license**; keep pointing teams to seats. The current state.

## What would answer it

- V2 shipped and four weeks of its support load in the log.
- Jae-won's view on a fair share for a team sale.
- A quick check with the nine requesters: would they buy at $249, and would five-plus-seat discounts do instead?
