## Working Context

By July 2025 the home office had three problems: client calls with a neighbor's renovation in the background, no room to run an in-person test, and days where I spoke to nobody. The Copperline discovery was coming, with member interviews I wanted to run somewhere neutral.

I compared three options in [[workspace-cost-comparison]]: staying home ($0 extra, plus about $45 a month for the meeting rooms I would hire by the hour), a dedicated desk at Tidewell ($340), or sharing a small studio with [[tomas-echeverria|Tomás Echeverría]] ($520 each on a 12-month lease, plus furniture). The studio lost on the lease and on being alone again with one other person ([[should-i-rent-a-shared-studio-with-tomas]], now archived).

The [[tidewell-trial-day-2025-07-23|trial day]] on 2025-07-23 settled it. [[hana-petrakis|Hana Petrakis]] showed me the phone booths and the small room the circle already used. Signed on 2025-08-11, moved on 2025-09-01.

What it changed, a year on: two referrals (one from Quentin at Tidewell, one from Nadia, whom I see there), a talk at Design Night, and a better week. My stance is in [[a-desk-outside-home-pays-for-itself]].
