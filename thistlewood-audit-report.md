# Thistlewood Audit Report

The 31-page accessibility audit report for Thistlewood Libraries' online catalog, delivered at the 2025-07-09 readout.

## Summary

74 findings across search, record pages and patron account pages against WCAG 2.2 AA. Six critical: unlabeled search facets, the "place hold" control as an image link without text, a keyboard trap in the date picker for hold pickup, the 10-minute session timeout without warning, error messages on the login form not announced, and the cover image carousel that could not be paused.

## Key Details

- Structure: one-page summary for the board; method; findings by area; each finding with criterion, severity, who can fix it, steps to reproduce and a suggested fix.
- Patron sessions (P1 to P5) reported as short quotes and outcomes, no names.
- Positive findings listed too: headings structure on record pages was good, and the account page worked well at 400% zoom.

## Why It Matters

My first full audit report and the template for the Tallowmere one. The one-page board summary was Odette's request and is now standard.
