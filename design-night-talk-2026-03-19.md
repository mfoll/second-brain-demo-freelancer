# Design Night Talk 2026-03-19

My talk "Two teals: accessible color tokens without a brand fight" at Design Night at Tidewell, the quarterly evening meetup [[felipe-aranda]] organises. About 60 people, the biggest Design Night so far according to Felipe.

## What happened

- 25 minutes plus 15 minutes of questions. Second of two talks; the first was on motion in onboarding.
- The failing-button opener worked: I showed a "Pay now" button in a 3.0:1 teal and asked who would ship it. About half the hands went up, which made the point better than any slide ([[color-contrast-for-text]]).
- The live contrast-checker demo dragged. People at the back could not read it.
- Best question: how to name tokens when the brand team insists on hue names. Answer: keep hue names in the primitive layer, role names in the semantic layer, and never let components touch primitives.
- Second-best question, from a developer: whether 3:1 is enough for focus rings. Yes for the indicator against its background under non-text contrast ([[w3c-understanding-non-text-contrast-docs]]), and I pointed her to the focus-appearance criterion for more.

- 11 Lintel Kit sales in the next seven days, against a usual 2 or 3 ([[lintel-sales-dashboard]]).
