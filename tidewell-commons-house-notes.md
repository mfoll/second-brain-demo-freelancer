# Tidewell Commons House Notes

Practical notes on working at Tidewell Commons, for me and for anyone I invite.

- Desk: corner desk by the east window, pedestal locks. Monitor and dock are mine.
- Rooms: small room seats four, has a screen; book a week ahead for anything over two hours. Large room for Design Night only.
- Research sessions: participants sign in as my guests; two a day free. For more, ask [[hana-petrakis|Hana]].
- Calls: booths 1 and 2 are quieter than 3. Booth time is first come.
- Circle: small room, third Thursday, 12:00 to 13:30, held by Hana.
- Design Night: quarterly, Thursday evenings, [[felipe-aranda|Felipe Aranda]] organises.
- Kitchen: where most useful conversations happen, including [[quentin-ashby-tallowmere-referral-2026-08-20|the Tallowmere one]].
- Mail: business mail can come here; it is on my invoices since October 2025.
