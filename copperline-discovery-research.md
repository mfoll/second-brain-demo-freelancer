# Copperline Discovery Research

Phase 1 of the Copperline work: ten weeks to find out how members actually use online banking and how badly the current site performs, before anyone draws a screen.

## Outcome

Research report from 14 member interviews and a 12-participant baseline usability test (SUS 58) delivered and accepted. Reached on 2025-11-14 at the readout ([[copperline-discovery-readout-2025-11-14]]).

## Current State

- Completed. Fixed fee $18,500, paid in two invoices (deposit and on acceptance) ([[copperline-statement-of-work]]).
- The report ([[copperline-research-report]]) is the baseline every later test is compared with.

## Scope and Boundaries

Interviews and a baseline test on the existing site only. No design work, no app research. Participants were recruited by [[ana-lucia-ferreyra]] from member services and are referred to as P1 to P14 for interviews and P1 to P12 for the test. Recordings stay on Copperline's shared drive.

## Working Context

Plan in [[copperline-discovery-research-plan]], interview guide in [[copperline-interview-guide]]. Interviews ran over three weeks in late September and early October 2025, mostly by video, four in a branch meeting room. The baseline test ran in the second half of October with five tasks; the transfer between own accounts was the headline because members do it most and fail it most.

Main findings, in the order I presented them: transfers are buried under a "Move money" menu that 9 of 14 interviewees had never opened; members use the branch phone line as a workaround; bill pay payees are a long unsorted list; the brand teal on white text buttons is hard to read for older members. That last one became the two-teal argument in phase 2.

The [[copperline-journey-map]] came out of this phase and is still the shared picture for the team.
