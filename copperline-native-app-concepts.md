# Copperline Native App Concepts

A short concept sprint on what a redesigned Copperline mobile app could look like, run because the board wanted an app story alongside the web redesign. Superseded before it turned into a design phase.

## Outcome

Superseded on 2026-02-20 by the decision to design responsive web first and leave the vendor mobile app untouched until 2027 ([[copperline-responsive-first-decision-2026-02-20]]).

## Current State

- Superseded. The concept sprint itself was delivered and paid ($9,800, invoiced December 2025). Nothing after it was started.
- The concept deck is archived ([[copperline-native-app-concept-deck]]); the old question it answered is archived too ([[should-copperline-start-with-the-native-app]]).

## Scope and Boundaries

Four concept directions for the app's home and transfer screens, two days of workshop with the digital team, and a recommendation. No research with members beyond what discovery had already found.

## Working Context

The sprint ran from 2025-11-17 to mid-December 2025, then sat waiting for a board decision. In January 2026 Hollis Dunmore went through the vendor contract with me: the mobile app is a white-label product with a fixed navigation and only theming exposed until the contract renews in 2027. Any concept beyond colors and type would have been a request to the vendor, not something Copperline could build.

Discovery also showed that 62% of online banking sessions came from phone browsers, not the app ([[copperline-research-report]]). A responsive site would reach those members sooner. Renata took that to the board, and on 2026-02-20 they agreed on responsive web first.

What I would do differently: ask about the vendor contract on day one of any app work. I sold a sprint whose main output was a decision I could have prompted in a single call.
