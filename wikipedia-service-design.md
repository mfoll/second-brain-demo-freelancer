# Service design

## Summary

Article on service design: planning and arranging the people, infrastructure, communication and material parts of a service to improve its quality and the interaction between provider and user. It covers history, methods (journey maps, blueprints, prototyping services) and its relation to design thinking and management.

## Why It Matters

The first thing I read when the Crumbhollow pickup complaints made me wonder what I had missed. It gave me words for the part of the service I never designed: staff, shelves, printers. Started the [[service-design]] cluster with [[wikipedia-service-blueprint]].

## Caveats

Broad and academic in places. No guidance on how a solo designer sells this.
