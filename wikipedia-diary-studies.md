# Diary studies

## Summary

Article on diary studies: participants log entries about an activity over a period of time, giving longitudinal, in-context data at lower cost than field research. It notes the method is also called experience sampling, and discusses formats (paper, online, app) and trade-offs such as participant fatigue and less detail than observation.

## Why It Matters

Read while drafting the [[lintel-diary-study-plan]] for the Marrowgate assignment. The fatigue point matched what [[lorna-beckett|Lorna]] said about cutting prompts; my completion did fall mid-study ([[lintel-buyer-diary-study-2026-07-13]]). Practice note: [[diary-study]].

## Caveats

Mixes design research with clinical and psychology uses. The design-specific advice I used came from the course, not from here.
