# Tallowmere Intro Call 2026-08-27

- She wanted someone who tests with screen readers, not only a scanner.
- She asked for a day rate and an estimate in days, because the board compares suppliers that way.
- Under the June rule (audits and work under 10 days at the day rate, [[pricing-check-in-2026-06-18]]), this is a day-rate job. Quoted 8 days at $720 on 2026-08-31 ([[tallowmere-audit-quote-2026-09]]).
- She mentioned the theatre had been quoted "a five-figure redesign" by an agency last year and never went ahead. A clear small audit was the easier yes.
