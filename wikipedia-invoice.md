# Invoice

## Summary

The encyclopedia article on invoices: the commercial document a seller issues to a buyer, listing what was supplied, the agreed prices and the terms of sale, including when payment is due.

## Why It Matters

I read it when setting up Paperbird in March 2025, to check that my invoices carried the basics a client's finance team expects: a unique number, dates, itemised lines, payment terms and how to pay. Crumbhollow's bookkeeper had asked for an invoice number on a reissued copy, which is what sent me looking ([[paperbird-invoicing-setup]], [[send-and-chase-invoices]]).

## Key Details

- An invoice indicates that money is owed: it asks for payment rather than confirming it.
- Typical header fields: invoice number, invoice date and payment due date; the body lists goods or services with unit prices, subtotals, taxes and the total due.
- Payment terms are usually stated on the invoice; the article describes credit terms only in general language.
- Caveat: much of the article concerns trade in goods and regional practice; my invoices are for services and follow my own [[standard-services-agreement]].
