# Service Design

Exploration cluster on designing the whole service, including the staff side, not only the screens. It started with Crumbhollow: the pre-order flow worked, but the pickup shelf in the shops did not, and that was never in my scope.

## Where I am

In October 2025 I sketched the Crumbhollow pickup as a [[service-blueprint]] after the fact ([[crumbhollow-pickup-blueprint-sketch]]). Three of the five complaints Josie forwarded in the first month were backstage problems: orders printed late, no shelf labels, staff not knowing a pickup slot had changed. A screen redesign would not have fixed them.

Open: [[should-i-offer-service-blueprinting]] as a paid half-day workshop. Tallowmere's box office would be a natural fit, but the audit is fixed at 8 days.
