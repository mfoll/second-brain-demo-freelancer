# Understanding Success Criterion 3.3.7: Redundant Entry

W3C's Understanding document for WCAG 2.2 success criterion 3.3.7, Level A, new in 2.2.

## Summary

Within one multi-step process, information the user has already entered must be filled in automatically or available to select, rather than asked for again, unless re-entry is essential (for example, confirming a new password), needed for security, or the earlier information is no longer valid. Browser autocomplete does not count; the site must provide it. The criterion does not require storing data between sessions.

## Why It Matters

The old Copperline transfer flow asked for the "from" account again on the review step if a member went back to change the amount. The redesigned transfer keeps every choice through back and forward navigation, and scheduled transfers prefill from the last transfer between the same accounts.
