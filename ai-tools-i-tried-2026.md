# AI Tools I Tried 2026

Short log of AI features and assistants I tried on my own work in 2026. No client data in any of them.

- February: AI-generated placeholder copy in a design tool for Lintel Kit examples. Kept for placeholders; the copy was bland but harmless.
- April: an assistant to draft alt text for Lintel Kit documentation images. Half usable, half described the wrong thing. Kept as a first draft with review.
- June: AI image generation for the portfolio hero. Dropped; it looked like everyone else's and I could not say who made the source material.
- September: research synthesis on the Lintel diary study ([[ai-synthesis-trial-notes]]). Useful first sort, missed what mattered.
- September: meeting summaries of circle sessions. Stopped after one; the circle agreed nothing said in the room goes into third-party tools.

Pattern: fine where a wrong answer is cheap and I check everything; not where my judgment is what the client pays for. See [[ai-should-not-write-my-research-findings]].
