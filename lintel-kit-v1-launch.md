# Lintel Kit V1 Launch

Build the first sellable version of Lintel Kit and put it on Gumroad.

## Outcome

Figma library and token files launched on Gumroad on 2025-11-12 at $79 per seat. Reached.

## Current State

- Completed on 2025-11-12 ([[lintel-kit-v1-launch-day-2025-11-12]]).
- 61 sales by the end of November, 84 by the end of 2025, $6,200 gross for the year.
- First bug report on 2025-11-26 from [[mirela-dobos]], fixed in 1.0.1 two days later ([[first-token-bug-report-2025-11-26]]).
- Lessons kept in [[lintel-kit-v1-launch-retrospective]].

## Scope and Boundaries

The V1 library, coded tokens, getting-started page, Gumroad listing, artwork and launch email. Later versions are separate projects.

## Working Context

I worked on it in gaps between Thistlewood and Copperline: about 120 hours from June to November 2025, mostly in July and August. The plan was a September launch; Copperline discovery started on 2025-09-08 and took the autumn, so the launch slid to November.

Decisions:

- **Figma only.** Penpot was tempting but would have doubled the work ([[should-lintel-kit-support-penpot]]).
- **Three variable collections** (primitives, semantic, component) so that buyers re-brand by changing primitives only ([[figma-variables-docs]]).
- **Coded tokens with Jae-won.** I could not maintain the build myself. [[jae-won-seo]] built the JSON and CSS output for a 20% share of net sales instead of a fee ([[jae-won-seo-revenue-share-agreement]]).
- **$79 per seat, one license**, with a 20% launch discount for the first week. A tiered plan was drafted and dropped ([[lintel-tiered-pricing-idea-2025]]).
- **Artwork by [[ellery-osei]]** for a flat commission of $900 ([[lintel-launch-artwork-brief]]).

Rejected: a free "lite" version (support load), and selling on a marketplace with a bigger audience but a bigger cut.
