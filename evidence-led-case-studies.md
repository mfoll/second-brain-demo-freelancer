# Evidence-Led Case Studies

A portfolio case study built around what changed and how I know, with screens as supporting material. My answer to Velora's "screens, not outcomes".

## The shape I use

1. **Result first.** One sentence with the outcome and its number, in the first paragraph (Yvonne's rule, [[portfolio-review-with-yvonne-2026-09-10]]).
2. **The problem** in the client's words.
3. **What I did and why**, including what we chose not to do.
4. **What we found**: method, sample size, the two or three findings that drove decisions. Participants as counts or pseudonyms only.
5. **What changed**: shipped changes and measured results, with dates.
6. **What I'd do differently.**
7. Screens, small, approved by the client.

About 900 words. Every number and quote cleared in writing ([[testimonial-quotes-collection]]).

## Examples

- [[ledgerleaf-case-study-draft]]: trial-to-first-invoice 31% to 44%.
- [[thistlewood-case-study-draft]]: fix list and what the library shipped.
- [[copperline-case-study-draft]]: SUS 58 to 76, waiting for approval ([[will-copperline-approve-a-case-study]]).

## Limits

A case study is one case; it argues, it doesn't prove ([[wikipedia-case-study]]). Small samples (10 or 12 participants) are honest only if the case study says how small they are.
