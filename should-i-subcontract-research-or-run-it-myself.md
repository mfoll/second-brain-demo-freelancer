# Should I Subcontract Research Or Run It Myself?

## Question

When a project includes user research, when do I bring in a researcher like [[imani-clarke|Imani Clarke]] and when do I do it alone?

## Current Answer

Working rule since the Copperline prototype test in May 2026:

- Up to 6 moderated sessions on a project: alone.
- More than 6 sessions in a day or two, or two rooms at once: subcontract moderation for part of it.
- Participants with specific needs (screen reader users, older members) and a sponsor watching: have a second experienced person in the room.
- Synthesis stays with me, with the subcontractor reviewing.

On Copperline, Imani moderated 4 of 10 and we finished in one day; alone it would have been two days and a tired moderator by session 8.

## Caveats

The fee ($4,800 on Copperline) has to be in the client price from the start. The Research Methods Course made me more confident running studies, not less in need of a second pair of eyes.
