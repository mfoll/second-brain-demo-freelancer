# Should I Niche Down To Accessibility?

## Question

Should I position myself as an accessibility specialist rather than a product designer who is strong on accessibility?

## For

- Audits are the work I can sell fastest at a day rate (Thistlewood, Tallowmere).
- The two-teal talk got more attention than anything else I've done ([[design-night-talk-2026]]).
- Clear positioning is easier to refer: "send her your accessibility problems".

## Against

- My largest and best-paid work, Copperline and Ledgerleaf, is product design with accessibility inside it.
- Audit-only work caps at day-rate income.

## Current lean

Middle position in [[positioning-statement-2026]]: product designer, accessibility first. Revisit after the portfolio relaunch, based on which case study draws inquiries.
