# Desmond Okafor

Brand director at Copperline Credit Union. Owner of the 2019 brand guidelines ([[copperline-brand-guidelines-2019]]) and the person who disagrees with the two-teal palette.

## How we work together

- He cares about recognition: members should know it is Copperline from across the room. That is a fair goal and I try to argue from it, not against it.
- He prefers printed boards and side-by-side comparisons to Figma links. For the accessibility review I brought A3 prints of buttons in both teals, at arm's length and at reading distance.
- He signed off the foundations on 2026-04-30 but still calls the text teal #0F766E "the hospital color". He wants primary buttons back in brand teal #2BA59F at the next brand refresh.

## Current threads

- The brand teal remains on illustrations, large headings and the header band, which he chose himself. Keeping him owner of where the brand teal goes has helped.
- He will review the launch marketing; expect the button color to come up again ([[can-buttons-use-the-brand-teal]]).
