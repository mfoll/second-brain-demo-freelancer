# User Research Methods

Everything about how I learn from users: interviews, usability tests, questionnaires, diary studies, card sorts and synthesis. Lateral surface; the notes belong to the courses and engagements where I used them.

## Where I am

I was trained at Orchard & Vane to run moderated tests and interviews and to treat research as a phase someone else sells. Since 2026 I treat it as the evidence my case studies need. The [[velora-feedback-call-2026-06-03|Velora feedback]] pushed me into the [[research-methods-course-2026|Marrowgate course]], and now I can run a diary study and an unmoderated test on my own.

The best evidence I have is on Copperline: [[system-usability-scale|SUS]] 58 at baseline and 76 on the prototype ([[copperline-usability-test-2026-05-21]]). Small samples, but numbers. See [[small-samples-beat-no-samples]].
