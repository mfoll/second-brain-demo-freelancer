# Calvin Moyo Team License Email 2026-09

[[calvin-moyo]]'s email of 2026-09-08 asking for a team license, and my reply of 2026-09-22. Summarised.

## Key Details

- His team: 12 designers, five seats held. Seven designers use the kit "unofficially", which he flagged himself.
- His ask: one license for the whole team, an invoice carrying his company's purchase-order number, and one contact for updates.
- His budget: he did not name one; said that $948 for 12 seats "needs a second approval" in his company, while anything under about $500 he can approve himself.
- My reply: a decision after V2 ships; his five seats will count toward any team price; until then, please buy seats for the seven unofficial users or remove the kit from their files.
- His answer, same day: he will wait and remind the seven.

## Why It Matters

The clearest evidence that seat sharing is happening and that an approval threshold, not the price per seat, drives team purchases ([[should-lintel-kit-get-a-team-license]]).
