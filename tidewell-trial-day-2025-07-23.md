# Tidewell Trial Day 2025-07-23

Free trial day at Tidewell Commons, arranged by [[hana-petrakis|Hana Petrakis]].

## What I checked

- Calls: booked a phone booth for a Copperline scoping call at 10:00. Quiet enough; the booth gets warm after 40 minutes.
- In-person testing: the small meeting room seats four, has a screen, and can be booked in two-hour blocks. Good enough for interviews and a moderated test with one observer.
- Dedicated desk: a corner desk free from September, with a locking pedestal. $340 a month, monthly rolling.
- People: lunch at the long table. Met [[quentin-ashby|Quentin Ashby]], who makes motion work for arts organisations, and saw Rosalind's circle room in use.

## Result

Decided that afternoon. The comparison in [[workspace-cost-comparison]] already favored the desk on money; the trial answered the two practical doubts (calls and testing). Signed on 2025-08-11. The shared studio with Tomás was declined the same week ([[should-i-rent-a-shared-studio-with-tomas]]).
