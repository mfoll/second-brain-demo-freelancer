# Should I Get An Accessibility Certification?

## Question

Is a professional accessibility certification (an exam-based credential, not a course certificate) worth the fee and study time for the audit work I sell?

## Current Answer

Deferred until the 2027 learning budget is set. Arguments so far:

- For: public sector and nonprofit buyers sometimes ask for one. Thistlewood did not; one of the audit requests Rosalind showed at the June 2026 check-in did.
- Against: roughly 60 to 80 hours of study for knowledge I mostly use already. My audits sell on the report and references.
- The Sablewing course certificate ([[sablewing-certificate]]) is not a certification and I should not describe it as one.

## Revisit When

Two audit requests in a year ask for a certification, or audits become more than a third of revenue. Linked to [[what-should-i-learn-in-2027]].
