# Copperline Interview Guide

The guide for the 14 discovery interviews with Copperline members, version 3 after two pilot interviews.

## Summary

Five blocks over 45 to 60 minutes: banking habits; the last time they moved money; the last bill they paid; anything they avoid doing online; show-and-tell on their own device if they agreed (screen only, no account details read out).

## Key Details

- Opening question that worked best: "Tell me about the last time you needed money in a different account." It surfaced phone calls and branch visits without leading.
- Removed after pilots: a question about the mobile app ("do you prefer the app") that produced only opinions.
- Show-and-tell was offered in 10 video interviews; 7 accepted. That is where the "Move money" menu showed up as unknown.
- No account numbers, balances or names in notes; P numbers only.

## Why It Matters

Reusable as a base for any banking or payments interview. Version 3 also became an example in my [[research-methods-course-2026]] assignment.
