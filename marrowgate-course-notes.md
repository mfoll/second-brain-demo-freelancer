# Marrowgate Course Notes

What I want to keep from the Marrowgate research methods course, in my words. Course readings stay on Marrowgate's portal.

## Planning

- Write the decision the research should inform before writing a single question.
- One research question per study; three is a survey.
- Pilot everything. (I learned this the hard way: [[lintel-docs-unmoderated-test-results]].)

## Methods by week

- Interviews: semi-structured with a theme list ([[wikipedia-semi-structured-interview]]).
- Questionnaires: SUS and Likert items, report ranges ([[system-usability-scale]], [[wikipedia-likert-scale]]).
- Contextual inquiry: watch the work where it happens ([[wikipedia-contextual-inquiry]]).
- Card sorting: open first, closed to check ([[wikipedia-card-sorting]]).
- Unmoderated tests: the task text is the moderator ([[unmoderated-testing]]).
- Diary studies: two prompts a day, plan for fatigue ([[diary-study]]).
- Synthesis: thematic analysis, done in practice as [[affinity-mapping]] ([[wikipedia-thematic-analysis]]).

## Reporting

- Lead with the decision and the evidence, then method. Clients read the first page.
- Say how many people, every time: "4 of 6", not "most".
- Name what the study cannot tell you.

## For proposals

A research plan in a proposal needs: question, method, participants, timing, and what decision it feeds. One page. Goes into the [[proposal-template-rework|proposal template]].
