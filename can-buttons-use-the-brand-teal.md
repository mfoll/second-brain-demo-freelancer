# Can Buttons Use The Brand Teal

Can Copperline's primary buttons use the brand teal #2BA59F with white labels, as the 2019 brand guidelines and Desmond Okafor want?

## Current Answer

> [!info]
> No, not with white labels at normal size. White on #2BA59F is 3.0:1 and button labels need 4.5:1. Primary buttons use text teal #0F766E (5.5:1). Signed off on 2026-04-30.

## Options considered and why they lost

- Larger labels to qualify as large text: would need 24 px labels. Rejected by everyone.
- Dark labels on brand teal: passes, but Desmond said it "looks like a warning".
- Brand teal outline buttons: the outline passes the 3:1 non-text rule, but the teal label inside does not.

## Why it stays findable

Desmond still disagrees and has asked for primary buttons to be reviewed at the next brand refresh. If he raises it, start from [[copperline-palette-contrast-audit]] and [[copperline-accessibility-review-2026-02-11]], and offer a new brand teal dark enough for both jobs rather than re-arguing the old one.
