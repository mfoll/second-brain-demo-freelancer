# Change Order

A short written agreement to change scope, dates or fee after a contract is signed, signed by both sides before the extra work starts.

## My rule

Any request that adds more than half a day of work, or moves a delivery date, gets a change order the same week. It can be one page: what changes, why, the new date, the extra fee or "no fee" stated explicitly. A no-fee change order is still worth writing; it records that I said yes to something.

## Why

At Crumbhollow I absorbed 9 extra days without one, because I wanted to seem easy to work with ([[crumbhollow-overrun-retrospective]]). The bakery would have accepted a change order for the cake form; I never asked. The [[standard-services-agreement]] now has a change clause and refers to this format.

## Examples

- Copperline, 2026-06-24: bill pay handoff moved to 2026-11-27, payee search rework added, $7,500 ([[copperline-bill-pay-change-order-2026-06-24]]).
- Ledgerleaf, February 2026: one extra email in the onboarding sequence, no fee, recorded anyway.
