# Customer experience

## Summary

Article on customer experience (CX): a customer's cognitive, emotional, sensory and behavioral responses across all stages of interacting with a product or service, before, during and after purchase. It covers measurement, touchpoints and journey mapping. I searched for "customer journey" and was redirected here.

## Why It Matters

Read when Ledgerleaf's kickoff kept using "CX" for everything from support emails to billing. It helped me frame onboarding as one stage of a longer journey for [[wen-zhao|Wen]]'s team. Filed in [[service-design]] because the journey view is where the two overlap.

## Caveats

Marketing-heavy. Little I would use as method.
