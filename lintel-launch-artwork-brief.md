# Lintel Launch Artwork Brief

The brief and commission terms for [[ellery-osei]]'s launch artwork, agreed on 2025-09-29.

## Key Details

- Deliverables: a cover image and five section illustrations for the Gumroad page and the getting-started page.
- Direction: architectural, lintel-and-column shapes built from token swatches; flat color only from the kit's own primitives, so the art re-colors with the brand demo.
- Fee: flat $900, half on agreement and half on delivery (my own deposit terms, applied to me).
- License: use for product marketing and inside the kit's documentation; not for resale as standalone art. Ellery keeps the right to show it.
- Delivered on 2025-10-20, one round of changes.

## Why It Matters

The artwork is the one part of the kit made from primitives only, which made it the demo for re-branding in the launch email ([[lintel-launch-email-2025-11-12]]). V2 needs dark versions; I will ask Ellery for a quote in November.
