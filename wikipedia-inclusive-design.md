# Inclusive design

## Summary

Article on inclusive design: designing products, services and environments usable by as many people as possible, especially groups traditionally excluded. It positions accessibility as one outcome of inclusive design, with a focus on meeting many different needs rather than an average user.

## Why It Matters

Sablewing module 6. Thistlewood's patrons included many older readers and people using library computers with no assistive tech of their own, which made "compliant" feel too narrow. I used the framing in the audit's introduction.

## Caveats

Little practical method. Overlaps heavily with [[wikipedia-web-accessibility]].
