# Accessibility

Retrieval surface for accessibility as I practice it on client work: WCAG 2.2 AA as the baseline, audits with real assistive technology, and accessible color and components built into design systems rather than patched in afterwards.

## Where it shows up

- Audits: [[thistlewood-catalog-audit]] (2025, the first one I ran end to end) and [[tallowmere-ticketing-audit]] (2026). The method is in [[run-an-accessibility-audit]], severity levels in [[audit-severity-levels]].
- Design systems: the two-teal palette at Copperline ([[copperline-accessibility-review-2026-02-11]], [[copperline-palette-contrast-audit]]) and contrast-checked pairs in [[lintel-kit-v2]].
- Learning: [[accessibility-deep-dive-2025]] with Sablewing Academy gave me the vocabulary and the screen reader habits.
- Talk: [[design-night-talk-2026]] on accessible color tokens.

## Standards I cite most

- [[wikipedia-web-content-accessibility-guidelines]] for the overview.
- [[wcag-contrast-minimum-docs]] and [[wcag-non-text-contrast-docs]]: the two contrast thresholds behind [[color-contrast-for-text]].
- [[wcag-target-size-minimum-docs]]: seat maps and small icon buttons.
- [[wcag-accessible-authentication-minimum-docs]] and [[wcag-redundant-entry-docs]]: banking login and multi-step transfers.
- [[wcag-timing-adjustable-docs]]: ticket hold timers at Tallowmere.
- [[wai-aria-combobox-pattern-docs]]: the payee search rework.
