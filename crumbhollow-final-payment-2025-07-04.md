# Crumbhollow Final Payment 2025-07-04

Crumbhollow Bakery paid its final invoice of $5,850 on 2025-07-04, 67 days after I sent it and after three reminders. It is the reason my contracts now ask for a deposit.

## What happened

- The [[crumbhollow-preorder-flow]] engagement was quoted at 18 days at $650, $11,700 in total, invoiced in two halves: $5,850 at the midpoint in March (paid in 12 days) and $5,850 at handoff.
- 2025-04-25: handoff. 2025-04-28: final invoice sent, net 30, due 2025-05-28.
- Three reminders on 2025-06-02, 2025-06-13 and 2025-06-27 ([[crumbhollow-payment-reminders]]). After the second, [[martin-abara]] explained that the bakery was waiting for an insurance payout after an oven failed. After the third, [[josie-kalani]] called to apologise and gave a date.
- 2025-07-04: paid in full. No late fee, because my 2024 agreement had none ([[services-agreement-2024]]).

## Why it mattered

The engagement had already overrun: 27 days of work for 18 days of fee, about $433 a day. Then the second half of the money arrived two months late, while the Thistlewood audit was starting and its first payment was weeks away. For about five weeks I paid rent and the desk deposit from personal savings. Nothing about the bakery was dishonest; they were a small business with a cash problem of their own, and my contract let them make it mine.

## What changed

- [[contract-terms-update]], started 2025-06-02, finished 2025-06-27: 50% deposit, net 15, 1.5% monthly late fee, IP transfer on final payment ([[standard-services-agreement]]).
- Reminders ask for a payment date from the first one ([[invoice-reminder-templates]], [[send-and-chase-invoices]]).
- The overrun side went to the pricing work ([[pricing-decision-2025-11-20]]).

## Relationship afterwards

Fine. Josie sent a box of pastries to the circle session in September and recommended me to a café owner who did not have a budget. I would work with them again, on the new terms.
