# In-House Offer Declined 2026-07-24

I called [[greta-solheim]] and declined the principal product designer offer at a payments company: $148,000 base plus bonus, hybrid three days a week ([[in-house-offer-summary-2026-07]]).

## Why it was hard

It came seven weeks after losing Velora, when the second half of 2026 was not yet booked. The salary was about what I expected to net from the business in 2026 before tax, with paid leave and no pipeline to feed.

## How I decided

- Spent a weekend on the numbers: 2026 revenue on track to beat 2025's $112,400, mostly Copperline and Ledgerleaf, with the kit growing.
- Asked myself Yvonne's question: what would I miss in each case? In-house: the variety and choosing my clients. Solo: a team to argue with and a steady salary.
- The circle covers most of the "team to argue with" ([[solo-studio-circle-launch]]).

Answer recorded as an Answered question in [[should-i-take-an-in-house-role]], reasons in [[why-i-stayed-independent]]. Greta offered to send freelance audits my way; logged in the [[client-pipeline-tracker]].
