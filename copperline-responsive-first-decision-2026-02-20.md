# Copperline Responsive First Decision 2026-02-20

Copperline's board approved Renata Solberg's recommendation to redesign online banking as a responsive website first and leave the vendor mobile app untouched until its contract renews in 2027.

## Why

- The vendor app is white-label with fixed navigation; only colors and type can change before 2027 (Hollis Dunmore's review of the contract, January 2026).
- 62% of online banking sessions already came from phone browsers ([[copperline-research-report]]).
- A responsive site reaches all members with one build, and phone-width layouts already cover most of what reflow at 400% zoom requires ([[wcag-reflow-docs]]).

## Consequences

- [[copperline-native-app-concepts]] marked Superseded; the concept deck archived ([[copperline-native-app-concept-deck]]).
- The old question [[should-copperline-start-with-the-native-app]] closed as Superseded.
- Transfers and bill pay designed mobile-width first. The app gets the new colors only.
- I lost a possible app phase in 2026. That is fine; it would have been a design nobody could build.
