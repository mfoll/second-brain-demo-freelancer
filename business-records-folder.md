# Business Records Folder

Pointer to where the business's paperwork lives. The vault holds summaries; the documents stay here.

## Source Location

My business drive, folder "Business", backed up nightly. Structure:

- Contracts / Templates (current agreement, the archived 2024 version, Nathaniel's review) and Contracts / Clients / <client> (signed agreements, schedules, change orders).
- Taxes / <year> (revenue summary, expense export, estimate worksheet, return).
- Taxes / Structure (Delphine's memo and notes for the [[business-structure-review]]).
- Insurance (policy and certificates).
- Advisers (engagement letters).
- Lintel / Exports (monthly Gumroad CSVs), Lintel / Agreements (revenue share, artwork license).

## Rules

- Signed contracts, returns and policies only as PDFs here; never paste their full text into the vault.
- No account numbers, tax identification numbers or logins in file names or notes.
- Client deliverables do not live here; they stay in each client's workspace.
- Checked last on 2026-10-05 during the monthly review.
