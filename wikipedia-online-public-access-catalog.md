# Online public access catalog

## Summary

Encyclopedia article on the online public access catalog (OPAC), now usually just called the library catalog: an online database of what a library or group of libraries holds, which has largely replaced card catalogs.

## Why It Matters

Read before the Thistlewood proposal to understand that the catalog is a vendor product with a long history, not something the library built. That framed the audit's "who can fix it" tag and kept the fix list realistic ([[thistlewood-fix-list]]).

## Caveats

No accessibility content. Vendor specifics came from Samir Haddadin, not from this article.
