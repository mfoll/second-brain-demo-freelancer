# Marrowgate Final Report

## Summary

My written report for the Marrowgate course, submitted 2026-09-21: "What Lintel Kit buyers struggle with, over ten days and in ten minutes." 14 pages, two studies, one set of recommendations.

## Key Details

- Diary study: 6 participants, 41 entries, three main findings (token mapping, dark mode workarounds, file organisation). See [[lintel-diary-study-findings]].
- Unmoderated test: 8 participants, three tasks; install found by 6 of 8, color token changed by 7 of 8, text color guidance found by 4 of 8 after the task rewording. See [[lintel-docs-unmoderated-test-results]].
- Recommendations: dark mode and contrast pairs in V2, rename "surface-raised", a single "colors for text" section in the docs.
- Method reflection, including the skipped pilot.

## Why It Matters

The first research case study I own outright and can publish. It goes into the [[portfolio-site-rebuild]] as the research evidence the Velora feedback said was missing, while the Copperline case study waits for approval.
