# Company Structure Memo 2026-07

[[delphine-arceneaux]]'s two-page memo of 2026-07-14 recommending that I form a company and pay myself a salary. Summarised in general terms; it applies her reading of rules I do not record in the vault.

## Key Details

- Recommendation: form a company and elect to pay myself a reasonable salary, taking the rest of the profit as distributions.
- Estimated saving: $4,000 to $6,000 a year once profit stays above about $90,000. Below that the saving roughly matches the extra costs.
- Extra costs she lists: formation and registration about $800 once; payroll service about $45 a month; a second return, about $1,000 a year on her fees. About $2,300 a year after the first.
- Timing: she recommends starting on 2026-10-01 so that a quarter of 2026 benefits.
- Side effects: contracts, the Gumroad account, the insurance policy and the bank accounts all move to the company.

## Why It Matters

This is the case I paused on 2026-08-03 ([[business-structure-review-paused-2026-08-03]]). Her saving depends on profit, and in July I could not say whether 2026 profit would clear $90,000 after the Velora loss. The September check now forecasts about $121,000 gross ([[quarterly-estimate-worksheet-2026]]), which strengthens her case for January.

## Caveats

Her numbers are estimates for my situation, not general advice. The non-money costs (a Copperline contract novation mid-project, moving the kit's sales account) are not in her figures.
