# Solo Studio Circle Session 2025-09-18

Circle session on subcontracting and overlap between members. My third week at Tidewell; [[nadia-ferro|Nadia Ferro]]'s first session as the seventh member.

## What happened

- [[imani-clarke|Imani]] hosted. Topic she proposed: how we hire each other without damaging friendships.
- Agreed defaults, written up afterwards as [[subcontracting-with-peers]]: the member who holds the client contract pays the subcontractor within 15 days of their invoice whatever the client does; the subcontractor never pitches the client directly for 12 months; who presents findings is agreed before kickoff.
- Nadia and I found we overlap most (product design for small software companies). We agreed to tell each other when we bid for the same client. It was Nadia who passed me Ledgerleaf seven weeks later ([[nadia-ferro-ledgerleaf-referral-2025-11-06]]).
- No referral fees. [[tomas-echeverria|Tomás]] argued for 10%; everyone else thought it would make referrals transactional. Recorded in [[solo-studio-circle-ground-rules]].

## For me

Copperline discovery had just started. I asked Imani whether she would be available in spring 2026 for the testing phase if phase 2 happened. She said yes in principle; it became the 2026-05-21 test.
