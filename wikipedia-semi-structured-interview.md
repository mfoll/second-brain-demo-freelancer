# Semi-structured interview

## Summary

Article on the semi-structured interview: an interviewer works from a framework of themes rather than a fixed list of questions, leaving room for new ideas from the interviewee.

## Why It Matters

Marrowgate week 2 compared interview formats. My exit interviews in the [[lintel-buyer-diary-study-2026-07-13|Lintel diary study]] used this format: three themes, any order, follow the participant. Notes in [[marrowgate-course-notes]].

## Caveats

Generic social science framing; nothing on product research.
