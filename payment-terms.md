# Payment Terms

The rules in a contract that say when money is due: deposits, invoice timing, how many days the client has (net 15, net 30), and what happens when they are late.

## The parts, and what each does

- **Deposit:** money before work. Commits the client and limits what is at risk ([[wikipedia-down-payment]]).
- **Milestones:** when invoices go out. More, smaller invoices keep cash flowing and surface problems early.
- **Net days:** how long the client has after an invoice. Shorter terms suit small clients; large ones run fixed cycles and need longer.
- **Late fee:** a stated charge for overdue balances. Its job is to make the due date real, not to earn money ([[wikipedia-late-fee]]).
- **IP on payment:** ownership moves when the client pays in full. Quiet but effective leverage.

## Trades

Terms are a package. Copperline swapped my deposit and net 15 for net 30 with six fixed monthly invoices ([[copperline-statement-of-work]]); that is a fair trade because the cash arrives regularly. A trade that only lengthens the wait is not.
