# Velora Lessons

What I learned from losing the Velora Mobility pitch on 2026-05-29, and where each lesson went. Written on 2026-06-05, updated 2026-10-20.

## At a Glance

> [!abstract]
> Velora chose Orchard & Vane's $38,000 discovery phase over my single $74,000 price. Three reasons: no smaller first step, case studies without research outcomes, and doubt about solo capacity. Fixes: three options with a paid first step, case studies with evidence, and named partners.

## The three lessons

### 1. Always give a smaller first step

I sent one price for the whole job, against my own rule ([[pricing-decision-2025-11-20]]), and even after the clarification log told me phasing was welcome ([[velora-clarification-log]]). A buyer with a budget line for discovery can say yes to discovery today; a buyer facing $74,000 has to win an internal argument first.

Where it went: the [[proposal-template-rework]] makes option A a paid first step in every project proposal ([[paid-discovery-phase]], [[three-option-proposals]]). It also fed the broader [[pricing-check-in-2026-06-18]].

### 2. Show what changed, not what it looked like

Velora's panel read my case studies and found screens, not outcomes. I had the numbers (Copperline's SUS 58 baseline and later 76) and could not use them without approval ([[will-copperline-approve-a-case-study]]).

Where it went: the [[portfolio-site-rebuild]] with three [[evidence-led-case-studies]], and the outcomes page in [[proposal-template-2026]]. Also ask for permission to publish numbers at kickoff, not at the end.

### 3. Make capacity concrete

"I'll bring in help if needed" scored badly. Named people with roles, rates and written availability would have scored better.

Where it went: [[partner-availability-agreements-2026]] with [[imani-clarke]] and [[bastian-kohl]], the "Who works on this" page, and [[capacity-signalling]]. On the research side, the [[research-methods-course-2026]] gave me methods I can now name and price (unmoderated tests and diary studies), so a research plan in a proposal is more than interviews.

## Smaller lessons

- Read the scoring sheet as the brief ([[velora-rfp-2026]]). Team and capacity was 20% of the score.
- A clickable concept in a bid wins praise, not contracts. Two and a half unbillable days ([[velora-pitch-deck-and-prototype]]).
- Ask for feedback every time. Annika's 30 minutes were worth more than any course.

## What I'm not concluding

- Not "agencies always win". The concept was rated best of the three finalists.
- Not "never answer RFPs". That is still open ([[should-i-answer-cold-rfps]]).
- Not "solo can't do big work". Copperline is bigger than Velora would have been, over a longer time. It came through a person who already trusted me.

## Status

All three lessons have owners. Lesson 1 and 3 are done (template finished 2026-07-17). Lesson 2 finishes with the relaunch, target 2026-11-30.
