# Peer learning

## Summary

Article on peer learning: learners interacting with other learners to reach educational goals, moving from independent to interdependent learning. It compares it with cooperative learning and notes broader views outside formal education.

## Why It Matters

Read before the ground rules session of the circle. The idea that peers learn by explaining to each other is behind the rotating host who brings one piece of work ([[run-a-circle-session]]).

## Caveats

School and university focus. Read with [[wikipedia-community-of-practice]].
