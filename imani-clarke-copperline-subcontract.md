# Imani Clarke Copperline Subcontract

Summary of my subcontract with Imani Clarke, freelance UX researcher from the Solo Studio Circle, for the Copperline prototype test.

## Key Details

- Signed 2026-04-27. Fixed fee $4,800 for: script review, pilot session on 2026-05-19, moderating 4 sessions on 2026-05-21, co-analysis and a findings workshop with me on 2026-05-26.
- Paid within 15 days of her invoice, from the phase 2 milestone. I pay subcontractors on my own terms whether or not the client has paid me.
- Confidentiality terms mirror Copperline's; she signed Copperline's own confidentiality form too. Copperline was notified as the statement of work requires ([[copperline-statement-of-work]]).
- Imani keeps no recordings or notes after delivery.

## Why It Matters

First time I subcontracted research. It worked: two moderators let us run 10 sessions in one day, and her calibration on scoring improved my own. It is also the proof behind naming her as a research partner in proposals after Velora ([[proposal-template-rework]]).
