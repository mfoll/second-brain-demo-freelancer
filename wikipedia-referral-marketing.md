# Referral marketing

## Summary

Referral marketing is a structured version of word of mouth: a company actively encourages, and often rewards, existing customers for introducing new ones, so that referrals can be tracked and measured rather than left to chance.

## Why It Matters

My pipeline runs on referrals ([[client-pipeline-tracker]], [[referrals-beat-cold-outreach]]) but nothing about it is structured. Read in October 2026 while asking whether I should formalize it. Decision for now: no referral fees (Owen and I agreed that in 2025, see [[owen-thibodeaux]]), but a thank-you note and a quarterly update to past clients and peers ([[past-client-update-email-2026-q3]]).

## Key Details

- Tracking the source is half the value; my tracker already records the first referrer.
- Incentives can change who refers and why. In a small peer circle, money would feel odd.

## Caveats

Written for consumer companies with many customers. A practice with 25 leads in two years is word of mouth, not a program.
