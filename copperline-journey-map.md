# Copperline Journey Map

A one-page journey map of "I need to move money" from discovery, drawn in FigJam and printed at A1 for the readout. Still pinned in Copperline's digital team room.

## Summary

Six stages from trigger (a bill due, a savings goal) to confirmation, with three lanes: what the member does online, what they do offline (phone, branch, asking a family member), and how they feel. The low point is the "Move money" menu, where 6 of 14 interviewees switched to the phone.

## Why It Matters

It is the shared picture the team refers to when deciding priorities; Priyanka Venkat uses it to explain the project to new staff. It also showed the branch phone line as part of the service, which pushed me towards [[service-design]] reading.

## Caveats

Built from 14 interviews; the offline lane is self-reported.
