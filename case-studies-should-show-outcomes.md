# Case Studies Should Show Outcomes

## Stance

A case study that shows only screens is a gallery. Buyers of product design want to know what changed, how it was measured and what I learned, and they read the first paragraph and the numbers.

## Why I Think This

- Velora's panel said so directly ([[velora-feedback-call-2026-06-03]]).
- My old site got about 310 visits a month and almost no relevant inquiries ([[portfolio-site-analytics-2026]]).
- Yvonne: "the part a buyer believes is what you'd do differently" ([[portfolio-review-with-yvonne-2026-09-10]]).

## What Would Change My Mind

Inquiries after the relaunch that mention specific screens, not results. Or visual-design buyers, for whom screens are the outcome; I don't sell to them.

## Implications

- The [[portfolio-site-rebuild]] uses three [[evidence-led-case-studies]].
- I need client permission for numbers, so ask for it at kickoff, not at the end. Added to the [[standard-services-agreement]] discussion for 2027.
