# Velora Pitch Presentation 2026-05-14

Written proposal submitted at 09:00 and a 45-minute video presentation at 14:00 to Velora's panel of four: [[annika-brandvold]] (product), [[rashid-kamau]] (procurement), their engineering lead and a customer operations manager. I was the third of three finalists.

## How it went

- 20 minutes of presentation, 25 of questions. I ran the clickable unlock-flow concept live in Figma's present mode ([[velora-pitch-deck-and-prototype]], [[figma-play-prototypes-docs]]). The engineering lead asked to see the error state for a bike that fails to unlock; I had it, which felt good.
- Annika's questions were all about research: how many riders, how I would recruit them at docking stations, whether I'd test in the street. Good questions, decent answers.
- Rashid asked what happens if I'm ill in week six. I said I would "bring in help if needed". I could see it land badly.
- Nobody asked about the price. In hindsight that was a signal.

## My notes written the same evening

- Strongest part: the concept and the research plan.
- Weakest: capacity, and a single $74,000 fee with no smaller entry point ([[velora-proposal-2026-05-14]]).
- Felt like a 50/50. It was not; see [[velora-decision-email-2026-05-29]] and [[velora-feedback-call-2026-06-03]].
