# Pricing Model 2025

The four-page pricing model I wrote during the [[pricing-model-review]] and finalised on the night of 2025-11-20. Superseded in part by [[pricing-rules-2026]]; kept as written.

## Summary

All work priced with three value-based options; no day rate in any proposal; a private floor of $650 a day; change requests priced as new options.

## Key Details

- Value questions for the discovery call: what does the problem cost now, what is a good result worth, who decides, what happens if nothing changes.
- Option rules: A is a useful first step, B is recommended, C adds one valuable extra. Different scope in each, not discounts.
- Floor check: each option's price divided by estimated days times 1.3 must stay above $650 ([[pricing-floor-calculation]], [[effective-day-rate]]).
- Change rule: anything outside the chosen option is quoted as a change order before work starts ([[change-order]]).
- Deposit and payment terms come from the [[standard-services-agreement]], not from the pricing model.

## Caveats

Section 3, "No exceptions", is the one that didn't survive contact with audit buyers ([[pricing-check-in-2026-06-18]]).
