# Solo Studio Circle Anniversary 2026-04-16

One-year review of the Solo Studio Circle, run by [[rosalind-ng|Rosalind]] and me. All seven members.

## What we counted

- 13 sessions since 2025-03-20, average attendance 5.8 of 6 then 7.
- Work that moved between members: two referrals to clients (one of them my Ledgerleaf, via Nadia), one subcontract agreed (Imani on Copperline testing, then a month away), four "can you look at this" reviews of proposals.
- Topics that came back most: pricing (four sessions), capacity and saying no (three), late payment (two, both after my Crumbhollow story).

## What we changed

- Kept the format: monthly, lunchtime, in person.
- Added a rotating host who brings one question and one piece of work ([[run-a-circle-session]]).
- Cap at seven members for now. Two people have asked to join since; the question is open ([[should-the-circle-take-new-members]]).
- Started a shared notes doc so absent members can catch up ([[solo-studio-circle-shared-notes]]).

## For me

Hearing the year counted made it clear the circle is my main source of work, not only of advice. That fed the referral view in the [[client-pipeline-tracker]].
