# Professional Liability Policy

Summary of my professional liability policy, placed by [[sunita-rao]] on 2025-03-01 and renewed on 2026-03-01.

## Key Details

- Cover: claims that my design or research work was negligent or caused a client financial loss, including defence costs.
- Limit: $1,000,000 per claim and in the aggregate. Deductible $1,000.
- Premium: $1,180 a year in 2025, $1,240 at the 2026 renewal (revenue band went up).
- Covered activities as declared: product and UX design, usability research, accessibility audits, workshops, sale of design templates.
- Not covered: cyber incidents and data breaches, bodily injury, and anything outside the declared activities.
- Certificates of insurance issued for Thistlewood (May 2025) and Copperline (September 2025).

## Why It Matters

The [[standard-services-agreement]] requires me to hold it, and two clients asked for proof before signing. Declaring Lintel Kit sales as an activity was Sunita's suggestion at the 2026 renewal. The cyber gap is an open question ([[do-i-need-cyber-insurance]]).
