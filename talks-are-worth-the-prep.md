# Talks Are Worth The Prep

## Stance

One local talk a year is worth about four days of preparation, but more for Lintel Kit and reputation than for client work.

## Why I Think This

- The March 2026 talk took about 30 hours including rehearsals ([[design-night-talk-2026]]).
- It sold 11 kits in a week and led to a podcast ([[design-careers-podcast-recording-2026-08-12]]).
- Client leads from it: one, dropped.

## What Would Change My Mind

A client engagement traceable to a talk would raise this to Medium. Two talks with no kit bump would lower it.

## Implications

One talk in 2027 at most, chosen from [[talk-ideas-backlog]] for overlap with paid work.
