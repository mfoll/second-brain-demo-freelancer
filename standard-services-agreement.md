# Standard Services Agreement

My contract template for client work since July 2025, revised in the [[contract-terms-update]] and reviewed by [[nathaniel-brook]] in June 2025. This note summarises the terms; the full text is not copied here.

## Summary

A short services agreement (seven pages) plus a one-page project schedule per engagement. The schedule carries scope, fee, milestones and dates; the agreement carries everything that should not be renegotiated each time. The four terms that matter most: a 50% deposit before work starts, net-15 invoices, a 1.5% monthly late fee, and IP transfer on final payment.

## Key Details

- **Deposit:** 50% of the fixed fee, invoiced on signature, due before the first working day. Work does not start until it clears. For day-rate engagements, 50% of the estimated days.
- **Invoices:** remaining fee invoiced per milestone in the schedule, or on handoff for short engagements. Payment due 15 days from the invoice date.
- **Late fee:** 1.5% a month on any balance more than 15 days past due, counted from the due date. I may pause work on overdue balances after 30 days, with written notice.
- **IP:** the client receives full rights in the final deliverables when the final invoice is paid. Until then they have a license to use drafts for the project. My pre-existing tools, templates and Lintel Kit stay mine; the client gets a license to anything of mine built into the deliverables.
- **Portfolio:** I may show the work only with the client's written approval, and never with confidential data. Copperline has not approved a case study yet ([[will-copperline-approve-a-case-study]]).
- **Confidentiality:** both ways, three years after the end of the engagement. Research recordings and participant data stay in the client's systems.
- **Changes:** scope changes by a written change order with its own fee ([[change-order]]).
- **Termination:** either party with 14 days' written notice; the client pays for work done to the termination date.
- **Liability:** capped at the fees paid under the schedule. Requires me to hold professional liability insurance ([[professional-liability-policy]]).

## Exceptions so far

- Copperline phase 2: no deposit and net 30, in exchange for six monthly milestone invoices of about $10,333 from January to June 2026, agreed in December 2025 ([[copperline-statement-of-work]]). Copperline's procurement also asked for their own data-protection addendum, which Nathaniel checked.
- Tallowmere (day rate, 8 days): deposit of 50% of the estimate, balance on handoff.

## Why It Matters

This is the document behind "why do new contracts ask for a 50% deposit?". The answer is Crumbhollow ([[crumbhollow-final-payment-2025-07-04]]): the deposit would have halved the money at risk, and the late fee would have given the reminders teeth. It replaced the [[services-agreement-2024]].

## Caveats

No late fee has been charged yet, so the clause is untested. It is a template for my business as it is now; if I form a company ([[business-structure-review]]) the party name and signature block change.
