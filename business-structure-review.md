## Working Context

The savings depend on profit, and 2026 profit was hard to call in July: Velora was lost in May, June had no new client starts, Tallowmere had not appeared yet, and Copperline phase 2 ends in November.

Costs of forming a company, as Delphine listed them: a one-off formation and registration of about $800, a payroll service at about $45 a month, and her own fee for a second return of about $1,000 a year. Non-money costs: every client contract and the Gumroad account would move to the company, and Copperline would need a contract novation in the middle of phase 2.

Options considered:

1. Form now, start the salary on 2026-10-01. Delphine's preference.
2. Form on 2027-01-01, after Copperline phase 2 closes. My preference if the numbers hold.
3. Stay as I am until profit is above $100,000 two years running.
