# AI Should Not Write My Research Findings

## Stance

An AI tool can sort raw research notes into a first rough grouping. The findings, their wording and what they mean for the design have to be mine, checked against the sessions.

## Scope

Research synthesis on my own and client projects. Not copy drafts, alt text suggestions or meeting summaries, where the stakes are lower.

## Why I Think This

- In my one real trial the tool merged two themes that mattered separately (token mapping and dark mode workarounds) and missed a workaround mentioned by only one participant, which turned out to be shared by four ([[ai-synthesis-trial-notes]]).
- Clients pay for my judgment. If a finding is wrong, it is my name on it.
- Client data cannot go into a third-party tool without written consent. Copperline's rules alone make this moot for most of my work.

## What Would Change My Mind

A tool that keeps every cluster traceable to the exact entries, run on a study where I compare it blind with Imani's synthesis and it does as well.

## Implications

Use AI only on my own pseudonymised data, as a first pass, and say so in any report. Never on client research without written approval.
