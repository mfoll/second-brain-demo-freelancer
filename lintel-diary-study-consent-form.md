# Lintel Diary Study Consent Form

## Summary

The one-page consent form for the Lintel diary study, adapted from the Marrowgate course template.

## Key Details

- What is collected: diary entries, optional screenshots, an exit interview recording.
- Use: improving Lintel Kit, a course report, and anonymised quotes in a public case study.
- Participants are identified only as P1 to P6 in anything shared.
- Recordings deleted 90 days after the study; entries kept until V2 ships.
- Added on Lorna's advice: permission to process entries with software tools for analysis. This is what allowed the AI comparison ([[ai-synthesis-trial-notes]]).
