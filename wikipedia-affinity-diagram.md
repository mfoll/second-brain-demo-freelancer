# Affinity diagram

## Summary

Article on the affinity diagram, a tool for organising ideas and data into groups by natural relationship. It attributes the term to Jiro Kawakita in the 1960s (also called the KJ method) and lists it among the seven management and planning tools.

## Why It Matters

Read the evening before synthesising the Lintel diary study. I had always called it "affinity mapping" and treated it as a designer's habit; it was useful to know it has a management-tools history. My practice note is [[affinity-mapping]], and the map from this study was the baseline for the [[ai-synthesis-trial-notes|AI comparison]].

## Caveats

Very short. Nothing on research synthesis specifically.
