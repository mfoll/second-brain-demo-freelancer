# AI In Design Tools

Exploration cluster on generative AI in my own design and research work. I do not sell AI features and no client has asked me to design one yet.

## Where I am

I tried an AI assistant on one real task: clustering the 41 entries from the Lintel diary study and comparing the result with my own affinity map ([[ai-synthesis-trial-notes]]). It saved about 40 minutes on a first pass and merged two themes that were different. My working stance is [[ai-should-not-write-my-research-findings]]: fine for a first sort, not for what goes in a report. Whether that holds with better tools is [[can-ai-tools-do-research-synthesis-for-me]].

Client data is the hard limit. Copperline's interview notes never leave Copperline's workspace, so any AI use on client research needs the client's written yes first.
