# Send And Chase Invoices

From signed contract to paid invoice, under the [[standard-services-agreement]].

## Steps

1. **On signature:** create the deposit invoice in Paperbird (50% of the fee, or of the estimated days), due on receipt. Do not book the kickoff as confirmed until it is paid.
2. **At each milestone in the schedule:** [[paloma-ruiz]] drafts the invoice from my milestone list; I check scope and amount and send it from Paperbird with net 15 (or the negotiated terms, such as Copperline's net 30).
3. **On payment:** mark paid, then set aside taxes the same day ([[set-aside-taxes-from-each-payment]]).
4. **Day after due date:** first reminder ([[invoice-reminder-templates]]).
5. **Seven days overdue:** second reminder, naming the late fee.
6. **Fourteen days overdue:** call, then confirm the agreed date by email.
7. **Thirty days overdue:** written notice that work pauses; decide whether to charge the 1.5% monthly fee. Never charged so far.
8. **Final invoice paid:** send the IP transfer confirmation with the final files.

## Checks

- First Monday of each month: refresh the [[invoice-register]]; anything unpaid past due gets a reminder that day.
- Every invoice carries number, dates, itemised milestones, terms and how to pay ([[wikipedia-invoice]]).
