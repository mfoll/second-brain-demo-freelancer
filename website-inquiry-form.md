# Website Inquiry Form

The contact form for the rebuilt site. The old form had name, email and message; this one asks enough to qualify a lead before the first call.

## Key Details

Fields:

1. Name, organization, email.
2. What are you trying to change? (free text)
3. Kind of work: audit, workshop, product design project, not sure.
4. When would you like to start?
5. Budget range: under $10,000, $10,000 to $30,000, $30,000 to $80,000, over $80,000, not sure yet.
6. How did you hear about me?

Answers land in my inbox and get a row in the [[client-pipeline-tracker]] the same Friday.

## Why It Matters

Question 5 does most of the filtering; question 6 keeps the referral data honest ([[where-2026-clients-came-from]]).
