# Does Sustainable Design Belong In Proposals?

## Question

Should my proposals include a page weight budget or a sustainability line as a standard part of the design work?

## Current Answer

Deferred on 2026-07-10, while reworking the proposal template.

- I can only promise what I control: images, fonts, video and layout. Scripts and hosting belong to the client.
- Without measurement it reads as marketing, which I said I would not do.
- The performance argument (fast on old phones) is easier to sell and already in my accessibility pitch.

## Revisit When

A client asks for it, or I have a second measured case beyond my own portfolio ([[portfolio-page-weight-check]]).
