# Request for proposal

## Summary

A request for proposal (RFP) is a procurement document in which an organization asks potential suppliers for business proposals, usually as part of a structured bidding process. The article describes it as a form of reverse auction and covers typical contents, scoring and the related request for information and request for quotation.

## Why It Matters

Read after the [[velora-pitch]], while deciding whether to answer cold RFPs at all ([[should-i-answer-cold-rfps]]). The useful reminder: an RFP is a buying process designed for comparison, and the buyer's scoring sheet is the real brief. Velora's sheet gave 20% to team and capacity ([[velora-rfp-2026]]), and I treated it as a formality.

## Key Details

- RFPs suit buyers who want comparable bids; they favor suppliers who fit the template.
- Requests for quotation are the simpler cousin, where price dominates. Audit inquiries often behave like those, which matches [[day-rates-suit-small-fixed-work]].

## Caveats

Generic and procurement-focused, with nothing specific to design services.
