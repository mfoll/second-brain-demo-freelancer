# Proposal Template Rework

Rebuilding my proposal template so that the next Velora-sized bid answers all three of Velora's objections before anyone asks: three options with a paid first step, named partners, and research outcomes from past work.

## Outcome

A proposal template with three options, a paid first step, named partners and research outcomes from past work. Reached on 2026-07-17 ([[proposal-template-2026]]).

## Current State

- Completed 2026-07-17. The template is a Figma Slides-style doc exported to PDF; source file in my own Figma drafts, "Proposal template 2026". The 2025 version is archived ([[proposal-template-2025]]).
- Used twice so far: the Tallowmere quote, which took the short day-rate variant ([[tallowmere-audit-quote-2026-09]]), and one proposal that is still open in the [[client-pipeline-tracker]].
- Named partners agreed in writing: [[imani-clarke]] for research and [[bastian-kohl]] for front end ([[partner-availability-agreements-2026]]).

## Scope and Boundaries

The proposal document and the steps for filling it in ([[write-a-three-option-proposal]]). Pricing rules are decided elsewhere ([[pricing-check-in-2026-06-18]]); the template applies them. Contract terms stay in the [[standard-services-agreement]], which the proposal references but does not repeat.

## Working Context

Each Velora reason ([[velora-feedback-call-2026-06-03]]) became one section:

1. **One price, no smaller first step.** The template now always has three options. Option A is a paid first step: a discovery sprint (usually $6,500 to $9,000, two to three weeks) or a day-rate audit. Option B is the recommended scope. Option C adds the extra I would most like to do, such as a second test round. I wrote the options page first and the rest around it ([[three-option-proposals]], [[paid-discovery-phase]]).
2. **Screens, not outcomes.** A "What changed for past clients" page with three numbers I am allowed to use: Ledgerleaf's trial-to-first-invoice rate (31% to 44%), the Thistlewood fix list (and how many items the library shipped), and Copperline's SUS numbers only once Copperline approves ([[will-copperline-approve-a-case-study]]).
3. **Capacity.** A "Who works on this" page naming Imani and Bastian with their roles and day rates, plus my booked weeks for the proposal period from the [[capacity-calendar-2026]].

[[rosalind-ng]] and [[nadia-ferro]] reviewed the draft at the 2026-07-16 circle session. Rosalind cut my "about me" page from two pages to half a page. Nadia asked for the options to show what is excluded, not only what is included; that went in.

Rejected: a proposal web page with live pricing toggles. Nice, but clients forward PDFs to finance and procurement, and Rashid at Velora printed mine.
