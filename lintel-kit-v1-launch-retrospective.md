# Lintel Kit V1 Launch Retrospective

Written on 2025-12-05, three weeks after launch.

## Went well

- The waitlist: 312 addresses from a sign-up on my site and two Design Night mentions produced 41 first-day sales.
- Plain listing copy; nobody asked what was in the box.
- Jae-won's share instead of a fee kept launch costs to the artwork ($900) and my time.

## Went badly

- Late renames in Figma broke an alias in code; [[mirela-dobos]] found it ([[first-token-bug-report-2025-11-26]]).
- Free-plan buyers could not publish the library and wrote in; the listing did not say so.
- Launch week support (six hours) landed on a Copperline week.
- The kit took about 120 hours, spread in gaps, and slid two months.

## Changes

- Freeze names a week before any release; alias check in the build ([[release-a-lintel-kit-update]]).
- Plan requirements on the listing and in getting started.
- Release on a week with no client deadline.
