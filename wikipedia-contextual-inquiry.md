# Contextual inquiry

## Summary

Article on contextual inquiry, a research method from contextual design: a roughly two-hour one-to-one session where the researcher watches someone do their normal work and discusses it with them as it happens.

## Why It Matters

Marrowgate week 3. It is the method I wish I had used at Crumbhollow's counter during pickup hours; see [[service-design]]. For Tallowmere I would like an hour in the box office, if the 8-day audit allows.

## Caveats

Two hours per session is a lot for a small fixed-fee job.
