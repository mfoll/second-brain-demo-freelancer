# Design Night 2025-12-11

My first Design Night at Tidewell, the quarterly evening meetup run by [[felipe-aranda|Felipe Aranda]]. About 45 people.

## What happened

- Two talks: one on design critique in remote teams, one on a public transit app's onboarding. Both in-house designers.
- In the questions I asked about contrast in brand palettes. Felipe found me afterwards: nobody had pitched an accessibility talk and he had a March slot.
- Met two in-house design leads who had looked at Lintel Kit; one later bought it.

## What it led to

Agreed to speak on 2026-03-19. The talk on accessible color tokens became [[design-night-talk-2026]]; the invitation is in [[felipe-speaker-invitation-email]]. The subject was Copperline's palette problem, which went to its decision two months later ([[copperline-accessibility-review-2026-02-11]]); the talk had to use Lintel Kit examples because Copperline material was not approved for publication.
