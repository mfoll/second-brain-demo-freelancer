# Thematic analysis

## Summary

Article on thematic analysis, a common way to identify, analyse and interpret patterns of meaning in qualitative data. It describes it as an umbrella for several approaches, contrasted with fuller methodologies such as grounded theory.

## Why It Matters

Lorna asked us to say which analysis we used. My honest answer for the Lintel diary study: an informal thematic analysis done through [[affinity-mapping]]. Findings in [[lintel-diary-study-findings]].

## Caveats

Academic. Far more rigour than a client project pays for.
