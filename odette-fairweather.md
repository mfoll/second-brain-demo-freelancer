# Odette Fairweather

Digital services librarian at Thistlewood Libraries (nine branches). Commissioned the [[thistlewood-catalog-audit]] and owns the relationship with the catalog vendor.

## How we work together

- She came with a budget, a deadline tied to the library board's July meeting and a clear question: what is wrong and who can fix it. Easiest brief I have had.
- She recruited five patrons who use assistive technology for short sessions and sat in on all of them.
- She uses the fix list as her vendor negotiation document. She asked for it as a spreadsheet, not a PDF, so she could add vendor ticket numbers ([[thistlewood-fix-list]]).

## Current threads

- In March 2026 she reported 19 of 31 high and critical issues fixed. She would like a retest when the vendor upgrade lands, probably in 2027 ([[should-audits-include-a-retest]]).
- She agreed to be a reference for audit work and gave me a two-sentence quote for the portfolio.
