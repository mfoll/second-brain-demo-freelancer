# Ledgerleaf Proposal 2025-12

My three-option proposal for the Ledgerleaf onboarding redesign, sent 2025-11-28 and accepted on 2025-12-08 at the middle option. The first proposal under the value-based rule.

## Key Details

- Framing page: the outcome in Ledgerleaf's own metric. Each point of trial-to-first-invoice rate is worth about 40 paying customers a year at their volumes, a number Wen Zhao gave me.
- Option A, $14,000: heuristic review and redesign of the first three screens. 4 weeks.
- Option B, $24,000: 8 interviews with recent trial users, redesign of the full path to first invoice, support during build. 10 weeks.
- Option C, $36,000: B plus an in-app checklist and a five-email onboarding sequence, and a follow-up review after 90 days.
- Terms: 50% deposit ($12,000 for B), balance on launch, net 15, standard agreement ([[standard-services-agreement]]).
- Assumptions and exclusions listed: no bank feed changes, Ledgerleaf engineers build, one round of copy review by Ifeoma Nwosu.

## Why It Matters

Wen chose B without negotiating and said the C option made B look sensible. Evidence for [[value-based-pricing]], with the caveat that one client is one client.
