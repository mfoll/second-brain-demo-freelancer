# Effective Day Rate

What an engagement actually paid per day worked: fee divided by days really spent, including calls, admin and fixes after handoff. The number that tells me whether a price was right, whatever model I used to set it.

## Formula

Effective day rate = fee received / days logged (a day = 7 billable-equivalent hours; I log in half days).

## Why it matters

The quoted rate hides overruns. Crumbhollow was quoted at $650 a day and paid $11,700 for 27 days: $433 a day ([[crumbhollow-time-log]]). Seeing that number next to the others in the [[effective-day-rate-sheet]] started the [[pricing-model-review]].

It is also the check on value-based prices. If an option would drop below my floor ($650 in 2025, $720 from June 2026, see [[pricing-floor-calculation]]) when it runs 30% over, the option is too cheap or too vague.

## Caveats

- Unbillable time between engagements (proposals, the pipeline, the portfolio) is not in it. My yearly average across all working days is much lower, which is why the floor matters.
- Late payment doesn't change the rate but costs real money; that is a [[run-the-business]] problem ([[crumbhollow-final-payment-2025-07-04]]).
