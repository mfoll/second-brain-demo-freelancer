# Crumbhollow Overrun Retrospective

Written 2025-05-09, after handoff. Why an 18-day estimate became 27 days.

## What happened

- Cake form: "simple, 4 options" became 11 options after a staff meeting. +5 days.
- Pickup slots: each shop's oven capacity needed its own rules. +3 days.
- Handoff: missing error states, a second round of developer questions. +1 day.
- Calendar: three days a week went to Orchard & Vane, so every client question waited ([[orchard-and-vane-overflow-contract-2025]]).

Result: $11,700 for 27 days, about $433 a day against $650 quoted ([[crumbhollow-time-log]]). Then the final invoice was paid 67 days late ([[crumbhollow-final-payment-2025-07-04]]).

## Root causes

1. Scope words without a list. "Simple form" meant nothing.
2. No change clause, and I did not want to seem difficult.
3. Pricing by days made every overrun my loss and gave Josie Kalani no reason to prioritise.

## What changed

- Proposals list assumptions and exclusions ([[crumbhollow-proposal-2025-01]] has none).
- A [[change-order]] for anything over half a day ([[how-do-i-handle-scope-requests-mid-project]]).
- 50% deposit and late fee ([[contract-terms-update]]).
- Day rates questioned, then dropped for most work ([[pricing-model-review]], [[pricing-decision-2025-11-20]]).
- Edge-case checklist at handoff ([[hand-off-designs-to-developers]]).
