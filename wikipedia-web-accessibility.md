# Web accessibility

## Summary

Article on web accessibility: making sure websites have no barriers for people with disabilities. It covers kinds of disability and assistive technology, the curb cut effect (benefits to people with situational limits or slow connections), WCAG, and laws in various countries.

## Why It Matters

My first reading for the [[accessibility-deep-dive-2025|Sablewing course]], the day before module 1. The curb cut framing is how I pitch accessibility to clients who think it is a compliance tax. Companion to [[wikipedia-web-content-accessibility-guidelines]].

## Caveats

The legal sections are not something I quote; I do not give legal advice in audits.
