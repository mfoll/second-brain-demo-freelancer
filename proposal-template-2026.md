# Proposal Template 2026

My current proposal template, finished on 2026-07-17. Two variants: the full three-option proposal for work over 10 days, and a two-page day-rate quote for audits, workshops and short work.

## Key Details

Full variant, in order:

1. The problem, in the client's words (half a page).
2. What changed for past clients: three approved numbers ([[testimonial-quotes-collection]]).
3. Options A, B and C, each with scope, what it excludes, timeline and price. B marked as recommended.
4. Approach and research plan.
5. Who works on this: me, [[imani-clarke]] for research and [[bastian-kohl]] for front end, with roles and availability ([[partner-availability-agreements-2026]]), plus my booked weeks.
6. About me (half a page).
7. Terms: points to the [[standard-services-agreement]] (50% deposit, net 15).

Short variant: scope, days, $720 day rate, deliverable, dates, terms.

## Why It Matters

Each of Velora's three reasons maps to a section: options (3), outcomes (2), capacity (5). See [[velora-lessons]].

## Caveats

The outcomes page only has two usable numbers until Copperline approves a case study ([[will-copperline-approve-a-case-study]]).
