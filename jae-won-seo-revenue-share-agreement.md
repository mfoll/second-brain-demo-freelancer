# Jae-won Seo Revenue Share Agreement

The two-page agreement with [[jae-won-seo]] for Lintel Kit's coded tokens, signed on 2025-07-31.

## Key Details

- Jae-won builds and maintains the coded tokens (JSON and CSS) for every 1.x and 2.x release.
- He receives 20% of net sales per seat: Gumroad sale price minus Gumroad's fees and refunds. Paid quarterly within 15 days of quarter end.
- He assigns me the rights to sell his code under the kit's license; he may show it in his own portfolio.
- Price changes: 30 days' notice to him. New license types (such as a team license) need a written addendum.
- Either side may end it with 60 days' notice; he is paid for sales up to the end date.
- Paid to 2026-09-30: about $2,700.

## Why It Matters

The "per seat" wording and the addendum clause are why the team license is not just a Gumroad setting ([[should-lintel-kit-get-a-team-license]]).
