# Revenue sharing

## Summary

Defines revenue sharing: dividing the income from sales among the people who contributed to them. It differs from profit sharing, which divides what is left after costs, and from holding shares in a company.

## Why It Matters

Read before agreeing terms with [[jae-won-seo]] in July 2025. The distinction from profit sharing settled one question quickly: his 20% is on net sales after Gumroad's fees and refunds, not on what is left after my own time and costs, which he could not check ([[jae-won-seo-revenue-share-agreement]]).

## Key Details

- Revenue sharing distributes revenue, before costs, among contributors or stakeholders.
- Profit sharing distributes only what remains after costs.
- The article covers sports leagues sharing ticket and broadcast income, affiliate marketing and platforms that share advertising revenue with creators; only the core definition applied here.
- A revenue share puts the contributor's income at the mercy of sales, which is why Jae-won also wanted a minimum notice period before any price change.
