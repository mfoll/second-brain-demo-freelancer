# Orchard & Vane Overflow Call 2025-01-08

[[graham-whitlock]] called to ask whether I could take overflow work on a retail client's checkout redesign: three days a week from 2025-01-13 to late April, for a flat fee.

- Agreed a flat $38,000 for the period at the agency's contractor rate. It came to 52 days, about $730 a day, more than my own $650 rate, paid monthly by an agency that pays on time ([[orchard-and-vane-overflow-contract-2025]]).
- Graham's condition: no contact with that client outside the agency for 12 months. Fine.
- My condition: two days a week free for my own clients. That is how Crumbhollow fitted in from February ([[crumbhollow-preorder-flow]]), and also part of why Crumbhollow overran.

This was the biggest single source of 2025 revenue. I logged it as the first won row in the [[client-pipeline-tracker]].
