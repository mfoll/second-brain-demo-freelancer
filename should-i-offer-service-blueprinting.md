# Should I Offer Service Blueprinting?

## Question

Should a half-day service blueprint workshop be a paid offer, sold at the $720 day rate as a first step before screen work?

## What I Know

- Crumbhollow's pickup problems were backstage ([[crumbhollow-pickup-blueprint-sketch]]). A blueprint in week one would have caught them.
- A paid first step is one of the Velora lessons ([[proposal-template-rework]]); a blueprint workshop could be that step for service-heavy clients.
- I have drawn one blueprint, after the fact. I have never facilitated one with staff.
- No client has asked for it.

## Leaning

Try it unpaid once inside an existing engagement (Tallowmere's box office would fit) and decide after. Revisit in January 2027 with the cluster review in [[exploratory-reading]].
