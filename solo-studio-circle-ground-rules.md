# Solo Studio Circle Ground Rules

The ground rules of the Solo Studio Circle, agreed on 2025-04-17 and amended on 2025-09-18 and 2026-04-16.

> [!note]
> Client names can be said in the room and are never repeated outside it, written in shared notes only as initials, and never used to approach that client.

1. Meet the third Thursday of each month, 12:00 to 13:30, Tidewell small room. In person; video only if someone is ill.
2. Money is a normal topic. Rates, fees and invoices are shared when useful, in the room only.
3. One host per session brings one question and one piece of work ([[run-a-circle-session]]).
4. Disagree in the room, then let it go.
5. No referral fees. Send work back when you can. (Added 2025-09-18, after Tomás argued for 10%.)
6. Members who overlap tell each other when they bid for the same client (added 2025-09-18).
7. Subcontracting between members follows [[subcontracting-with-peers]].
8. Seven members at most for now (added 2026-04-16). New members need everyone's yes; see [[should-the-circle-take-new-members]].

Kept short on purpose. When a rule needs explaining, we talk about it rather than write more.
