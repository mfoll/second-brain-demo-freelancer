# Solo Weekly Rhythm

How my week is laid out since moving to Tidewell Commons in September 2025. Adjusted when a client needs a specific day, but this is the default.

- **Monday to Thursday**: client work, up to 4 days. Mornings for design, afternoons for calls and reviews.
- **Friday morning**: admin. Invoices (Paloma handles receipts), the tax set-aside ([[set-aside-taxes-from-each-payment]]), email.
- **Friday, one hour**: pipeline. Update the [[client-pipeline-tracker]], check the [[capacity-calendar-2026]] three months ahead, follow up on open proposals.
- **Friday afternoon**: Lintel Kit support and portfolio work.
- **Third Thursday evening**: Solo Studio Circle.

The Friday hour on the pipeline is the one rule I don't break, even when busy ([[feast-or-famine-cycle]]).
