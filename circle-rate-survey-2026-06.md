# Circle Rate Survey 2026-06

Anonymous survey I ran in the Solo Studio Circle the week before the [[pricing-check-in-2026-06-18]]. Five of the other six members answered.

## Key Details

- Four of five quote a day rate for short work; one prices everything by project.
- Day rates for short work: $640 to $800; median $700.
- Three of five use options in proposals for longer work.
- Two of five had lost a bid in the past year to an agency offering a smaller first phase.

## Why It Matters

Peer benchmark behind the $720 rate ([[pricing-rules-2026]]). Small and local, but it's the market my clients compare against.

## Caveats

Five people, self-reported, different specialities (content, research, brand, front end, product).
