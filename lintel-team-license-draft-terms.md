# Lintel Team License Draft Terms

My draft of team terms, written in the working session of 2026-09-21. Not published.

## Key Details

- Price: $249 for up to 10 seats. Above 10, buy a second team license or add seats at $79.
- One named admin receives updates and is the only support contact.
- Seats may be reassigned when people leave the team, by the admin.
- Existing seats held by the team count toward a team purchase: a team with five seats pays $249 minus the seats' price, never less than zero.
- Invoices: Gumroad receipt only. No purchase-order invoices, no supplier forms.
- Revenue share for [[jae-won-seo]]: blank. Needs an addendum ([[jae-won-seo-revenue-share-agreement]]).

## Why It Matters

The concrete version of option 1 in [[should-lintel-kit-get-a-team-license]]. The "Gumroad receipt only" line is the support-load guardrail and the one most likely to annoy [[calvin-moyo]].
