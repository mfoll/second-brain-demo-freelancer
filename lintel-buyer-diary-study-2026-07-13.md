# Lintel Buyer Diary Study 2026-07-13

Start of my first diary study, the main assignment of the Marrowgate course: six Lintel Kit buyers recording how they used the kit for ten working days, 2026-07-13 to 2026-07-24.

## Setup

- Plan: [[lintel-diary-study-plan]], reviewed three times by [[lorna-beckett|Lorna Beckett]].
- Participants P1 to P6, chosen from 31 buyers who answered an email; mix of solo designers (3), in-house designers on small teams (2) and one developer. Each received a free V2 seat.
- Two prompts a day by form, plus an optional screenshot: what did you try to do with the kit today, and where did you get stuck or work around it.
- Kick-off call of 15 minutes with each participant on 2026-07-13 and an exit interview of 30 minutes after day 10.

## How it went

- 41 entries out of a possible 60. Completion fell from 6 on day 1 to 3 on day 8, then rose for the last two days after a reminder.
- One participant dropped out on day 4 for a deadline; kept the entries.
- Synthesis by [[affinity-mapping]] on 2026-07-28, later used as the test case for the AI comparison ([[ai-synthesis-trial-notes]]).

Results are in [[lintel-diary-study-findings]].
