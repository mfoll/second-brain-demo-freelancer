# Should V2 Be Free For Existing Buyers?

## Current Answer

Yes. Decided on 2026-08-10.

## Why

- The license promises all 1.x and 2.x updates for a seat ([[lintel-kit-license-terms]]). Charging would break my own terms.
- Even without that clause, the contrast fix is partly a fix: V1's single `color.primary` led three buyers to ship unreadable button labels ([[lintel-kit-v2]]).
- Goodwill: existing buyers are the ones who recommend the kit.

## Rejected

A paid upgrade at $29, which would have brought maybe $2,000 and a month of license emails. A paid 3.0 later remains possible; the license only covers 2.x.
