# Lintel Kit License Terms

The buyer license for Lintel Kit, written in October 2025 and checked by [[nathaniel-brook]] in a 20-minute call on 2025-10-28, billed at $150. Summarised.

## Key Details

- One license per seat: one named person may use the kit, in any number of projects, commercial or not.
- Allowed: use in client work and in shipped products; modify freely.
- Not allowed: resell or redistribute the kit, the Figma file or the coded tokens as a kit or template; share a seat between people.
- Updates: all 1.x and 2.x updates included for the seat.
- Support: by email, best effort, no response time promised.
- Coded tokens: same terms; [[jae-won-seo]] assigned me the rights to sell them under this license.

## Why It Matters

"One named person" is the clause teams break when they share a seat, and the reason a team license keeps coming up ([[lintel-team-license-requests]]).
