# How Many Participants Is Enough For A Usability Test?

## Question

How many participants should I plan and budget for in a usability test, for clients who want both problems found and a number to compare?

## Current Answer

Working policy since the Marrowgate course:

- Finding problems in one round: 5 to 6 per distinct user group. Most serious problems show up by the fifth session.
- Comparing rounds with a number like [[system-usability-scale|SUS]] or completion rate: 10 to 12 per round, same tasks, and report the range.
- Distinct groups (Copperline's older members, small-business members, screen reader users) each need some representation; one screen reader participant is a minimum, not a sample.

Copperline used 12 at baseline and 10 on the prototype; Lintel's unmoderated test used 8.

## Caveats

The "five users" rule is about finding problems, not measuring. Clients mix the two up, and so did I at the agency. See [[wikipedia-usability-testing]] and [[think-aloud-testing]].
