# Contract Review With Nathaniel Brook 2025-06-20

A 60-minute video call with [[nathaniel-brook]] to go through his redlines on my new services agreement.

## What we decided

- Keep the 50% deposit but call it a deposit credited against the fee, not non-refundable.
- Late fee as 1.5% a month from the due date, not a flat amount.
- IP transfers on final payment; drafts licensed during the project. He called this "the clause that does the collecting for you".
- Drop the kill fee; termination for work done is enough.
- Liability capped at fees paid; insurance stated as an obligation.

## What I pushed back on

He suggested net 10. I kept net 15: two of my likely clients run fortnightly payment cycles, and a term they cannot meet gets ignored.

## After

Final text on 2025-06-27 ([[standard-services-agreement]]). Fee $1,200 flat. Details per clause in [[contract-review-notes-nathaniel-brook-2025-06]].
