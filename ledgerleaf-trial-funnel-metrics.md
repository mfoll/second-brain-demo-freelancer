# Ledgerleaf Trial Funnel Metrics

Monthly trial funnel figures Wen Zhao shares with me, from Ledgerleaf's product analytics. Aggregates only.

## Key Details

| Period | Trials started | Sent first invoice | Rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sep to Nov 2025 (baseline) | about 2,100 | about 650 | 31% |
| Split test, 2026-03-12 to 03-26, new flow | 380 | 156 | 41% |
| Split test, old flow | 372 | 112 | 30% |
| April 2026 | 820 | 336 | 41% |
| June 2026 | 760 | 334 | 44% |

- "Sent first invoice" means within 14 days of signup.
- Biggest single step change: drop-off at business details fell from 38% to 9% once the tax ID moved to the send step.

## Why It Matters

My cleanest outcome evidence, and the only one a client has approved for a case study ([[ledgerleaf-case-study-approval-email]]).

## Caveats

April to June is the busy season for sole trader signups; Wen thinks seasonality accounts for a point or two. The split test is the stronger evidence.
