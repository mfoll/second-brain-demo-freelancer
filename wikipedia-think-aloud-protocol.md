# Think aloud protocol

## Summary

Article on the think-aloud protocol as a data-gathering method in usability testing, psychology and the social sciences. It distinguishes concurrent thinking aloud (during the task) from retrospective (after it) and notes that verbalising can change how people do the task.

## Why It Matters

I looked it up while writing the moderator guide for the Copperline discovery baseline test in October 2025, because I wanted a name for what I did with the screen reader participant: pause and explain rather than talk over the speech output. That is the retrospective variant. My practice note is [[think-aloud-testing]].

## Caveats

Short on practical moderation advice. Says nothing about combining it with screen readers.
