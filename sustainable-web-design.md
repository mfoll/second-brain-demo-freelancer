# Sustainable Web Design

Exploration cluster on the energy and data cost of what I design: page weight, images, fonts, video. Nobody has asked for it. I started reading because the old portfolio home page weighed 6.8 MB.

## Where I am

I measured my own portfolio in May 2026 ([[portfolio-page-weight-check]]): the home page dropped from 6.8 MB to 1.9 MB by replacing two autoplay videos with posters and subsetting one font. That was also faster on phones, which is the argument clients actually hear. A [[page-weight-budget]] is the one practice I would carry into client work.

Whether to put it in proposals is parked: [[does-sustainable-design-belong-in-proposals]]. It sounds like marketing unless I can measure it, and I can only measure what I build or what the client's developers let me test.
