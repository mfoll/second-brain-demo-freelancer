# Orchard And Vane Overflow Contract 2025

Summary of my contractor agreement with Orchard & Vane, my former agency, for overflow work on a retail client's checkout redesign from 2025-01-13 to 2025-04-30.

## Key Details

- Flat fee $38,000 for the period, invoiced in four monthly parts of $9,500. Paid within 14 days every time.
- Three days a week, Monday to Wednesday, about 52 days in the end (roughly $730 a day).
- I worked inside the agency's tools and under Graham Whitlock's creative direction; the agency owns all work.
- Non-solicitation: no direct work for that retail client for 12 months after the end date (expired 2026-04-30).
- No exclusivity. Thursdays and Fridays were mine, which is where [[crumbhollow-preorder-flow]] fitted.

## Why It Matters

The largest single item in 2025 revenue and the reason the late Crumbhollow payment did not hurt more. Also the first sign of a pattern I still manage: one anchor client crowds out the rest ([[how-many-engagements-can-i-run-at-once]]).

## Caveats

I describe the agency's client only as "a retail client"; the confidentiality clause still applies.
