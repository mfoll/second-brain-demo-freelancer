# Qualify A New Lead

What I do between a first message and deciding whether to write a proposal. Takes one short call and about an hour.

## Steps

1. Log the lead in the [[client-pipeline-tracker]] the same day: source, referrer, rough size.
2. Read the inquiry against [[lead-red-flags]]. Reply within one working day either way.
3. Book a free 30-minute call. It is a sales call, not discovery; no design advice beyond general pointers.
4. On the call, ask:
   - What are you trying to change, and what does it cost you now?
   - What would a good result be worth?
   - Who decides, and how do you compare suppliers? (Day rates or proposals.)
   - When do you need to start and finish?
   - What budget range have you set aside?
5. Check the [[capacity-calendar-2026]] before promising dates.
6. Decide: proposal ([[write-a-three-option-proposal]]), day-rate quote ([[pricing-rules-2026]]), referral to a peer, or polite no.
7. For an RFP, use the same steps plus the filter in [[rfp-watchlist]] and a go/no-go note like [[velora-go-no-go-2026-04-22]].

## Output

A row updated in the tracker with the decision and date; a proposal or quote within 5 working days if going ahead.
