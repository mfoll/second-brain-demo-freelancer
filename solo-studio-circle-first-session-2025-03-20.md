# Solo Studio Circle First Session 2025-03-20

First meeting of the Solo Studio Circle, 12:00 to 13:40 in the Tidewell small room, booked through [[rosalind-ng|Rosalind]]'s membership. All six founding members came.

## What happened

- Rosalind opened with the one rule we had agreed in advance: what is said about clients stays in the room.
- Rates round. Everyone wrote their current day rate on a card, unsigned, and we read them out: $520, $650 (mine), $700, $750, $800, $950. The spread surprised everyone. The $520 was an illustrator's day for commissioned work and not comparable; the rest were product, research, front end and content.
- Discussion: the two highest rates belonged to the people who quote fixed fees and translate to a day rate only when asked. [[bastian-kohl|Bastian]] said his day rate is a floor, not a price.
- I talked about Crumbhollow, then six weeks in and already over plan. Nobody said "raise your rate"; [[imani-clarke|Imani]] asked how I had estimated the days. Honest answer: from the agency's old estimates, which assumed a team.

## Decisions

- Meet monthly, third Thursday, lunchtime, in person.
- Next session: ground rules and hosting ([[solo-studio-circle-ground-rules]]).

## What it led to

The $650 card stayed in my head until the [[pricing-model-review]] in October. The Crumbhollow outcome (27 days on an 18-day quote) is in [[crumbhollow-preorder-flow]].
