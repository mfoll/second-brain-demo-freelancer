# Velora Clarification Log

My notes from the shared clarification document Velora ran during the RFP, visible to all bidders, closed on 2026-05-07.

## Key Details

- 23 questions from bidders in total; I asked 3.
- My questions: whether rider research could include street intercepts at docking stations (yes, with their operations team present); whether the pass purchase flow was in scope for the web as well (no, app only); whether a phased price was acceptable (answer: "bidders may structure pricing as they see fit").
- That third answer is the one that hurts in hindsight. I asked about phasing, got a yes, and still sent one price ([[velora-proposal-2026-05-14]]).
- Another bidder asked whether subcontractors had to be named. Answer: "preferred". I missed the importance of that.

Evidence that both lessons in [[velora-lessons]] were visible before submission, not only in the feedback.
