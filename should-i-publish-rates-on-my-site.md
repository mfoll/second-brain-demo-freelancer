# Should I Publish Rates On My Site?

## Question

Should the rebuilt portfolio show my day rate or project price ranges, so leads can filter themselves?

## Current Answer

> [!info]
> No rates on the site. The services page says audits and workshops are quoted at a day rate on request and projects get a proposal with three options. The inquiry form asks for a budget range instead.

## Why

- Published rates go stale: the old site still shows $600 from 2024 ([[old-portfolio-site-2024]]).
- A day rate on the site anchors project buyers on days, which undoes [[value-based-pricing]] for larger work.
- The budget question on the [[website-inquiry-form]] does the filtering.

## What would reopen it

Many inquiries with budgets far below any of my options after the relaunch. Answered on 2026-06-22 with [[pricing-rules-2026]].
