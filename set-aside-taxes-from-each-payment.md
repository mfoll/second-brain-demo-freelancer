# Set Aside Taxes From Each Payment

What I do with every payment that reaches the business account, so that the tax account always holds what the Revenue Office will ask for.

## Rule

> [!info]
> Since 2026-04-13, 32% of every payment goes to the tax account on the day it arrives, client payments and Gumroad payouts alike. From January 2025 to 2026-04-12 it was 30% of client payments only.

Why 32%: the 2025 return needed about 29.3% of what I received, which left only $720 of slack, and kit income was outside the rule ([[set-aside-rate-raised-2026-04-13]], [[tax-year-2025-filing]]).

## Steps

1. When Paperbird marks an invoice paid, or a Gumroad payout lands, open the business account.
2. Transfer 32% of the gross amount to the tax account. Round up to the next $10.
3. For Gumroad payouts: take 32% of the payout itself, after Gumroad's fees, then pay [[jae-won-seo]]'s quarterly share from what remains, not from the tax money.
4. Add a row to the [[tax-account-ledger]]: date, payer, amount received, amount transferred, running balance.
5. If the payment is a deposit, set aside on the deposit too. Deposits are income when received.

Takes two minutes. Do it the same day; a week's delay is how the money gets spent.

## Quarterly estimates

The fictional Revenue Office takes estimated payments four times a year. My dates: 15 April, 15 June, 15 September and 15 January (for the previous year's last quarter).

- 2025: $7,000 a quarter, set in February 2025 on a $90,000 projection.
- 2026: $8,500 a quarter, set with [[delphine-arceneaux]] on 2026-04-08 on a $125,000 projection.
- Pay each estimate from the tax account, and log it in the ledger as an outflow.
- Every September: compare year-to-date income with the projection. If it is more than 10% off, ask Delphine whether to change the remaining estimates ([[quarterly-estimate-worksheet-2026]]).

## Checks

- At each estimate date, the tax account balance after paying should still be at least 25% of what came in since the previous estimate. If not, something was missed.
- At year end, the ledger total transferred should match 32% of Paperbird's paid total plus Gumroad payouts, within $50.

## What it is not

Not tax advice and not a statement of any country's rules: it is my cash rule, set with my accountant for my income. The rate is reviewed after each annual return. See [[estimated-tax-payments]] for the general idea and [[three-account-money-setup]] for where the accounts sit.
