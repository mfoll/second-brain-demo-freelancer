# Crumbhollow Pickup Blueprint Sketch

## Summary

A service blueprint of the Crumbhollow pre-order pickup that I drew in October 2025, six months after the project ended, from my notes and the complaints [[josie-kalani|Josie]] forwarded in May.

## Key Details

- Lanes: customer, counter staff, kitchen and back office, systems (order platform, label printer).
- Three of the first five complaints sat below the line of visibility: order lists printed at 6:00 missed later orders, no shelf labels by slot, and slot changes not shown to counter staff.
- Two were front stage: confirmation email unclear on which shop, and no "ready" message.

## Why It Matters

Started the [[service-design]] cluster and the open [[should-i-offer-service-blueprinting]]. The project itself is [[crumbhollow-preorder-flow]].

## Caveats

Drawn from memory and forwarded complaints, not with staff. I did not share it with Crumbhollow.
