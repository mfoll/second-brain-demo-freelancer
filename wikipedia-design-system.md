# Design system

## Summary

Describes a design system: a framework of standards, reusable components and documentation that keeps an organisation's digital products consistent and acts as a single source of truth for designers and developers. Parts can include component libraries, style guides, design languages, coded components and documentation.

## Why It Matters

Read on the first day of the Lintel Kit project, to check my scope against the usual definition. Lintel Kit is a starter for a design system, not a design system: it gives tokens, components and a getting-started page, and leaves the organisation-specific standards to the buyer. That framing went into the listing copy ([[lintel-kit-gumroad-listing]]).

## Key Details

- A design system combines standards, reusable components and documentation.
- Typical parts: pattern and component libraries, style guides (type, color, spacing), coded components, documentation.
- Its value is consistency and efficiency across products and teams.
- Caveat: the article is short and general; tokens get no detailed treatment, see [[design-tokens]].
