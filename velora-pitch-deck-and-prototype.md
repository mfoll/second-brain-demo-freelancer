# Velora Pitch Deck And Prototype

Pointer to the 14-slide presentation and the clickable unlock-flow concept I built for the Velora presentation.

## Key Details

- Slides: problem, three rider insights from public app reviews (I had no Velora research), approach, timeline, the concept, price, me.
- Prototype: 11 frames covering find a bike, scan, unlocking, unlock failed, ride in progress, end ride. Built in about two and a half days, entirely on my own time.
- Played in present mode with the UI hidden ([[figma-play-prototypes-docs]]).

## Why It Matters

The best-received part of the pitch ([[velora-feedback-call-2026-06-03]]). Also a lesson in cost: two and a half unbillable days on a concept for a bid I lost. Next time a prototype goes into a bid only if the RFP scores it.
