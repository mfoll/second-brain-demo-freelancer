A short list of public procurement feeds and nonprofit job boards I skim once a fortnight for design and accessibility RFPs, plus the filter I apply.

## Key Details

- Sources: two public sector procurement portals, a nonprofit technology board and a library consortium mailing list.
- Filter: accessibility or UX scope; budget stated; timeline I can meet; capacity scoring I can answer with named partners.
- Since Velora: 9 RFPs seen, 2 passed the filter, 0 answered. Both clashed with Copperline's handoff.
