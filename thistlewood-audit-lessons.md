# Thistlewood Audit Lessons

Notes after my first independent accessibility audit, July 2025.

- **Tag who can fix it.** Samir Haddadin's first reaction to the draft was defensive. Adding an owner tag (configuration, templates, vendor) changed the conversation ([[audit-severity-levels]]).
- **Board summary first.** Odette Fairweather needed one page for the board. Now standard.
- **Spreadsheet, not PDF.** The fix list became a working document only because it was editable ([[thistlewood-fix-list]]).
- **Screen reader time.** I estimated 2 days and needed 3, partly because I was still learning NVDA ([[thistlewood-screen-reader-test-log]]).
- **Patron sessions are worth the day.** Five short sessions produced the two clips that moved the board.
- **Have a retest price ready** ([[should-audits-include-a-retest]]).
- 12.5 days actual against 13 estimated, on a $9,200 fixed fee: about $736 a day. A clear scope with exclusions protected me in a way Crumbhollow's never did.
