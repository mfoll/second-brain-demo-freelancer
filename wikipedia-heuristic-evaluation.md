# Heuristic evaluation

## Summary

Article on heuristic evaluation, a usability inspection method in which evaluators judge an interface against recognised usability principles. It describes common heuristic sets and the method's appeal when budgets do not allow user testing.

## Why It Matters

Read the week before the Thistlewood kickoff to decide how to combine a WCAG audit with a usability review in one report. I kept them separate: WCAG findings cite success criteria ([[wcag-audit-checklist]]), usability findings cite a heuristic, so the client can tell compliance from preference.

## Caveats

Says little about how severe findings are or how to rank them; my ranking came from the Sablewing course.
