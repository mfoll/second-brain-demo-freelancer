# Public speaking

## Summary

Public speaking is delivering a speech to a live audience to inform, teach or persuade. The article covers its history from classical rhetoric, the main purposes of a speech, training and practice, the use of visual aids such as slides, and fear of public speaking.

## Why It Matters

Opened while choosing a 2027 talk from the [[talk-ideas-backlog]]. The section on visual aids matches what went wrong at Design Night: the live contrast-checker demo competed with me for attention and the back rows couldn't read it ([[design-night-talk-2026-03-19]]).

## Key Details

- Purpose first: inform, persuade or teach. My Design Night talk tried to do all three; the next one picks one.
- Rehearsal reduces anxiety and length. Two dry runs cut my talk from 34 to 25 minutes.

## Caveats

General overview, not a guide to conference talks.
