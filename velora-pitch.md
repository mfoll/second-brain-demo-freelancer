# Velora Pitch

My bid for the Velora Mobility app redesign. The RFP came in cold on 2026-04-20, I submitted and presented on 2026-05-14 with one fee of $74,000, and on 2026-05-29 Velora chose Orchard & Vane, my former agency. The project closed after the feedback call on 2026-06-03.

## Outcome

Pitch submitted on 2026-05-14 and lost on 2026-05-29; lessons recorded after the feedback call. Reached. The lessons are the useful part and now live in [[velora-lessons]], [[proposal-template-rework]] and [[portfolio-site-rebuild]].

## Current State

- Closed. Lost to Orchard & Vane, who offered a $38,000 discovery phase first instead of the whole redesign ([[velora-decision-email-2026-05-29]]).
- Annika Brandvold gave three reasons on 2026-06-03: one price with no smaller first step, case studies that show screens rather than research outcomes, and doubt that one person had the capacity ([[velora-feedback-call-2026-06-03]]).
- All three reasons have an owner: the [[proposal-template-rework]] (completed 2026-07-17), the [[portfolio-site-rebuild]] (active) and the [[research-methods-course-2026]] (completed).
- Velora is marked "lost, keep warm" in the [[client-pipeline-tracker]]. Annika said she would be open to hearing from me for a later phase.

## Scope and Boundaries

The pitch only: reading the RFP, the proposal, the clickable concept, the presentation, the decision and the debrief. The lessons as general practice belong to [[win-new-work]] and its notes. I do not keep Velora's internal documents beyond the RFP summary; their app analytics excerpt went back with the NDA closure on 2026-06-10.

## Working Context

Velora is an e-bike sharing operator. The RFP ([[velora-rfp-2026]]) asked for a redesign of the rider app: finding a bike, unlocking, ending a ride and the pass purchase flow. Procurement was run by [[rashid-kamau]]; [[annika-brandvold]], head of product, chaired the panel. Five firms submitted and three were invited to present.

It was the biggest thing I had ever bid on alone. I priced it as one fixed fee of $74,000 for 14 weeks covering research, design and handoff ([[velora-proposal-2026-05-14]]). That contradicted my own November 2025 rule of three options ([[pricing-decision-2025-11-20]]): I told myself an RFP with a fixed scope did not need options. In hindsight that was the mistake that mattered most. Orchard & Vane gave Velora a cheap way to say yes; I gave them one large yes-or-no.

The presentation on 2026-05-14 ([[velora-pitch-presentation-2026-05-14]]) went well on the design side. The clickable concept of the unlock flow ([[velora-pitch-deck-and-prototype]], run with [[figma-play-prototypes-docs|Figma's present mode]]) got the most questions. The capacity question came from Rashid in the Q&A and I answered it badly: "I'll bring in help if needed" sounded like improvisation.

What I did not know then and found out on the feedback call: they had read my case studies and found only screens, no numbers. I had the Copperline baseline (SUS 58) and could not show it because Copperline has not approved a case study ([[will-copperline-approve-a-case-study]]).
