# The Kit Must Not Eat Client Time

## Stance

Lintel Kit is a side product. Any kit decision that regularly costs more than four hours a week, or takes a client deadline week, is a bad decision even if it makes money.

## Scope

Support, releases and licensing for Lintel Kit. Not the one-off building of a new version, which I schedule like a small project.

## Why I think this

- Client work pays about $720 a day at my audit rate; the kit earns about $1,050 a month. A week of kit support costs more than a month of kit income.
- Launch week took six hours of support and pushed Copperline work into the weekend ([[lintel-kit-v1-launch-day-2025-11-12]]).
- My reputation comes from client work; a late client deliverable costs more than a slow support reply.

## What Would Change My Mind

The kit reliably earning more than $3,000 a month, at which point it deserves real time, or help: [[jae-won-seo]] taking more, or [[paloma-ruiz]] triaging support.

## Implications

Say no to custom license terms, purchase-order invoices and Penpot for now ([[should-lintel-kit-get-a-team-license]], [[should-lintel-kit-support-penpot]]).
