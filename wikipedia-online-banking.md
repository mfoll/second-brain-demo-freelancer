# Online banking

## Summary

Encyclopedia overview of online banking (also called internet, digital or web banking): customers of a bank or other financial institution view account information and carry out transactions through the institution's website or app. The article notes it has been the most common way customers reach their accounts since the early 2010s.

## Why It Matters

Background reading before the Copperline kickoff, to get the vocabulary straight before interviewing members about what they actually use ([[copperline-interview-guide]]). Nothing in it is about Copperline.

## Caveats

General and not design-focused. Says nothing about usability or accessibility of online banking.
