## Why It Matters

This is the evidence for every kit decision: whether the team license would add or cannibalise revenue ([[should-lintel-kit-get-a-team-license]]), whether V2 can carry a higher price ([[should-the-kit-price-rise-with-v2]]), and whether the kit is worth the hours I give it ([[the-kit-must-not-eat-client-time]]). The numbers also feed the tax set-aside, since kit payouts count from 2026-04-13 ([[set-aside-taxes-from-each-payment]]).

## Caveats

- Gross is before Gumroad's fees and refunds; "about" because the export rounds currency conversion for a few non-dollar buyers.
- Seats, not people: one buyer with five seats is five sales.
- Referrer data is missing for about a seventh of sales.
- No buyer names in the sheet except customer IDs; named buyers in the vault are only Person notes ([[calvin-moyo]], [[mirela-dobos]]).
